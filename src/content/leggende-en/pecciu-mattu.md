This is one of those stories grandmothers used to tell their grandchildren by the fireplace: *"Oggi vi racconterò la storia di Pecciu Mattu"*, "today I'll tell you the story of Pecciu Mattu".

Many years ago there lived a mother who had a rather simple son called Pecciu. Since he didn't have all his marbles, everyone in town called him **Pecciu Mattu**, "Crazy Pecciu". To help him earn a little money, his mother would send him to the market to sell honey, *o mèle* in the local dialect.

## The honey sold to the statue

One morning Pecciu arrives at the market and starts shouting: *"Chi vone o mèle, mmè?"*, "Who wants honey?". Women answer from every side, *"Eo! Eo! Eo!"*, "Me! Me! Me!", and he, irritated by all that shouting, snaps: *"Lo dongo a chi fa minu parole!"*, "I'll give it to whoever talks the least!".

And nobody in the square talks less than the statue. So Pecciu walks up to it and hangs the honey on it: *"Ecco, lo dongo a ti!"*, "Here, I'm giving it to you!".

## "And the money?"

Back home, his mother asks: *"L'hai vennutu tuttu o mèle?"*, "Did you sell all the honey?". "Yes, Ma!". "And the money?". "She didn't give me any money!".

At that, his mother starts screaming like a woman possessed, and the blows rain down. "Enough, Maaaa! Stop, I'll go and get it!", Pecciu promises. But she throws him out: *"Nn'areveni senza sordi!"*, "Don't come back without the money!".

So Pecciu goes back to the statue to settle the account: "You took the honey, now give me the money, or my mother won't let me back in the house! Do you understand? I want the money! So, are you giving me the money?". The statue, of course, says nothing.

## The hidden treasure

Exasperated, Pecciu picks up a rock and hurls it at the statue: it breaks, and out of it tumble sacks full of coins. *"Se rompe a statua e comenzeno a cascà nzacchi e sordi!"*.

Delighted, Pecciu gathers up the sacks and happily brings them to his mother, who, in disbelief, *"pe la prima vota in vita sea, aremase senza parole"*: for the first time in her life, was left speechless.
