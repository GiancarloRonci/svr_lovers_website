This is an example legend (the events described are invented, not a real legend
handed down in San Vito Romano) showing how an entry in this section looks.

The story goes that, many years ago, an old well stood in an alley of the old
town whose waters, according to the town's elders, could grant a wish to anyone
who looked into them on the night of St. John's Eve. No one remembers for certain
where that well stood any more, but the story has been told from generation to
generation among the houses of the village.

To add a real legend, just create a new file in `src/content/leggende-en/` (and
the matching one in `src/content/leggende/` for the Italian version) with:

- `title`: the legend's title
- `description`: a short description, shown in the list
- `immagine` (optional): an illustration or photo of the place tied to the story
- the story's text in the file body, in Markdown

The best sources for these stories are the accounts of people who heard them told
within their own families: it's worth collecting them before they're lost.
