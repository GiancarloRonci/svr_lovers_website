Once upon a time there was a village woman called **Ciunna**, who lived at the top of the Borgo, the old town. She wore corn leaves in her hair and her *parnanza*, her apron, tied with *vinci*, thin, flexible willow twigs.

Poor thing, she had a glass eye. The villagers said she wore a pair of little boots that clattered on the cobblestones of the Borgo like roof tiles. And she had a ruined hand, covered in scars, because she had been bitten by the pig. So much for her husband, who was a shoemaker by trade.

Ciunna dressed in shabby clothes, yet when she walked along the Borgo she looked like a fairy. Sparrows flew around her and landed on the tuft of leaves in her hair, where they always found a grain or two; then they hopped down onto her chest and rummaged about, finding more grains.

And Ciunna was happy, and forgot all about the shoemaker.
