22 November is Saint Cecilia's day, the patron saint of musicians, and you can tell: this weekend bands, choirs and orchestras are playing all around, from Cave to Genazzano. In Colonna the first Christmas cellars open, and in Palestrina the week leading up to 25 November, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, fills up with talks and events. On Saturday, though, the first appointment is right here at home, with Sanvitese.

A practical note: on Friday 20 there is a 24-hour general strike, with Cotral buses at risk outside the guaranteed time bands, and from Friday evening to Saturday evening trains are on strike too. If you don't have a car, check timetables before going out. Keep an eye on the weather too: the yellow alert for strong winds issued on Thursday for the whole of Lazio should ease off on Friday, and forecasts point to Saturday as the best day, with a cloudier, cooler Sunday.

## Friday: two talks in Palestrina and ceramics at Campaegli

In Palestrina, at 4:30 pm, UNITRE presents ["Cartoline d'amore"](/en/eventi/cartoline-d-amore-unitre-palestrina-2026) (Love postcards), a talk by Angelo Pinci on our grandparents' love letters and postcards, in the ITOP conference hall. Later, from 6 to 8 pm, the Albergo Ristorante Stella hosts the second session of ["Troppo Donna – Ritrova te stessa"](/en/eventi/troppo-donna-palestrina-2026): mental coach Monia Cupellini talks about the "logical levels of change". It's free, but booking is recommended.

Above Cervara di Roma the [ceramics workshop](/en/eventi/laboratorio-ceramica-campaegli-cervara-di-roma-2026) at the Campaegli mountain hut continues until 28 November: ask the organisers for days and times.

## Saturday: Sanvitese, Palestrina and music in the evening

It starts in the village: [Sanvitese Calcio a 5](/en/eventi/sanvitese-calcio-a5-sporting-fondi-2026-11-21) play at home against Sporting Fondi, in round 11 of Serie C1, with kick-off at 4 pm at the municipal futsal pitch.

Walkers can set off early for Jenne: at 9:30 am the [Aniene loop from Jenne to Trevi](/en/eventi/anello-aniene-jenne-trevi-2026) begins, about 14 km with an environmental guide to the Trevi waterfall, back around 3:30 pm. It costs 20 euros and only goes ahead with a minimum number of participants, so book early.

A quarter of an hour from home, at 11 am, Capranica Prenestina opens the [new displays of the Prenestini Mountains Natural History Museum](/en/eventi/museo-naturalistico-capranica-prenestina-2026) in Palazzo Barberini, with a tasting of local products at 12:30 pm. It's a good way to get to know the Prenestini woods before Sunday's hikes at the Mentorella.

Palestrina has a full Saturday. At Pierluigi's birthplace the [Dipinti&Vini d'Autore](/en/eventi/dipinti-e-vini-d-autore-palestrina-2026) exhibition opens, pairing works by fifteen artists with local wines, from 10 am to 1 pm and from 3 to 5 pm. The ["Gerbera Rossa"](/en/eventi/gerbera-rossa-palestrina-2026) day against gender violence begins at 11 am with a red bench in the Valvarino park; at 5 pm there's a flash mob in Piazza Santa Maria degli Angeli, and at 6 pm, in the Sala Borghesani, the exhibition "Nonostante tutto VIVO" opens, on view until 25 November. On the same theme, in Valmontone, ["Non chiamatelo amore"](/en/eventi/non-chiamatelo-amore-valmontone-2026) brings the photo exhibition "Mai più silenzio" to Palazzo Doria Pamphilj at 5 pm and, at 6 pm, the theatre reading "Quello che resta. Per Giulia, per tutte". In the evening, at 8 pm, La Muracciola restaurant hosts a dinner show with the [Sugar Soul Band](/en/eventi/sugar-soul-band-zucchero-palestrina-2026) and the songs of Zucchero: 40 euros, booking required. At the Auditorium Pierluigi, on Saturday and Sunday, the students of Arte Madre stage ["A Midsummer Night's Dream"](/en/eventi/sogno-notte-mezza-estate-palestrina-2026); times have not been announced yet.

In the afternoon, at 4 pm, Colonna opens its [Christmas Cellars](/en/eventi/cantine-di-natale-colonna-2026): the old cellars of the historic centre reopen with music, bagpipers and the arrival of Father Christmas, and at 7 pm the Little Big Band of Colonna plays its Saint Cecilia concert. The cellars already open at 11 am, with markets, tastings and children's workshops, and a free shuttle bus runs to the historic centre from the car park in via Colle Sant'Andrea.

Fans of video games or digital art can pop over to Zagarolo: Palazzo Rospigliosi hosts the Italia Media Art Festival exhibition and, on Saturday and Sunday morning, ["On The Road"](/en/eventi/on-the-road-game-design-imaf-zagarolo-2026), the international conference on game design. It's free, but you need to register online by 1 pm on Friday.

In Cave the Teatro Comunale hosts the first of two [Saint Cecilia concerts](/en/eventi/concerti-santa-cecilia-cave-2026), with the Banda Città di Cave. Classical music lovers, meanwhile, have the weekend's biggest event at 8:45 pm: at Palazzo Colonna in Genazzano the Orchestra delle Cento Città, the Corale Perosi and the Schola Cantorum Belli perform ["Mozart: Grazia e Invenzione"](/en/eventi/corale-perosi-schola-cantorum-belli-genazzano-2026), with Symphony No. 29 and the Coronation Mass. It's the feast of the Virgo Fidelis: beforehand, at 6 pm, there's Mass at the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Good Counsel. Booking for the concert is required, by email to fondazionepalestrina@gmail.com.

## Sunday: autumn colours in the Prenestini and Simbruini, bands, cellars and another day of the exhibition

Sunday morning is for those who want to walk in the mountains, and for once you don't need to go far: two free hikes through the autumn woods leave from the car park of the Mentorella Sanctuary, both by booking. At 8 am there's ["Discovering the colours of autumn"](/en/eventi/colori-autunno-mentorella-castel-san-pietro-2026), with the Pro Loco of Castel San Pietro Romano and CAI Palestrina, about 7 km through the Faggeta delle Meridiane beech wood to Monte Spina Santa; at 9 am the Pro Loco of Capranica leads the [Mentorella Loop](/en/eventi/escursione-foliage-anello-mentorella-2026), with an environmental guide and at most 25 places.

Further afield, in the Simbruini, from Cervara di Roma, at 9 am, a hike sets off for [Rocca La Prugna](/en/eventi/rocca-la-prugna-cervara-di-roma-2026), the ancient stronghold destroyed in the 17th century: about 12 easy km, passing the red deer wildlife area. It costs 15 euros and you need to book on WhatsApp by Saturday. If you prefer a slower pace, head up to Monte Livata for [Shinrin-yoku](/en/eventi/shinrin-yoku-monte-livata-2026), "forest bathing" from 9:30 am to 1:30 pm for National Tree Day, also by booking. If you'd rather run, head down to Colleferro for the [Trofeo Santa Barbara](/en/eventi/trofeo-santa-barbara-colleferro-2026), a 9.8 km competitive race through the town centre starting at 9:30 am from the "Maurizio Natali" stadium: registration is online via Race Service.

Cave closes its Saint Cecilia celebrations with a concert by the Banda G. Puccini, again at the Teatro Comunale. If you went down to Colleferro for the race in the morning, it's worth going back in the afternoon: at 5 pm the exhibition ["Oltre il silenzio"](/en/eventi/oltre-il-silenzio-colleferro-2026) against violence against women opens in the former BPD headquarters, and at 5:30 pm the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica celebrates Saint Cecilia with the traditional music of ["Incanto Popolare"](/en/eventi/incanto-popolare-santa-cecilia-colleferro-2026). In Colonna the cellars stay open for a second day, with music by Ilgra and the Corrida talent show. In Palestrina there's still time for Dipinti&Vini d'Autore, with the same hours as Saturday, and for the second performance of "A Midsummer Night's Dream".

**This week's tip:** on Saturday at 4 pm go and cheer on Sanvitese at the pitch, and then, if you still feel like going out, in the evening there's Mozart at Palazzo Colonna in Genazzano, ten minutes away by car.

**If you only have half a day:** Saturday afternoon in Colonna for the opening of the Christmas Cellars, with the bagpipers and the 7 pm concert.

Times, prices and contacts for each event are on the [events](/en/eventi/) pages: it's always worth checking before you set off, as programmes can change.
