It's the last weekend of November and the calendar is starting to talk about Christmas: in Colonna the cellars reopen for a second weekend, in Gallicano the first Christmas markets appear, and Sunday is the first Sunday of Advent. Meanwhile the theatres around us are starting their new seasons, and on Sunday afternoon the first appointment is right here in San Vito, at the Teatro Caesar.

## Friday: cinema in San Cesareo

In San Cesareo, at 5 pm, the municipal Auditorium hosts ["Quanto Basta"](/en/eventi/quanto-basta-san-cesareo-2026), the film competition organised with the Artefix association: the competing works are screened, followed by a public debate and the awards. It closes "Respect", the town's programme against violence against women.

## Saturday: Saint Andrew, autumn flavours and Pierluigi in the Cathedral

Gallicano nel Lazio starts celebrating its patron with the [Feast of Saint Andrew the Apostle](/en/eventi/festa-sant-andrea-gallicano-nel-lazio-2026): on Saturday the Rosary at 5:30 pm and Mass at 6 pm, then the feast continues until Monday 30, with the procession and the band concert.

In Colonna the [Christmas Cellars](/en/eventi/cantine-di-natale-colonna-2026) are back: the old cellars of the historic centre open from 11 am, on Saturday and Sunday, with markets, tastings, children's workshops and Father Christmas's little house. A free shuttle bus runs from the car park in via Colle Sant'Andrea.

If you fancy polenta, chestnuts and mulled wine, Colleferro's covered market hosts the Pro Loco's [Sapori d'Autunno](/en/eventi/sapori-d-autunno-colleferro-2026) (Autumn flavours), on Saturday from 6 pm to midnight. With children you can arrive earlier: at 5 pm, at the Teatro Vittorio Veneto, there's ["Rime insaponate"](/en/eventi/rime-insaponate-colleferro-2026), the first show of the children's theatre series. Saturday is also the last day to see the exhibition ["Oltre il silenzio"](/en/eventi/oltre-il-silenzio-colleferro-2026) against violence against women.

Lovers of sacred music have their evening appointment in Palestrina: at 7:15 pm, in the Cathedral of Sant'Agapito, the [Cappella Musicale di Santa Maria in Via](/en/eventi/cappella-musicale-santa-maria-in-via-palestrina-2026) sings Pierluigi da Palestrina's Missa "L'Homme armé", in the composer's own town.

In Monte Compatri, on Saturday and Sunday at 6 pm, the free series ["Omaggio ai Grandi del Cinema"](/en/eventi/omaggio-grandi-del-cinema-monte-compatri-2026) remembers Alberto Sordi, Mario Verdone and Angelo Infanti, the actor from Zagarolo; Sunday is devoted to Manfredi, Tognazzi, Anna Magnani and Lina Wertmüller. In the Simbruini, meanwhile, ["Tracce d'inverno"](/en/eventi/tracce-d-inverno-simbruini-2026-11-28) offers walks and creative workshops on Christmas traditions around Subiaco, Filettino and Vallepietra: ask the organisers for the place and times.

## Sunday: a walk, then an afternoon at the theatre

The morning is for walking: CAI Palestrina leads the [Fossa Ampilla](/en/eventi/sentieri-borghi-fossa-ampilla-cai-palestrina-2026) loop between Genazzano and Rocca di Cave, past the Cretaceous fossil reef, with about 600 metres of ascent. Sign up at the section by 8 pm on Friday.

The afternoon, instead, is for the theatre, and there's plenty of choice. In San Vito, at 5 pm, the Teatro Caesar opens its free "AmiAMO il Teatro" season with ["Trilussa e il vino"](/en/eventi/trilussa-e-il-vino-san-vito-romano-2026), starring Sergio Zecca. At 5:30 pm, at the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano, there's ["Aldo Fabrizi"](/en/eventi/aldo-fabrizi-genazzano-2026), with Fabrizio Gaetani; in Cave the TrePerCaso company brings Maurizio de Giovanni's ["Il silenzio grande"](/en/eventi/il-silenzio-grande-cave-2026) to the Teatro Comunale. In Colleferro, at 6 pm, the Vittorio Veneto season opens with ["Romeo e Giulietta – L'amore è saltimbanco"](/en/eventi/romeo-e-giulietta-colleferro-2026), a comic take on Shakespeare. All shows are in Italian.

Also in Colleferro on Sunday, [Sapori d'Autunno](/en/eventi/sapori-d-autunno-colleferro-2026) is open from 11 am to 9 pm, and at 6:30 pm, in the church of the Immacolata, the Ensemble Butterfly remembers the Maestro with the [First Memorial for Maestro Giuseppe Pignatelli](/en/eventi/concerto-santa-cecilia-pignatelli-colleferro-2026). The date and venue have changed since the first announcement. In Palestrina, at 5 pm, the Auditorium Pierluigi hosts the [closing evening of Gerbera Rossa](/en/eventi/gerbera-rossa-serata-auditorium-palestrina-2026), with music and testimonies against violence against women, free admission. In Gallicano, for Saint Andrew, there's the solemn Mass at 11 am with the bishop and the Christmas market from 10 am to 1 pm.

**This week's tip:** on Sunday at 5 pm stay in the village for "Trilussa e il vino" at the Teatro Caesar: it's free, it's on our doorstep, and it opens a season that in December also brings "Il malato immaginario" and a comedy gala to San Vito.

**If you only have half a day:** on Sunday morning, a stroll through Colonna's Christmas Cellars, then back to San Vito for the 5 pm show.

Times, prices and contacts for each event are on the [events](/en/eventi/) pages: it's always worth checking before you set off, as programmes can change.
