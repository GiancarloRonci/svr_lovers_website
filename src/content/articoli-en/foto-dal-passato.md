Old postcards, family photos, faded snapshots found in a drawer: small fragments of memory that tell us what San Vito Romano looked like before time changed its streets, squares and habits. On this page we collect vintage images of the town, to look at them together and rediscover familiar places through the eyes of those who lived there before us.

We open the gallery with a postcard of the Church of San Vito: the façade with its dome, the entrance doorway, the bare trees lining the avenue and the cobbled path rising towards the church, with a small figure standing in front of the steps.

The gallery will grow over time: if you have old photos or postcards of San Vito Romano and would like to share them, post them in the [San Vito Romano Lovers Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/sanvitoromano.lovers).
