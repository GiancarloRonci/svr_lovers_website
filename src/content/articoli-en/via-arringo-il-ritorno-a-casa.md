*A Christmas to find one another again.*

The streets where we were born and grew up, or where our grandparents, aunts and uncles lived, are never forgotten. Even when their doors stay shut for years, they keep waiting for someone.

And there are places that belong not only to those who live there today, but also to those who left them, to those who grew up there and to those who still carry a piece of their own story in their hearts.

I wanted to imagine [Via Arringo](/en/luoghi/via-dellarringo/) like this: a winter evening, at Christmas time, dressed up for the holidays, with snow, lights and the warmth of the houses.

Not an empty street, but a street that moves you.
A street that invites you to come back.

## Via Arringo – Coming Home

Why not meet right here, perhaps during the coming Christmas holidays, or in the warmer season, for a day devoted to memories, reunions and rediscovering this corner of our village?

Those who still have a house here, those who once lived here, those who keep photographs, stories and memories: why not start with us at San Vito Lovers, as well as with the Pro Loco?

It doesn't take a big organisation to light a first light.
A few people, a shared idea and the wish to meet again are enough.

And perhaps, from that day, something could be born that lasts well beyond a single celebration.

Because our village doesn't just need to be remembered.
It needs to be lived in.

Sometimes, to find the way home, all it takes is for someone to leave a light on, waiting for us in our holiday best.
