---
title: '"Angelo Sallusti" scholarship 2025/2026, beneficiaries approved'
description: The Municipality has approved the lists of beneficiaries of the "Angelo Sallusti" scholarship for the 2025-2026 school year, for lower and upper secondary schools.
data: 2026-10-23
---

The lists of beneficiaries of the "Angelo Sallusti" scholarship for the 2025-2026 school year, renewed again this year by the Municipality, have been approved. The approval came with executive decision no. 952 of 23 October 2026, after applications closed on 30 September.

There are two lists, one for lower secondary school and one for upper secondary school, and they show the registration (protocol) number of each application: applicants can therefore check the outcome by looking for their own number. The decision and the lists are attached to the Municipality's notice.

For information, contact the General Secretariat at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
