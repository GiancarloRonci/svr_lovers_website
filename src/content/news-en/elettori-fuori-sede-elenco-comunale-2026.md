---
title: Voting away from home, applications by 31 December for the municipal list
description: People living in San Vito Romano for study or work but registered to vote in another region can apply by 31 December 2026 to be added to the municipal list of away-from-home voters, so they can vote here in the 2027 elections.
data: 2026-10-28
---

With Law no. 180 of 9 October 2026, voting away from home becomes permanent for general elections, European elections and referendums. The Municipality has therefore set up a municipal list of away-from-home voters, ahead of the elections expected in 2027.

Voters can register if they are temporarily domiciled in San Vito Romano for study or work, for at least nine months, and are on the electoral roll of a municipality in another region. Applications must be submitted **by 31 December 2026** to the municipality where you are temporarily domiciled: in the same way, people from San Vito who study or work outside Lazio must apply to the municipality where they are currently living.

The application must include a copy of a valid identity document and a document proving your away-from-home status, for example a university enrolment certificate, or a contract or employer's statement showing the length of employment; a self-declaration is also accepted. The application form, as a fillable PDF, is attached to the Municipality's notice and is also available on the Ministry of the Interior website (dait.interno.gov.it/elezioni).

Applications can be handed in at the Registry and Electoral Office, Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, during opening hours, or sent by email to protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it or by certified email (PEC). For information: tel. +39 06 9571006 (extension 2), demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
