---
title: "Villa Comunale: please respect the work site areas"
description: The Mayor asks visitors not to cross the fences around the extraordinary maintenance works at the Villa Comunale "V. Bachelet" and to keep an eye on children.
data: 2026-10-14
---

At the [Villa Comunale "V. Bachelet"](/en/luoghi/villa-comunale/) extraordinary maintenance works are under way and will continue for some time; after the various areas of the park, they will also reach the small wood. So as not to close the park entirely, the works are being carried out zone by zone, with work site areas fenced off, so that the rest of the park remains open to visitors.

However, the Municipality reports that the fences are often knocked down, causing damage to the contractor and risks for park visitors. In a notice signed by Mayor Maurizio Pasquali, everyone is asked to respect the fenced-off areas and, in particular, those accompanying children are asked to watch them so that they do not enter the work sites. If the situation does not change, the Municipality warns that it may take more drastic measures.

For information: Land Office, territorio@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it, tel. +39 06 9571006.
