---
title: "Forest fire prevention: measures in force until 15 October"
description: "Under order no. 26/2026, the Municipality has set forest fire prevention measures for the highest-risk period, from 15 June to 15 October 2026."
data: 2026-06-08
---

With order no. 26 of 2026, the Municipality of San Vito Romano has set forest fire
prevention measures ahead of the highest-risk period, from 15 June to 15 October 2026, with
the related penalties for non-compliance.

The full text of the order is available in the attachments published on the Municipality's
website. In case of emergency, these numbers remain active: 112 (emergency services), 803
555 (Regional Civil Protection Operations Centre) and 06.9571006 ext. 3 (San Vito Romano
Local Police).

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3593810/ordinanza-n-26-2026-applicazione-misure)*
