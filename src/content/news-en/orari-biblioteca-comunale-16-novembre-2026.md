From **Monday 16 November 2026** the town library of San Vito Romano returns to its usual opening hours. It is in the "Dott. Carlo De Paolis" Conference Hall, at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 32, and is open:

- **Monday** from 9 am to 1 pm;
- **Wednesday** from 9 am to 1 pm;
- **Friday** from 9 am to 1 pm.

There is also a small surprise for those who come to borrow a book: gadgets from the Prenestino Library System, which the library belongs to.

For information: 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3681593/orari-biblioteca-comunale-16-novembre-2026)*
