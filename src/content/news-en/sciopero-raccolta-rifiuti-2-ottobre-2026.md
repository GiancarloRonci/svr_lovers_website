---
title: Strike on Friday 2 October, possible disruption to waste collection
description: Because of the strike on Friday 2 October 2026, the urban waste collection scheduled for that day may be disrupted.
data: 2026-09-30
---

The Municipality advises that on Friday 2 October 2026, because of the strike called for that day, there may be disruption to the urban waste collection scheduled for that date.

The notice gives no details on minimum services or on when missed collections will be made up: for information, please contact the town council offices.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3656388/avviso-sciopero-generale-venerdi-17-ottobre-2025)*
