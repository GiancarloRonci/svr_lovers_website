---
title: "Town council meeting called for Tuesday 27 October"
description: An extraordinary public session at 6:00 PM in the council chamber, with two questions from the opposition on the municipal swimming pool and the Cotral bus service.
data: 2026-10-20
---

The San Vito Romano town council has been called to an extraordinary public session on **Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 6:00 PM**, in the council chamber of the town hall in Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34. The notice of meeting is signed by Mayor Maurizio Pasquali.

On the agenda, after the approval of the minutes of previous sessions, are the answers to two questions from the council group "Insieme per San Vito". The first concerns the failure to restore access for people with reduced mobility to the municipal swimming pool, reported by the group on 8 August. The second concerns their urgent request for clarification on the Cotral bus service in San Vito Romano, which has so far gone unanswered. The session closes with the Mayor's announcements.

The session is open to the public. The full text of the notice of meeting is attached to the news item on the Municipality's website.
