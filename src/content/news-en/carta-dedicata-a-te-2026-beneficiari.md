The Municipality has acknowledged the list of beneficiaries of the 2026 "Carta dedicata a te" ("A card for you"), provided for by the interministerial decree of 24 June 2026. Beneficiaries are selected and the contribution is paid by INPS, the national social security institute, not by the Municipality. In the coming days the Social Services office will contact those concerned to complete the procedure.

The contribution is loaded onto prepaid, rechargeable electronic cards from Poste Italiane, provided through Postepay and delivered to those entitled. These are the dates to remember:

- **from 4 November 2026:** the first 500 euros are credited;
- **by 16 December 2026:** first purchase, required to activate the card and keep it valid;
- **from April 2027:** the second instalment is paid.

The Municipality's notice comes with the administrative act, the list, the interministerial decree and the list of goods that can be purchased. For information, contact the General Secretariat at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
