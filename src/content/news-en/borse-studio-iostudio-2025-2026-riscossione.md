title: "\"IoStudio\" scholarships 2025/26, payments under way"
description: The Lazio Region has announced that the "IoStudio" scholarships for the 2025/26 school year, worth 207.12 euros each, are being paid out.
data: 2026-10-01
---

The "IoStudio" scholarships for the 2025/26 school year are being paid out, the Lazio Region has announced. Each scholarship is worth 207.12 euros.

First-time recipients can collect it at any post office, by presenting the "Borsa di Studio" Postepay card, their tax code and a valid identity document. The "IoStudio" Student Card is not required.

Those who received the scholarship in previous years do not need to go to the post office: the 2025/26 payment is credited directly to the Postepay card they already hold.

The payment procedure set out by the Ministry of Education and Merit is described in [this document](https://mycity.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/5349745/Modalita_di_erogazione_MIM.pdf) (in Italian). For information, contact the School Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, call +39 06 9571006 or write to tributi@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3657196/borse-studio-io-s-2024-2025)*
