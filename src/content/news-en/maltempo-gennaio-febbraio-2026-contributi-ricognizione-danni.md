title: Bad weather of January and February 2026, damage survey and first grants open
description: "Families and businesses hit by the bad weather of early 2026 can apply for a grant of up to 5,000 euros for their home and up to 20,000 euros for businesses: applications must reach the Comune by 10 December."
data: 2026-11-23
---

Anyone who suffered damage from the bad weather of January and February 2026 can now apply for financial help. For those events the Council of Ministers, with its resolution of 14 July 2026, declared a twelve-month state of emergency in the province of Frosinone and the Metropolitan City of Rome, allocating 3.2 million euros; with civil protection order OCDPC no. 1207 of 11 August, the regional director of Emergency and Civil Protection of Lazio was appointed Delegated Commissioner.

The Comune has published the notice with the three measures available:

- **support for residents**: a grant of up to **5,000 euros** for each household whose main home was damaged (form B1);
- **recovery of economic and productive activities**: a grant of up to **20,000 euros** per business (form C1);
- **needs assessment** for repairing private buildings and damage to businesses, using the same forms B1 and C1.

Completed forms must reach the Comune **by 5:30 pm on 10 December 2026**, as set out in the notice. The [notice](https://mycity.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/5446030/AVVISO_ALL_AI_CALAMITA_2026.pdf), [form B1](https://mycity.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/5446029/Mod._B1_ricogn._danni_sostegno_alla_popolazione.pdf) and [form C1](https://mycity.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/5446031/Mod._C1_ricogn._danni_sostegno_alle_attivita.pdf) (in Italian) can be downloaded from the Comune's website. Information: Planning Office, 06 957 1006, urbanistica@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
