---
title: "2025 rent allowance: payments made to beneficiaries"
description: The Municipality announces that it has issued the payment orders for the 2025 rent allowance to the beneficiaries on the ranking.
data: 2026-10-19
---

The Municipality of San Vito Romano has issued the payment orders for the rent allowance (contributo per la locazione) for the year 2025. The amounts due are being paid to those who applied and were listed as beneficiaries in the ranking published earlier.

The notice does not state the individual amounts, which depend on each person's position in the ranking.

For information: Economic and Financial Department of the Municipality, via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, San Vito Romano, phone +39 06 957 1006, ragioneria@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3665388/contributo-locazione-annualita-2025)*
