---
title: Register of polling station presidents, applications by 31 October
description: The Municipality is updating its register of polling station presidents for 2026. Those not yet listed who meet the requirements can apply by 31 October.
data: 2026-10-01
---

As it does every year, the Municipality is updating its register of polling station presidents. Anyone who is not yet listed and meets the requirements set by law can ask to be added.

Applications must be submitted **by 31 October 2026**, using the form published by the Municipality together with the notice: both can be downloaded from the news page on the municipal website (in Italian).

For information, contact the Demographic and Cemetery Services Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, call +39 06 9571006 or write to demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it (certified email: protocollo@pec.comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it).

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3655240/avviso-aggiornamento-albo-presidenti-seggio)*
