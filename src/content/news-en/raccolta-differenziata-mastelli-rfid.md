---
title: "Recycling: new bins with RFID tags now required"
description: "Order no. 28/2026 requires organic and residual waste to be placed in bins fitted with RFID tags, for households, businesses and public events."
data: 2026-06-12
---

With order no. 28 of 2026, the Municipality has introduced new rules to improve recycling:
organic and residual waste must now be placed only in bins fitted with RFID tags, properly
closed, with no bags left outside. Anyone who doesn't have one yet can collect it for free
at the municipal offices.

The requirement applies to all households and businesses, as well as to associations,
committees and organisers of public events (fairs, festivals, food events), who must ensure
proper waste separation with clear signage and correctly placed bins. Compliance checks will
be carried out by the Local Police together with the waste operator, with penalties for
non-compliant disposal.

The goal is to raise the Municipality's recycling rate, currently stalled at around 70%.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3597391/ordinanza-28-azioni-miglioramento-raccolta)*
