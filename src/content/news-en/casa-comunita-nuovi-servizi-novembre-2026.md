San Vito Romano's Community Health Centre (Casa di Comunità), at **via Guido Baccelli 35**, has added services. The CUP desk is now open Monday to Friday, and there are two new specialist clinics, pulmonology and diabetology, which you book at the CUP.

The updated hours:

- **CUP** (collecting test results, bookings, choosing a GP, exemptions): Monday to Friday 8:00-12:45; on Thursdays and on the first and third Saturday of the month 10:15-12:45.
- **Blood tests**: every Thursday and the first and third Saturday of the month, 8:00-10:00.
- **GP**: Monday 14:00-17:00, Tuesday 8:00-13:30, Wednesday 11:00-17:00.
- **Nursing clinic** (with a prescription and a CUP booking): Monday to Saturday 8:00-14:00, Mondays and Wednesdays until 17:00.
- **Pulmonology** (book at the CUP): Monday 9:00-11:00.
- **Diabetology** (book at the CUP): third Wednesday of the month, 8:00-17:00.

On site you can only pay by card (POS), not in cash.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3688440/nuovi-servizi-attivi-presso-casa-comunita-san)*
