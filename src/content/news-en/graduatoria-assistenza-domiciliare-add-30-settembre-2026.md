---
title: "District home care service: ranking updated to 30 September"
description: The RM 5.5 Social and Health District has approved the update to 30 September 2026 of the ranking for the District Home Care service (ADD).
data: 2026-10-16
---

A management decision has approved the update to 30 September 2026 of the ranking for the District Home Care service (Assistenza Domiciliare Distrettuale, ADD), run by the RM 5.5 Social and Health District.

The updated ranking is published as a PDF ("Aggiornamento graduatoria al 30.09.2026 - ADD") on the notice page of the Municipality's website.

For information: District Social Plan Office (Piano di Zona Distrettuale), via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, San Vito Romano, distrettorm5.5@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3664382/aggiornamento-30-settembre-2026-graduatoria)*
