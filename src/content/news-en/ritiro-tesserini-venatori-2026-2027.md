---
title: Hunting permits, new opening hours at the local police office
description: The office was closed on Monday 7 September for operational reasons, so pickup of the 2026/2027 hunting season permits moved to new dates.
data: 2026-09-04
---

For operational reasons, the local police office was unable to issue hunting permits for the 2026-2027 hunting season on Monday 7 September. The office therefore opened on an extraordinary basis on Thursday 10 September, from 3:30 PM to 5:30 PM, and on Friday 11 September, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Anyone unable to collect their permit on those dates can do so on Monday 28 September, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Please remember to bring all original documentation: photocopies or photos taken on a mobile phone are not accepted.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3641458/ritiro-tesserini-venatori-stagione-caccia-2026)*
