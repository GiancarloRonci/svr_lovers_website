---
title: All Souls' Day, traffic changes around the cemetery
description: From 30 October to 3 November 2026 there are no-entry and no-parking rules and a one-way system in Via dell'Olmata because of the larger number of visitors to the municipal cemetery; on 2 November the Local Police office is closed to the public.
data: 2026-10-23
---

For All Souls' Day, when many more visitors come to the municipal cemetery, traffic in the access roads changes. This is set out in ordinance no. 42 of 23 October 2026, in force **from 8:00 AM on Friday 30 October to 6:00 PM on Tuesday 3 November**.

During those days:

- **no entry** on the last stretch of Via dell'Olmata, up to the main entrance of the cemetery;
- **no parking** in the car parks in front of the cemetery entrance;
- **one-way traffic** on Via dell'Olmata towards Circonvallazione Vitellia, from Via del Castagno to the cemetery entrance, with **parking allowed on the right-hand side** of the same stretch;
- **no entry** on Via dell'Olmata towards Piazza Roma, from the cemetery entrance to Via del Castagno.

The no-entry and no-parking rules do not apply to vehicles of people with disabilities, emergency vehicles and Red Cross volunteers, or vehicles authorised for cemetery maintenance and for transporting flowers.

Because officers will be on duty in the streets, on Monday 2 November the Local Police office is not open to the public (8:45 AM-1:00 PM); opening hours resume on Thursday 5 November, from 3:15 to 5:30 PM. For urgent matters, write to polizialocale@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it or call +39 06 9571006.
