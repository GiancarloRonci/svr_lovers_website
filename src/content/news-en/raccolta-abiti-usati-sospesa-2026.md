---
title: "Used clothing collection temporarily suspended"
description: "The service is on hold while the operator changes: the Municipality asks residents not to leave clothes in or next to the bins until it resumes."
data: 2026-10-20
---

In San Vito Romano the collection of used clothing is **temporarily suspended**: the service is being renewed, with a switch to a new operator. The Municipality has not yet said when it will resume and says that updates will follow.

In the meantime, residents are asked not to leave used clothing in the collection bins or next to them, and not to dump bags in areas not intended for this purpose: it is best to keep them at home until the service resumes.

For information: Land Office, territorio@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it, tel. +39 06 9571006.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3665522/conferimento-abiti-usati-temporanea-sospensione)*
