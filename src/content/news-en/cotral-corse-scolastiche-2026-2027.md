---
title: School transport, Cotral confirms services for the 2026/2027 year
description: No changes to student bus services, also guaranteed in areas where the new network units are being rolled out.
data: 2026-09-03
---

With the start of the new school year, Cotral informed the Municipality that it is confirming its school bus service programme without changes: the same routes and schedules already in place last year will be maintained, including in areas where the new Network Units (UdR) will be progressively activated.

Continuity of service is guaranteed by Lazio Region resolution no. 494 of 25 June 2026, which supports the rollout of the Network Units while ensuring the daily commute of students.

Anyone using only Cotral services needs a Metrebus Lazio pass valid for the relevant travel zones; anyone who also uses Network Unit services (such as urban routes run by private operators) needs a Metrebus Plus pass instead. Timetables are available on the cotralspa.it website and the Cotral app.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3640994/cotral-comunicazione-orari-scolastici-abbonamenti)*
