---
title: Private home slaughtering, forms available for the 2026-2027 season
description: "The forms for private home slaughtering in the 2026-2027 season can be collected from the Local Police office, open on Monday mornings and Thursday afternoons."
data: 2026-11-13
---

Anyone planning to slaughter a pig or other animals at home for family consumption in the 2026-2027 season can already collect the forms. The Municipality announces that the forms, with the instructions to follow, are available at the **Local Police office**, in the Town Hall at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34.

The office is open to the public on **Mondays from 8:45 am to 1 pm** and on **Thursday afternoons from 3:15 to 5:30 pm**.

For information, call the Municipality on 06 9571006 or write to protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3681621/avviso-macellazione-domestica-privata-stagione)*
