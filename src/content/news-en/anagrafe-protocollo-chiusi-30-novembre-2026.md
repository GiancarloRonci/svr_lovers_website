On **Monday 30 November 2026** the registry office (anagrafe and stato civile) and the protocol office of the Municipality of San Vito Romano are closed to the public. That morning the Prefecture of Rome carries out its routine yearly inspection.

If you need a certificate or have documents to hand in, it's best to go on another day. The offices are at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34; for information call 06 9571006 or write to demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3689024/chiusura-ufficio-anagrafe-stato-civile-protocollo)*
