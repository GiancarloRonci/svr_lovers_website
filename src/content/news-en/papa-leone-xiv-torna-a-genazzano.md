Pope Leo XIV will return to Genazzano on 7 September 2026, at 6:00 pm, to celebrate
Mass at the Sanctuary of the Mother of Good Counsel, on the eve of the feast of the
Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary. The news, reported by Laura Ferri on
montiprenestini.info, closely concerns the whole Monti Prenestini area, which San
Vito Romano is also part of.

This is not the Pontiff's first visit to the sanctuary: Pope Leo XIV had already
gone there on 10 May 2025, just two days after his election, a testament to the
deep bond that ties him to the Augustinian community. The Augustinians are indeed
the custodians of the Sanctuary of Genazzano, one of the most venerated Marian
shrines in Lazio, and represent a community to which the Pope has been personally
tied since the beginning of his religious life.

For the occasion of the 7 September event, local authorities and the Vatican
Gendarmerie, together with the Police Forces, have arranged a reception and
security plan designed to ensure both the Pontiff's protection and the possibility
for residents and pilgrims to take part in the Eucharistic celebration safely.

The event is expected to be a moment of great spiritual and symbolic significance
for the whole Monti Prenestini area, drawing faithful and pilgrims from across the
region and beyond. For those who live in San Vito Romano and the surrounding
towns, it is a unique opportunity to attend a celebration presided over directly
by the Holy Father just a few kilometres from home.

Stay tuned to this page for further logistical details, final timings and traffic
information, which will be released in the days before the event.
