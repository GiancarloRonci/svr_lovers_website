---
title: School meals and school bus, third child exempt in the 2026/2027 school year
description: "The Town Council executive has set the fees for school meals and school transport for 2026/2027 and exempted the third child and any further children from payment."
data: 2026-11-13
---

Good news for large families. With Council executive resolution no. 9 of 2026, the Municipality has set the fees for the **school meals** and **school transport** services for the 2026/2027 school year and approved the **exemption from payment from the third child onwards**.

The notice comes with the sending of the school meal and school bus invoices. For questions about the amounts or the exemption, contact the Municipality's School Office, at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, by writing to tributi@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3681350/comunicazione-fatture-mensa-scuolabus-s-2026-2027)*
