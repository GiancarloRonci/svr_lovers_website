---
title: Municipal cemetery closed from 12 to 16 October
description: From Monday 12 to Friday 16 October 2026 the municipal cemetery is closed for cemetery works; access is guaranteed only for funerals.
data: 2026-10-08
---

The municipal cemetery of San Vito Romano is closed from Monday 12 to Friday 16 October 2026 to allow cemetery works to be carried out. The closure was ordered by ordinance no. 40 of 8 October 2026.

During the week, access is guaranteed only for funerals. In the event of bad weather the works, and therefore the closure, may be postponed by a new ordinance.

For information, contact the Demographic and Cemetery Services Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34: tel. +39 06 9571006, demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it. The text of the ordinance is attached to the Municipality's notice.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3660398/chiusura-cimitero-comunale-lunedi-10-novembre)*
