---
title: Call for the "Angelo Sallusti" scholarship 2025/2026
description: Applications due by Wednesday 30 September for the scholarship reserved for students of the 2025-2026 school year.
data: 2026-08-31
---

The Municipality has approved the call for applications for the "Angelo Sallusti" scholarship, for the 2025-2026 school year.

Applications, using the forms attached to the call, must be submitted by Wednesday 30 September 2026 at the latest, through one of the following channels: email to protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it, certified email (PEC) to protocollo@pec.comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it, or in person at the registry office (Monday 9:00 AM-1:00 PM and 3:30 PM-5:30 PM, Wednesday 9:00 AM-1:00 PM, Thursday 3:30 PM-5:30 PM). The subject line of the email or PEC must read "DOMANDA BORSA DI STUDIO ANGELO SALLUSTI anno scolastico 2025-2026".

The call for applications, forms and privacy notice are available in the attachments published on the Municipality's website.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3637293/borsa-studio-angelo-sallusti-anno-scolastico-2024)*
