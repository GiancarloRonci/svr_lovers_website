---
title: "Town council meeting called for Tuesday 24 November"
description: An extraordinary public session at 6:30 PM in the council chamber, with joining the CER Italia foundation, a change to the waste tax rules and the standing council committees.
data: 2026-11-16
---

The San Vito Romano town council has been called to an extraordinary public session on **Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 6:30 PM**, in the council chamber of the town hall in Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34. The notice of meeting is signed by Mayor Maurizio Pasquali.

After the approval of the minutes of the previous session, the council will discuss an agreement with the Municipality of Montevarchi, under article 15 of Law 241/1990, and joining the CER Italia participation foundation. This is followed by an amendment to the rules on the waste tax (Tari-Ttp), approved in March this year.

The council group "Insieme per San Vito" will then propose setting up standing council committees, and the Mayor will answer a question from the group "Ci siamo per San Vito Romano". The session closes with the Mayor's announcements.

The session is open to the public. The full text of the notice of meeting is attached to the news item on the Municipality's website.
