title: SanvitoLab, the co-working space open in San Vito Romano
description: Equipped workstations, ultra-broadband connection and free consulting services in via Borgo Mario Theodoli, by reservation.
data: 2026-09-06
---

San Vito Romano now has its own co-working space: it's called SanvitoLab and it's located at via Borgo Mario Theodoli n. 34. The project was made possible by funding from the Lazio Region, under the "Community Workshops" call for the modernisation of employment policies (D.E. G05680 of 27 April 2023), and provides the community with a modern space designed for work and training.

The space offers 24 equipped workstations with ultra-broadband internet connectivity, a multimedia area and meeting rooms, as well as CAF and Patronato services and consulting on fiscal, legal, business and European funding matters.

Access is free and by reservation, aimed at freelancers, remote-working employees, startups and students. To book a workstation, simply send the completed request form to coworking@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it. The space is open Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

To stay updated on initiatives and events organised at the space, you can follow the Instagram profile [@sanvitolab_spaziocoworking](https://www.instagram.com/sanvitolab_spaziocoworking/) and the Facebook page [SanvitoLab](https://www.facebook.com/sanvitolab).
