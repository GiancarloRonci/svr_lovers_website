title: Registry and records offices closed on the afternoon of Monday 23 November
description: "On Monday 23 November 2026 the registry office and the records office will not receive the public in the afternoon because of a training course for municipal staff: morning hours are unchanged."
data: 2026-11-21
---

On **Monday 23 November 2026, in the afternoon**, the registry office (anagrafe and stato civile) and the records office (protocollo) of the Comune di San Vito Romano will be closed to the public. All municipal staff are attending the compulsory workplace safety training course.

In the morning the offices open as usual, **from 8:45 to 13:00**, at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34.

For information: 06 9571006, demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3686007/chiusura-pubblico-ufficio-anagrafe-stato-civile)*
