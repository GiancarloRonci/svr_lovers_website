---
title: "25 November, International Day against violence towards women"
description: "The Municipality marks the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women and reminds residents of the 1522 helpline, active throughout Italy."
data: 2026-11-24
---

Wednesday **25 November** is the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, established by the United Nations General Assembly with resolution 54/134 of 17 December 1999. The Municipality of San Vito Romano marks it with a statement inviting everyone to reflect on gender-based violence and its consequences for society as a whole.

The Municipality calls on institutions to organise initiatives, meetings and educational activities on managing emotions and on balance between men and women, and stresses that every form of violence, whoever it targets, is a defeat for everyone.

Anyone who needs help can call **1522**, the free national helpline against violence and stalking, active throughout Italy. An updated list of support centres is on the [1522.eu](https://www.1522.eu/mappatura-1522/) website.
