---
title: School bus, provisional timetables from 15 to 25 September
description: The Municipality has published the provisional school bus timetables for nursery and primary pupils ahead of the 2026/2027 school year.
data: 2026-09-11
---

The Municipality has published the provisional timetables for the school bus service for nursery and primary school pupils, valid for the week from 15 to 25 September 2026, the first days of the new school year.

All pupils enrolled in the service must hand the bus attendant, on the first day they use the service, the authorisation form allowing them to get off alone or in the presence of a designated adult: without this document, pupils will not be allowed on board. Those who have already submitted it to the registry office do not need to do so again.

First-year nursery school pupils and pupils living in Pisoniano will not yet use the service during this period.

Detailed timetables for both nursery and primary school are available in the attachments published on the Municipality's website.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3646591/servizio-scuolabus-s-2025-2026)*
