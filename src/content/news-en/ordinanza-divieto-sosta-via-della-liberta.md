---
title: No-parking order on via della Libertà for roadworks
description: "Order no. 39/2026: forced removal for vehicles parked in front of no. 3 via della Libertà, from 6 AM on 12 September until the end of the works."
data: 2026-09-11
---

With order no. 39 of 2026, the Municipality's Public Works Area has established a no-parking zone with forced vehicle removal on via della Libertà, at house number 3, starting from 6:00 AM on Saturday 12 September 2026 and lasting until the ongoing works are completed.

The full text of the order is available in the attachments published on the Municipality's website.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3646440/ordinanza-n-39-2026-divieto-sosta-rimozione-via)*
