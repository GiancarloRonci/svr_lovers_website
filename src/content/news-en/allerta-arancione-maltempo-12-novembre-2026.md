From the early afternoon of Thursday 12 November and for the following 24-30 hours, that is until **Friday 13 November**, Lazio Civil Protection has issued an **orange alert** for hydrogeological and hydraulic risk and a **yellow alert** for wind in zone G, the Liri Basin, which includes San Vito Romano. The Municipality has published the regional bulletin and urges the utmost caution when travelling.

Scattered to widespread rain is expected, especially inland, including heavy showers or thunderstorms with frequent lightning and gusts of wind. On the Apennine ridges the south-westerly wind may reach strong gale force.

Useful numbers: Lazio Civil Protection operations room 803 555, Fire Brigade 115, European emergency number 112. For information from the Municipality: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
