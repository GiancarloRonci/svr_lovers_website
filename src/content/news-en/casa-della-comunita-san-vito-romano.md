---
title: San Vito Romano's Community Health Centre is now open
description: "At via Guido Baccelli 35, the first nursing, medical and administrative services run by ASL Roma 5 have started, and will be expanded progressively."
data: 2026-06-01
---

The Casa della Comunità (Community Health Centre) has opened in San Vito Romano, at via
Guido Baccelli n. 35: a facility that brings healthcare and administrative services closer
to residents, run by ASL Roma 5 in cooperation with the Municipality.

For June 2026 a first set of nursing, medical and administrative services has started, and
will be expanded and enriched in the coming months. The detailed schedule is available in
the attachments published on the Municipality's website.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3591023/attivazione-casa-comunita-san-vito-romano-servizi)*
