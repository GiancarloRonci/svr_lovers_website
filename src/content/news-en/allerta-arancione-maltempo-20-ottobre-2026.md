From late on the evening of Tuesday 20 October and for the following 18-24 hours, that is for much of **Wednesday 21 October**, Lazio Civil Protection has issued an **orange alert** for bad weather in zone G, the Liri Basin, which includes San Vito Romano. The Municipality has published the regional bulletin and urges caution.

Scattered to widespread rain is expected, including heavy showers or thunderstorms with frequent lightning, local hail and strong gusts of wind.

Useful numbers: Lazio Civil Protection operations room 803 555, Fire Brigade 115, European emergency number 112. For information from the Municipality: Secretariat, tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
