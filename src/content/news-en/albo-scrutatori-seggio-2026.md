---
title: Register of polling station scrutineers, applications by 30 November
description: The Municipality is updating its register of polling station scrutineers for 2026. Those not yet listed who meet the requirements can apply by 30 November.
data: 2026-10-01
---

As the law requires every year, the Municipality is updating its register of polling station scrutineers. Anyone who is not already listed and meets the requirements, which are set out in the notice, can apply to be added.

Applications must be submitted **by 30 November 2026**, using the form published by the Municipality: the notice and the form can be downloaded from the news page on the municipal website (in Italian).

For information, contact the Demographic and Cemetery Services Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, call +39 06 9571006 or write to demografici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it (certified email: protocollo@pec.comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it).

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3653303/avviso-aggiornamento-albo-scrutatori-seggio)*
