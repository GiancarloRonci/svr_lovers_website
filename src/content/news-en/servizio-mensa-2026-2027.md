---
title: School canteen, menus published for 2026/2027
description: The Municipality has published the summer and winter school canteen menus for the 2026/2027 school year.
data: 2026-09-28
---

For the 2026/2027 school year the Municipality has published the school canteen menus in two versions: the summer menu, valid from 1 April to 31 October, and the winter menu, from 1 November to 31 March. Both can be downloaded as PDFs from the Municipality's website.

This week the canteen follows the first week of the summer menu.

For information, contact the School Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, or write to tributi@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3654918/servizio-mensa-s-2025-2026)*
