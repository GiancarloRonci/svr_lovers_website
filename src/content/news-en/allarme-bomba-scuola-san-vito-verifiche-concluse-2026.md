---
title: "Bomb threat at the San Vito school: checks completed, no danger"
description: "On Wednesday 11 November the San Vito Romano branch of the Garibaldi comprehensive school was also evacuated after the bomb threat emailed to schools all over Italy: checks by the Carabinieri ruled out any explosives."
data: 2026-11-11
---

On the morning of Wednesday 11 November the San Vito Romano branch of the Istituto Comprensivo Garibaldi, whose main seat is in Genazzano, was among the schools reached by the bomb threat sent by email to schools all over Italy. The building was evacuated and the competent authorities, together with the Carabinieri, carried out the checks.

The checks ended by ruling out the presence of any explosives. Mayor Maurizio Pasquali thanked the Carabinieri and the school staff, teachers, operators and educators, who led the evacuation of the children.

In its notice the Municipality also reports the words of Education Minister Giuseppe Valditara, who urged people not to give in to alarmism: according to the minister it was a cyberattack, and the very high number of schools involved makes the threat hardly credible.
