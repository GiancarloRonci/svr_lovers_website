title: Snow in Guadagnolo, road to the Mentorella Sanctuary closed
description: Bad weather caught some people out with their cars at the Mentorella Sanctuary. They are all well, but the road is closed and the Municipality of Capranica Prenestina is asking people to limit travel towards Guadagnolo.
data: 2026-11-01
ente: Municipality of Capranica Prenestina
---

The sudden change in the weather and the snow that fell on Guadagnolo have left some people stranded with their cars at the [Mentorella Sanctuary](/en/luoghi/santuario-della-mentorella). The Municipality of Capranica Prenestina reports that they are all well: the problem is the steep climb, made treacherous by the snow, which cars without suitable equipment cannot manage.

The Carabinieri, the Civil Protection teams of Capranica Prenestina and Castel San Pietro Romano and the Palestrina Fire Brigade are on the scene to bring everyone back safely.

The access road to the Sanctuary remains closed to traffic until it is safe again. The Municipality asks people to limit travel towards Guadagnolo, so as not to hinder the rescue teams, and to follow the instructions of the authorities and crews on site. Updates will come through the Municipality's channels.
