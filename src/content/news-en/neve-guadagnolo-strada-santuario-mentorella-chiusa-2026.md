title: Snow in Guadagnolo, rescue completed and road to the Mentorella Sanctuary reopened
description: Bad weather caught some motorists out at the Mentorella Sanctuary. Those involved were brought to safety and, according to the local press, the road has been cleared and traffic restored.
data: 2026-11-01
ente: Municipality of Capranica Prenestina
---

**Update, 2 November:** the rescue operation ended successfully, as announced by the Civil Protection, and everyone involved was brought to safety. According to the local press the road has been cleared of snow and traffic restored, but no time was given and we have found no official confirmation of the reopening from the Municipality: anyone heading up to Guadagnolo should check the road conditions first. Staff from ARES 118 Lazio, the regional emergency medical service, also took part in the operation, which was activated by the Regional Operations Room.

The sudden change in the weather and the snow that fell on Guadagnolo on the afternoon of 1 November left some people stranded with their cars at the [Mentorella Sanctuary](/en/luoghi/santuario-della-mentorella). The Municipality of Capranica Prenestina reported that they were all well: the problem was the steep climb, made treacherous by the snow, which cars without suitable equipment could not manage.

The Carabinieri, the Civil Protection teams of Capranica Prenestina and Castel San Pietro Romano and the Palestrina Fire Brigade went to the scene to bring everyone back safely.

During the operation the access road to the Sanctuary was closed to traffic and the Municipality asked people to limit travel towards Guadagnolo, so as not to hinder the rescue teams.
