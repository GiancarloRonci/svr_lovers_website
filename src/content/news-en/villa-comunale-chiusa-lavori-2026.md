title: Villa Comunale closed for works until 10 December

From today, Tuesday 3 November 2026, the whole [Villa Comunale](/en/luoghi/villa-comunale/) "Vittorio Bachelet" is closed to the public. The renovation works announced in mid-October have turned the entire park into a building site, and mayoral ordinance no. 43 of 2026 forbids entering or staying inside it.

The ban remains in force **until the works are finished, expected on 10 December 2026**, unless extended. The full rules are in the text of [ordinance no. 43](https://mycity.s3.sbg.io.cloud.ovh.net/5409663/ORDINANZA-43.pdf), published by the Municipality.

For information, contact the Public Works Office (lavoripubblici@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it) or the Municipal Secretariat (protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it), both on 06 9571006.

*Source: [Municipality of San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3675679/avviso-importante-ordinanza-sindacale-n-43-2026)*
