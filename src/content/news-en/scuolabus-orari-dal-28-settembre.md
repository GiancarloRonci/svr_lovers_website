---
title: School bus, new timetable from 28 September
description: The school bus timetable valid from 28 September 2026 for nursery and primary school has been published.
data: 2026-09-24
---

From 28 September 2026 the updated school bus timetable for the 2026/2027 school year takes effect. Detailed timetables for nursery and primary school are available as PDFs on the Municipality's website.

For first-year nursery pupils, during the settling-in period the service starts on 28 September for outbound trips and on 5 October for return trips.

For information, contact the School Office at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, or write to tributi@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3652910/servizio-scuolabus-orari-16-20-settembre)*
