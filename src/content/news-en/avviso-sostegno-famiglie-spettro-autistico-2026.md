---
title: Public notice for support to families of minors on the autism spectrum
description: Applications due by 2:00 pm on 16 October for support measures for families with minors on the autism spectrum, 2026.
data: 2026-09-14
---

The Municipality, through the RM 5.5 District Zone Plan office, has published the notice for support measures for families with minors on the autism spectrum aged 0 to 17 years and 364 days, for the year 2026 (resolution no. 775 of 14 September 2026).

Applications, accompanied by the "Support Request" and "Expense Declaration" forms available on the Municipality's website, must be submitted no later than 2:00 pm on Friday 16 October 2026.

For information, contact the RM 5.5 District Zone Plan office, at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 34, or write to distrettorm5.5@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3647564/avviso-pubblico-interventi-sostegno-famiglie)*
