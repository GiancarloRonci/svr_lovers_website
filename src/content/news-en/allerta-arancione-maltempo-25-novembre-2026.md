From the morning of **Wednesday 25 November** and for the following 18-24 hours, the Lazio Regional Functional Centre has set an **orange alert** for hydrogeological risk and a **yellow alert** for hydraulic risk in zone G, the Liri Basin, which includes San Vito Romano. The alert follows severe weather warning no. 26079 from the national Civil Protection Department, published by the Municipality together with the regional bulletin.

Scattered to widespread rain is expected across Lazio, especially in the southern areas, including showers or thunderstorms with heavy downpours, frequent lightning and gusts of wind. It is best to travel only when necessary and to take care near roads and ditches.

Useful numbers: Lazio Civil Protection operations room 803 555, Fire Brigade 115, European emergency number 112. For information from the Municipality: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
