---
title: World Tourism Event in Assisi, the Municipality collects promotional material from businesses and artisans
description: Anyone wanting to promote their business at the Assisi tourism fair can drop off brochures and catalogues at the town hall by 17 September.
data: 2026-09-11
---

On 24 and 25 September 2026, the Rome Capital Metropolitan City will take part in the World Tourism Event in Assisi with a stand and a panel dedicated to promoting the territories and tourism of the metropolitan area's 120 municipalities, including San Vito Romano.

To give visibility to local products, the municipal administration invites local businesses, farmers and artisans to bring their promotional material (brochures, catalogues and the like) to the town hall by Friday 17 September 2026 at the latest. The material collected, together with brochures about the area, will then be taken to the Metropolitan City offices for display at the fair.

*Source: [Comune di San Vito Romano](https://comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it/notizie/3646707/world-tourism-event-assisi-24-25-settembre)*
