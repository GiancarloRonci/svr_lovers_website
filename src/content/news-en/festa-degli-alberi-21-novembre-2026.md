title: "21 November, Tree Day and its link to Guido Baccelli"

---

**21 November** is Italy's National Tree Day, and the Comune di San Vito Romano has looked back at its history, which begins with a name close to home: minister **Guido Baccelli**. With a circular of 27 June 1899, he promoted the first autumn school walk dedicated to the celebration.

A royal decree of 2 February 1902 made it official in every Italian municipality, but without a fixed date. 21 November only came in 1951, with a circular by minister Amintore Fanfani, which allowed high-mountain municipalities to move it to 21 March. Since 2013 a national law has recognised 21 November as the "National Tree Day".

A curiosity: Baccelli chose 21 November because it was Queen Margherita's birthday, and the first celebration, in Rome, was held by planting trees along via Latina.
