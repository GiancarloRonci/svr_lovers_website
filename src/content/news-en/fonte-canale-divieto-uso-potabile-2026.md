---
title: Fonte Canale, do not drink the water from the fountain
description: With ordinance no. 41 of 23 October 2026 the mayor bans the drinking and food use of the water from the public fountain in Via del Canale, after ARPA Lazio found total coliforms in it.
data: 2026-10-23
---

The water from the public fountain in Via del Canale, the Fonte Canale, can no longer be drunk or used for cooking. This is established by mayoral ordinance no. 41 of 23 October 2026, signed by the mayor, Maurizio Pasquali.

The measure follows a note from the ASL Roma 5 health authority dated 22 October: checks by ARPA Lazio found total coliforms in the fountain's water. For public health reasons, its use for drinking, including indirect ingestion, and for food is therefore banned.

The ban remains in force until it is documented that the causes have ceased; the Municipality has said that updates on the restoration of safe conditions will follow. The ordinance is posted on the official notice board (Albo Pretorio), on the municipal notice boards and at the fountain. For information: Local Police, tel. +39 06 9571006, polizialocale@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
