---
title: Municipal library, reduced opening hours until 30 October
description: Until 30 October 2026 the municipal library opens only on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, at varying times, because of staff training.
data: 2026-10-07
---

Until Friday 30 October 2026 the municipal library is keeping reduced opening hours: the staff are taking part in the training activities of the Sistema Bibliotecario Prenestino (the Prenestine library network). The library opens only on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, at times that change from day to day:

- Wednesday 7 October: 9:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Friday 9 October: 9:00 AM-12:00 PM
- Monday 12 October: 9:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Wednesday 14 October: 9:00 AM-12:00 PM
- Friday 16 October: 9:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Monday 19 October: 9:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Wednesday 21 October: 11:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Friday 23 October: 9:00 AM-1:00 PM
- Monday 26 October: 9:00 AM-12:00 PM
- Wednesday 28 October: 9:00 AM-12:00 PM
- Friday 30 October: closed

The library is at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 32. For information: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it.
