---
title: Books on Augmentative and Alternative Communication arrive at the library
description: Thanks to funding from the Ministry of Culture, the municipal library is expanding its catalogue with books devoted to inclusion, available for loan from 27 October 2026.
data: 2026-10-07
---

The municipal library of San Vito Romano has obtained substantial funding from the Italian Ministry of Culture (MiC) to buy new books. Part of it goes towards inclusion: the catalogue is growing with titles for teaching, home and professional use devoted to cognitive disabilities, language difficulties and special educational needs.

The first books on Augmentative and Alternative Communication can already be consulted in the library and will be available for loan from Tuesday 27 October 2026. The cataloguing of the other volumes will be completed over the coming months.

The library is at Via Borgo Mario Theodoli 32. For information: tel. +39 06 9571006, protocollo@comune.sanvitoromano.rm.it. Until 30 October the library is keeping [reduced opening hours](/en/news/biblioteca-orari-fino-al-30-ottobre-2026).
