---
title: The municipal coat of arms and banner
description: The warrior with the palm of martyrdom and the silver column that tell the village's history through images.
data: 1956-03-16
---

The coat of arms and banner of San Vito Romano were formally recognised by decree
of the President of the Republic on 16 March 1956. The coat of arms depicts Saint
Vitus as a young warrior with the palm of martyrdom and a red cloak, beside a
silver column taken from the coat of arms of the Colonna family, the former lords
of the fief; the whole is surmounted by a sixteen-pointed sun, a symbol of
eternity.
