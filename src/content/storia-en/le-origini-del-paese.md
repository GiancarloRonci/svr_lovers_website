---
title: The origins of the town
description: From the Aequi to Roman Vitellia, the ancient roots of the San Vito Romano area.
data: 0100-01-01
---

The area where San Vito Romano now stands was inhabited as early as the 10th
century BC by the Aequi, an ancient Italic people settled along the Aniene
valley, who long resisted Rome's expansion before being subdued in 456 BC.

Many scholars believe the town's origins are linked to **Vitellia**, an ancient
Roman settlement whose name would derive from the gens Vitellia. Its existence
is recorded by ancient historians and writers such as Livy, Pliny the Elder and
Stephanus of Byzantium. However, there is no certain archaeological evidence
linking the present-day town of San Vito Romano to the ancient city of Vitellia:
the link rests mainly on tradition and the continuity of the name over the
centuries.
