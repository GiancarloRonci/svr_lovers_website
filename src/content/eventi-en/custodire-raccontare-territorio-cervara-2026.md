On Saturday 24 October 2026 Cervara di Roma, the hilltop village of the Simbruini Mountains, celebrates the completed restoration of the Ponte della Valle with a day of free events promoted by the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park, to discover the village, its stories, its flavours and its sky.

The programme includes guided tours of the village, sun observation from the Fortress of Cervara and a tasting of local products, which must be booked at info@ethea.org. Times for the individual activities are not given: ask at the same address or at the Park office (tel. +39 0774 827221).

The day opens "Custodire e raccontare il territorio" ("Caring for and telling the story of the land"), the Park's programme of hikes, guided tours, workshops and tastings in the Simbruini villages running until 26 December.
