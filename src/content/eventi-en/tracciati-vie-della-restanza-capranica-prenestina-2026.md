On Sunday 18 October 2026 at 4:30 PM, the series "Tracciati. Luoghi, storie e culture attraversando il Museumgrandtour" comes to Capranica Prenestina with the talk "Le vie della restanza (The paths of staying). Paths, communities and cultural landscapes", at the Civic Natural History Museum of the Monti Prenestini.

The speakers are Patrizia di Fazio, Roberta Iacono and Libero Middei. Promoted by the Castel San Pietro Romano Widespread Museum and the Civic Natural History Museum, the event reflects on walking routes and on the choice to keep living in the villages of the Monti Prenestini. Participation is free.
