On Sunday 27 September 2026 at 9:00 PM, Enrico Nigiotti takes the stage in Piazza Umberto Pilozzi, in the centre of Valmontone, as part of his "Maledetti Innamorati Summer Tour". The singer-songwriter from Livorno, born in 1987, brings his heartfelt pop, centred on feelings and relationships and built on a direct connection with the audience, to a live setting.

Admission is free, and the concert closes the Sunday of the feast of the patron saint San Luigi Gonzaga, which the same weekend also features a procession, fireworks and the Fiera delle Merci market fair.
