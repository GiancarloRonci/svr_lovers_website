On Friday 13 November 2026, at 5 pm, Palazzo Conti hosts the conference "Francesco Ficoroni: una figura principe dell'antiquaria romana" (a leading figure of Roman antiquarianism), organised by the Municipality of Labico and the Francesco Ficoroni Study Centre. After welcoming remarks from the mayor Danilo Giovannoli, the councillor for culture Clementina Miele and Anna Lucia Paris, president of the Castello di Lugnano association, the meeting is chaired by Prof. Arnaldo Colasanti.

Speakers include Marzia Guerrieri, on collecting and the drawings market in early 18th-century Rome, and Luigi Lavia, on the Roman antiquarian world; the guest of honour is Thérèse Ridley, with a talk on the historian Pietro Giannone and the Inquisition. Professors Ronald T. Ridley and Valentino Nizzo also take part.

Francesco de' Ficoroni was born in Lugnano, today's Labico, in 1664 and died in Rome in 1747: an antiquarian, archaeologist and collector, he guided travellers on the Grand Tour. His name is tied to the Ficoroni Cista, the bronze casket found in 1738 between Palestrina and Labico and now in the National Etruscan Museum of Villa Giulia.
