On Sunday 25 October 2026, at 5:30 PM, the MuDi, the Museo Diffuso of [Castel San Pietro Romano](/en/luoghi/castel-san-pietro-romano), hosts a screening of the short film "Porry Pastorel - Dovere Civile" (Civic Duty). With this event the village remembers the Nazi massacre it escaped on 25 October 1943, an episode also told in Leonardo Nardi's book "Episodi della seconda guerra mondiale vissuti dalla nostra gente" (Episodes of the Second World War lived by our people).

The film centres on Adolfo Porry Pastorel, the photojournalist who had taken refuge in the village and put his civic sense at the service of the community, helping to save the whole population from a Nazi massacre. The short film was made by the Libera Università del Cinema together with Off RomeTour, thanks to a Lazio Region call for the eightieth anniversary of the Liberation, and was also presented at the Rome Film Fest. The story is by Igor Geat, the cinematography by Jessica Giaconi, and the historical advice by Dr Roberta Iacono.

After the institutional greetings by Maria Roscicarelli, the speakers are Roberta Iacono, director of the MuDi, and the mayor, Gianpaolo Nardi. A special thought goes to Angelo Ferracci, a witness of those days and the oldest of the film's extras.

The event is part of the October calendar in the Monti Prenestini put together by Visit Monti Prenestini. The day before, Saturday 24, the MuDi hosts the presentation of the book ["Mare Nero"](/en/eventi/mare-nero-ansaldo-castel-san-pietro-romano-2026) by Marco Ansaldo.
