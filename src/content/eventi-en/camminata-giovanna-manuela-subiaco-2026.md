On Sunday 11 October 2026 the walk "Ricordando Giovanna e Manuela" (Remembering Giovanna and Manuela) returns to Subiaco for its sixth edition, as part of the "Subiaco in Rosa" programme. The start is at 9:30 AM from Piazza del Campo.

The route, about 5 km long and walked at your own pace, follows the foothill trails and the historic centre of Subiaco, passing the Monastery of Santa Scolastica, the medieval Valle quarter and the old mills. Donations collected go to Komen Italia, for breast cancer prevention and treatment.

For information: +39 328 453 8950, ss-antares@tiscali.it.
