On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 5:00 PM, the Castagnata (Chestnut Party) returns to Jenne: everyone meets in the public gardens of Piazza Vittorio Emanuele III to share the flavours of autumn.

It is a simple get-together, with chestnuts and good company, organised by the Pro Loco and the Senior Citizens' Centre (Centro Anziani) of Jenne, and open to everyone.
