On Sunday 29 November 2026, at 6:00 PM, the 2026-2027 subscription season of the Teatro Comunale Vittorio Veneto in Colleferro opens with "Romeo e Giulietta – L'amore è saltimbanco" ("Romeo and Juliet – Love Is a Street Performer"), written and directed by Marco Zoppello, who is on stage with Anna De Franceschi and Michele Mori. The show is produced by Stivalaccio Teatro and TSV – Teatro Nazionale.

It is a comic reading of Shakespeare: two street performers who have escaped the Inquisition must perform the story of Romeo and Juliet for Henry III, and Veronica Franco appears in the little square and tries her hand at the part of Juliet. The result is an open rehearsal full of dialects, songs, duels and pantomime, in the spirit of popular theatre.

The season is organised by the Municipality of Colleferro with ATCL. A full-price ticket costs 20 euros plus a 2-euro booking fee, a reduced ticket 17 euros plus 2, and an under-30 reduced ticket 10 euros; in the gallery, 18 euros plus 2, or 15 plus 2 reduced. The box office, at Via Artigianato 47, is open from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM on the days before the show and from 4:00 PM on the day itself. For information: 345 798 3567, 06 870 85371, teatrocomunalecolleferro@gmail.com.

The programme comes from the local press: it is worth checking with the theatre before you set off.
