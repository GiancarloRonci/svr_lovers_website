On Sunday 4 October 2026 the SMuRC, the museum system of Rocca di Cave, invites families to play with science. The museum is open from 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM, with a day of activities ranging from palaeontology to astronomy.

It starts at 11:00 AM in the planetarium with the "Regata Spaziale" (Space Regatta), an interactive race for children aged 6 to 11. At 11:30 AM there is the "Dinosauri con i Mattoncini" workshop for children aged 6 to 10: they learn about the great reptiles of the past and build a brick dinosaur to take home. The fulldome show "Dalla Terra all'Universo" (From Earth to the Universe) follows at 12:30 PM, and a guided tour of the Geo-Palaeontological Museum at 4:30 PM.

Booking is required and is done online on the [SMuRC booking site](https://smurc-prenotazioni.sumupstore.com/). For information: [www.smurc.it](https://www.smurc.it/).
