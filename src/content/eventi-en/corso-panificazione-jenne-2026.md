On Sunday 11 October 2026, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, the Antico Forno di Jenne (the village's old bakery) hosts "Gli antichi mestieri: corso di panificazione" (Old crafts: bread-making course), a workshop on the secrets of bread: basic techniques, leavening and baking in the oven. The initiative is part of the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park programme and is designed for families and children.

Participation costs 25 euros per person and must be booked with La Forneria di Nonna Mirella: tel. 335 699 3778, info@laforneriadinonnamirella.com.
