To mark Italy's National Tree Day on 21 November, on Sunday 22 November 2026 Casa Ahisma offers a morning of Shinrin-yoku, the Japanese practice of "forest bathing", in the woods of Monte Livata, in collaboration with and under the patronage of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park.

From 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM the group walks slowly through the forest, with no hurry and no destination, stopping to listen to the sounds of the woods and breathe in their scents. Over the same weekend, on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22, the organisers also offer individual tree-inspired treatments, booked separately.

To book a place or ask for information, call or message +39 338 391 1606. The Park's page does not give the cost or the exact meeting point: ask when booking. The activity is led in Italian.
