On Wednesday 25 November 2026, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro hosts "Voci oltre il silenzio" ("Voices beyond silence"), a theatre reading freely based on Serena Dandini's "Ferite a morte", combining theatre, music and words to give a voice to those who too often go unheard.

The show is a collaboration between the Istituto Stanislao Cannizzaro, the Libero Sorriso association and dance educator Francesca Pisatri, under the patronage of the Comune di Colleferro. There are two performances: in the morning from 9:00 to 11:00 and in the afternoon at 17:00. No tickets are sold. The reading is in Italian.
