The 102nd Sagra dell'Uva of Marino, Italy's oldest grape festival (first held in 1925), runs over two weekends this year: after 26 and 27 September, the celebration returns from Saturday 3 to Monday 5 October 2026 in the historic centre of this Castelli Romani town. Admission is free: you only pay for tastings, stands and taverns.

On Saturday 3 October the Food Village and the Coldiretti Village open at 10:00 AM, with reenactments and guided tours during the day and, at 8:30 PM in Piazza Matteotti, a show by DJ Fargetta. Sunday 4 October is the main day: at 10:30 AM a solemn Mass in the Basilica of San Barnaba celebrated by Cardinal Marcello Semeraro, at 3:30 PM the historical parade re-enacting Marcantonio Colonna's return from the Battle of Lepanto, and at 5:30 PM the famous "miracle of the fountains", when wine flows from the Fountain of the Four Moors. The evening ends with a show by Francesco Cicchella at 9:30 PM and fireworks at 11:45 PM.

Monday 5 October is the quieter "Sagra dei Marinesi": the last historical parade at 5:00 PM, the second miracle of the fountains at 6:45 PM and closing fireworks. Free shuttles run from the car parks; Marino can also be reached by train (FL4 line, Marino Laziale station).
