On Friday 18 September 2026, at 9:00 PM, the Cathedral of Sant'Agapito in Palestrina hosts the concert "Sulle ali del Genio" (On the Wings of Genius), a tribute to Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart performed by the Nova Amadeus ensemble under the direction of Maestro Mirca Rosciani.

The event is part of the celebrations for the 500th anniversary of the birth of Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, organised by the Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina Foundation, and offers residents, enthusiasts and visitors a chance to experience music in one of the town's most evocative historic settings.

For information about attending, contact the Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina Foundation.
