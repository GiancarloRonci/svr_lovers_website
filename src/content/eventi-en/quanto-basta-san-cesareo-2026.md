On Friday 27 November 2026, at 5 pm, the San Cesareo municipal auditorium hosts "Quanto Basta" (Just enough), a film competition open to young people and adults, organised by the Municipality with the cultural association Artefix. The afternoon features the screening of the works in competition, followed by a debate open to the audience and the final awards ceremony.

The event closes "Respect: mettiamo a fuoco il rispetto", the Municipality's programme for the International Day against violence against women, which on Wednesday 25 November brings the [students' march "Dal Silenzio alla Voce"](/en/eventi/dal-silenzio-alla-voce-san-cesareo-2026) to the streets.
