On Saturday 24 October 2026 Pisoniano hosts the 21st edition of the Corn Pizzocchio Festival, organised by the Confraternita del Santo Rosario. Pizzocchio is the town's traditional corn flatbread, served with local sausage and broccoli rabe sautéed with garlic and chilli; the menu also includes French fries, traditional sweets, local wine and drinks.

The food stands open at 7:30 PM among the lanes and small squares of the historic center, and the evening goes on with live music, dancing and stornelli folk songs until late at night.
