On Sundays 11, 18 and 25 October 2026, from 10am to 1pm, La Mieleria nel Bosco on Monte Livata opens its doors for "Alla scoperta del magico mondo delle api" (Discovering the magical world of bees), a morning devoted to bees and hive products.

The programme includes watching the glass-sided teaching hives, a visit to the apiary, honey-extraction and beeswax candle workshops, a honey tasting and a walk through the beech forest. The fee is 12 euros.

*Source: [La Mieleria nel Bosco](https://www.lamielerianelbosco.it/eventi/)*
