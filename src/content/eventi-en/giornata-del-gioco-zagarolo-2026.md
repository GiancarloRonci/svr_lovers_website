On Friday 23 October 2026, from 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM, the historic centre of Zagarolo turns into a big playground for World Play Day ("Giornata del Gioco"), promoted by the Toy Museum (Museo del Giocattolo): between Piazza Paparelli and Corso Vittorio Emanuele there are 17 stations with 19 games to try. In the morning, from 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon, there are also games and activities for children in Valle Martella, in Via Vittorio Alfieri, run by the Istituto Comprensivo Rita Levi Montalcini.

The town council provides a shuttle bus for children from Valle Martella: it leaves Via Vittorio Alfieri at 4:20 PM for the historic centre and returns at around 7:00 PM from Piazza del Mercato. For information: Museo del Giocattolo, tel. +39 06 95769405, museodelgiocattolo@comunedizagarolo.it.

*Source: [Zagarolo Toy Museum](https://www.museogiocattolo.it/eventi-mostre/)*
