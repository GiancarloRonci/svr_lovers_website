On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 3:30 to 5:30 PM, the Oratorio San Pietro Apostolo in Poli hosts "Holyween", a party for children, teenagers and families organised by the town's youth leaders and educators as an alternative to Halloween.

The programme includes face painting, team challenges and group games, along with the stories of the local saints as examples of generosity and of commitment to others. For information, write to parrocchiapoli@gmail.com.
