On Saturday 19 December 2026 "Esperienze di territorio" ("Local experiences") by ASD Vivere la montagna returns to Monte Livata, an outdoor activity to discover Subiaco's mountain slowly and sustainably, even in winter. On the same day the [snowshoe hike to the summit](/en/eventi/ciaspolata-monte-autore-livata-2026) of nearby Monte Autore is scheduled.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 328 838 3863, viverelamontagna@gmail.com.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144185)*
