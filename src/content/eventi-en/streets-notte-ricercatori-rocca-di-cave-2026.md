On 25 September 2026, from 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM, the "Ardito Desio" Geopalaeontological Museum in Rocca di Cave takes part in "Streets", the European Researchers' Night, with an afternoon of workshops, interactive activities and guided tours.

Admission is free but reservation is required. The detailed programme of activities was still being finalised at the time of publication: check the museum's official channels for updates before visiting.
