On Saturday 10 October 2026 the 18th Sagra delle Tacchie ai Funghi Porcini in Bellegra hosts 883 Max Mania, a tribute band bringing back the pop hits of 883 and Max Pezzali. The concert starts at 9:30 PM in Piazza del Municipio; at 11:30 PM the evening continues with a DJ set.

The festival's food stands stay open during the concert, serving tacchie pasta with porcini mushrooms and other traditional dishes. The next day, Sunday 11 October, the festival closes with the "Gens Vitellia" historical parade.
