Valmontone celebrates its patron saint, Saint Aloysius Gonzaga (San Luigi Gonzaga), with several days of free events. It opens on Thursday 24 September at 5:30 PM at Palazzo Doria Pamphilj with the award ceremony of the 4th Premio Letterario Città di Valmontone literary prize.

On Saturday 26 September, a solemn Mass is held at 7:00 PM in the Collegiata di Santa Maria Maggiore, followed at 9:00 PM by a procession through the old town with the band, and at 10:00 PM by fireworks in Piazza Umberto Pilozzi.

On Sunday 27 September, from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, the Fiera delle Merci market fair fills Via Sant'Antonio; at 10:00 AM a memorial on Viale XXV Aprile honours the two American soldiers who died in Valmontone during the Liberation, and from 10:30 AM Palazzo Doria Pamphilj opens for guided tours as part of the Museumgrandtour. At 9:00 PM, Enrico Nigiotti plays a free concert in Piazza Umberto Pilozzi. On Monday 28 September the feast closes with demonstrations by local sports clubs, a tractor gathering in Piazza Brodolini and activities organised by the town library.
