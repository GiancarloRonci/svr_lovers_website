On Sunday 29 November 2026, at 5 pm, the Auditorium Pierluigi in Palestrina hosts the closing evening of the second edition of "Gerbera Rossa – Tra arte, musica e parole" ("Art, music and words"), the initiative against gender violence organised by the Praeneste Eventi APS association with the patronage of the Comune of Palestrina. The programme features music and testimonies; full details are on the event poster.

Admission is free. The evening closes the Comune's "Mai più violenza sulle donne" ("No more violence against women") programme, which began on 21 November with a flash mob in Piazza Santa Maria degli Angeli and the exhibition "Nonostante tutto VIVO" in the Sala Borghesani.
