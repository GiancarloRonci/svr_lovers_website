This year Christmas in Zagarolo is called "Natale d'Incanto" and runs from 5 December 2026 to Epiphany. It is organised by the town council.

It starts on Saturday 5 December at 17:00 at Palazzo Rospigliosi, inside the Toy Museum, with two exhibitions: one of photographs devoted to Angelo Frontoni, and "Ciak, si gioca!", on the faces and films that made cinema history. Concerts, shows, workshops, children's activities and Christmas markets in the old town will follow in the next weeks, but the dates and times of each event have not been published yet. Keep an eye on the Comune di Zagarolo's channels.
