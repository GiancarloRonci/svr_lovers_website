On Saturday 5 December 2026 the ASD Vivere la montagna association offers "Esperienze di territorio" ("Local experiences") at Monte Livata, an outdoor activity to discover Subiaco's mountain slowly and sustainably, even in the cold season. It is repeated on Saturday 19 December.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 328 838 3863, viverelamontagna@gmail.com.
