On Saturday 19 September 2026, Colleferro hosts the "Notte Bianca di Fine Estate" (End of Summer White Night), an evening designed to turn the close of the summer season into an occasion for celebration and to promote the local area.

The initiative, organised by the town council, aims to encourage participation and liveliness in the town, with shops open and activities planned to accompany residents and visitors as they say goodbye to summer.

*The detailed programme for the evening will be announced by the town council in the days before the event.*
