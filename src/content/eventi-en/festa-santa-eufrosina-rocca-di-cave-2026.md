On 27 September 2026, Rocca di Cave celebrates its patron saint, Saint Eufrosina, with a mass and procession through the village streets from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, accompanied by the "Giacomo Puccini" marching band.

The day ends in Piazza San Nicola Vescovo with a tombola draw at 6:00 PM and a shared moment with pasta and sandwiches for all participants.
