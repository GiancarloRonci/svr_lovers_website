On Saturday 31 October 2026 the church of San Francesco in Palestrina hosts a concert by the choir I Cantori di Caudium, part of the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina devoted to choral music and the great composer's heritage.

The start time has not been announced yet: for information on time and admission, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
