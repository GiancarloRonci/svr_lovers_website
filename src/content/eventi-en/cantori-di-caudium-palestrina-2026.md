On Saturday 31 October 2026, at 6:30 PM, the church of San Francesco in Palestrina hosts a concert by the vocal ensemble I Cantori di Caudium, part of the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina devoted to choral music and the great composer's heritage.

The concert is titled "Armonia del Cielo: Il Palestrina e de Victoria tra polifonia e preghiera" (Harmony of Heaven: Palestrina and de Victoria between polyphony and prayer) and is conducted by Pompeo Ferraro, with works by Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina and Tomás Luis de Victoria. For information on admission, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
