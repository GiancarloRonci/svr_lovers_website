On Sunday 4 October 2026, from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, the Craft Markets ("Mercatini dell'Artigianato") return to Colleferro, organised by the Town Council together with the LepinArt association. The stalls are set up along Viale XXV Aprile, in the town centre.

On display are handmade objects and products by local artisans, to be discovered while strolling among the stalls. The initiative aims to showcase local craftsmanship and creativity, support local trade and turn one of the town's streets into a place to meet and walk throughout the day.
