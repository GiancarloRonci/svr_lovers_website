On Sunday 29 November 2026 the Mercatini dell'Artigianato (craft market) is back in Colleferro: from 9 am to 8 pm Viale XXV Aprile fills up with stalls selling handmade products. It is organised by the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro with the Lepinart association.

On the same day the Covered Market, a few minutes away, is open for Sapori d'Autunno. For information: Pro Loco Città di Colleferro, 331 461 6819.
