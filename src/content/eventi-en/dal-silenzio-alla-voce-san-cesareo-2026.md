On Wednesday 25 November 2026, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, San Cesareo holds "Dal Silenzio alla Voce" (From silence to voice), a march with reflections by students in memory of the victims of femicide. At 9 am a silent procession leaves the "Cesare Pavese" school building in Via Giordano Bruno and crosses the town to Piazza Giulio Cesare, where the pupils read out their thoughts and reflections.

The march, organised by the San Cesareo school institute with the Municipality and the Insieme Crescere association, is open to everyone. It is part of "Respect: mettiamo a fuoco il rispetto", the Municipality's programme against gender-based violence, which ends on Friday 27 November with the [film competition "Quanto Basta"](/en/eventi/quanto-basta-san-cesareo-2026).
