On Sunday 25 October 2026 San Vito Romano takes part in the tenth national "Camminata tra gli Olivi" (Walk Among the Olive Trees), promoted by the Associazione Nazionale Città dell'Olio and held on the same day all over Italy. The meeting point is at 10:00 AM in Via Guido Baccelli, in front of the post office.

The walk, about 2 km long and lasting a couple of hours, of medium difficulty, crosses the historic center up to Via Porta Olevano in the medieval village, with guided visits to churches, monuments and historic buildings led by Dr Irene Quaresima. At around 12:30 PM it reaches the La Rosciola farm for a visit to the olive mill, a meeting with farmers and technicians on caring for olive groves and the effects of their abandonment, and a guided tasting of extra virgin olive oil and local products. This is followed by a shared lunch with local products and then by workshops at the farm.

The walk is free and open to all; lunch is optional and costs €15 per person, with booking required on +39 328 155 1091 (Desirè, WhatsApp preferred) or at info@larosciola.it. It is organized by the Pro Loco and the Associazione Italiana Volontari Protezione Civile of San Vito Romano. Comfortable shoes and sportswear are recommended; in case of bad weather the event will be cancelled, and the route is not recommended for people with reduced mobility.
