On Saturday 24 October 2026, from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro, in Piazza 29 Marzo 1950 at Colleferro Scalo, hosts the new edition of "Armonie d'Autunno" ("Autumn Harmonies"), the choral music series organised by the Corale Polifonica G.B. Martini under the patronage of the Municipality. Three local choirs perform.

Admission is free.
