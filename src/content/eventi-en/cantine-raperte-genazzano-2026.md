From 11 to 13 September 2026, **"Cantine Raperte"** returns to Genazzano, the historic festival that for three days opens to the public the ancient cellars carved into the tuff of the town's historic centre. It's a chance to taste traditional dishes made with local products, paired with wines from the town's cellars, amid folk music, craft markets and street performances.

**Programme**

- **Friday 11 September** - 5:00 PM opening of craft markets and food stalls; 7:30 PM cellars open for dinner, with roaming marching bands (Ritmania, Titubanda); 10:00 PM folk concert by Cavesja in Piazza E. D'Amico
- **Saturday 12 September** - 5:00 PM markets and tastings reopen; 7:30 PM dinner in the cellars, with street bands Lestofunky and S-Banda-Ti; 10:00 PM folk performance by Scorribande Popolari
- **Sunday 13 September** - 12:00 PM lunch with tastings; 4:30 PM street performers and family entertainment; 7:00 PM closing dinner in the cellars

**Useful information**

Gluten-free menu options are available, along with a children's play area at Castello Colonna. A free shuttle service runs to the historic centre.
