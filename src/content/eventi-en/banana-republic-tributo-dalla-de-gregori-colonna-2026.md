On Saturday 10 October 2026, at 8:30 PM, the 64th Italia Grape Festival and 32nd Pincinelle Festival in Colonna hosts a concert by Banana Republic, a tribute band bringing back the songs of Lucio Dalla and Francesco De Gregori. The name recalls the two singer-songwriters' famous 1979 stadium tour and the live album of the same name.

The concert is the highlight of the festival's Saturday, which continues at 10:00 PM with a DJ set by Provenzano DJ. During the festival, the Food and Wine Tour is available at 25 euros, with pincinelle pasta and wine from local wineries.
