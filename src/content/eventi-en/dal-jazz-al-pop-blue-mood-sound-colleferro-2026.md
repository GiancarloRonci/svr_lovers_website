On Friday 23 October 2026, from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro hosts "Dal Jazz al Pop" (From Jazz to Pop), a concert by Blue Mood Sound. The band offers a musical journey that starts from blues and cool jazz and reaches modern pop, with live arrangements and well-known classics.

Admission is free and no tickets are required. The concert is organised by the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro: for information you can call 331 4616819.
