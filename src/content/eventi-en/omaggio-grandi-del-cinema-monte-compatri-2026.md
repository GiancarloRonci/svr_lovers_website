On Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026, at 18:00, the conference hall of Casa San Silvestro in Monte Compatri hosts "Omaggio ai Grandi del Cinema" ("A tribute to the greats of cinema"), two evenings recalling the legacy of Italian film-makers and actors linked to Rome and Lazio.

The first evening is dedicated to film historian and critic Mario Verdone, to Angelo Infanti, an actor from Zagarolo, and to Alberto Sordi; the second to Nino Manfredi, Ugo Tognazzi, Anna Magnani and director Lina Wertmüller. Admission is free. The event is promoted by the Comunità Montana Castelli Romani e Prenestini with the RomArtEventi cultural association and the support of the Lazio Region. The evenings are in Italian.
