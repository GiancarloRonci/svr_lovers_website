On Sunday 15 November 2026, at 9 am, Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park guide Stefania Calzini leads a hike from Cervara di Roma to La Prugna, an old stronghold destroyed in the 1600s. Along the way the route passes the Prataglia astronomical observatory and the deer wildlife area, where some of the animals can be seen.

Booking is required by Saturday 14 November, by WhatsApp message to +39 340 408 4321 with your first name, surname and Italian tax code: these are needed for the daily accident insurance. The Park's page does not give the exact meeting point, the length of the route or the fee, so it's best to ask when booking.

For information: +39 340 408 4321, stefaniacalzini@yahoo.it.
