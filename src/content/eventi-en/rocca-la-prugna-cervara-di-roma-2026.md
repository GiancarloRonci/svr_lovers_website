On Sunday 22 November 2026 environmental hiking guide Stefania Calzini leads a hike from Cervara di Roma, the "village of artists", to La Prugna, an ancient stronghold destroyed in the 17th century, in the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park. Along the way the route passes the Prataglia astronomical observatory and the red deer wildlife area, where with a bit of luck you can see some of the animals up close.

The route is about 12 km long, with 350 metres of climb and a walking time of 4-5 hours not counting stops. It is rated easy and suits adults and children from about 10 who are used to walking. Dogs are welcome. You will need ankle-high hiking boots, layered clothing, a waterproof jacket, a packed lunch and at least 1.5 litres of water.

The meeting point is in Cervara di Roma at 9:00 AM. The fee is 15 euros for adults and 10 euros for under-14s, including the guide and insurance. Booking is required by Saturday 21 November with a WhatsApp message to +39 340 408 4321, giving your full name and Italian tax code (needed for the daily accident insurance). The hike is led in Italian.
