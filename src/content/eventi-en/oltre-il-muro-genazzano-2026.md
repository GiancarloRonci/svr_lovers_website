On Sunday 25 October 2026, at 4:00 PM, the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano hosts a special screening of the documentary film "Oltre il muro - Madre del Buon Consiglio" (Beyond the Wall - Mother of Good Counsel), produced by Crossinmedia Production and Rai Documentari. It is a journey through centuries, traditions and testimonies around the fresco of Our Lady of Good Counsel, kept in the town's [Sanctuary](/en/luoghi/santuario-madonna-buon-consiglio-genazzano).

After the screening there is a meeting with the director, Gjon Kolndrekaj. Admission is free. The event is promoted by the Pro Loco of Genazzano together with the Basilica of Our Lady of Good Counsel and the town council.
