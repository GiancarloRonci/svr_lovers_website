On Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November 2026 the historic centre of Ciciliano hosts the Festa d'Autunno "Passeggia e Assaggia" (Stroll and Taste), organised by the Gruppo Storico Panarda with the patronage of the Municipality. The route starts from Piazza di Corte and winds through the streets of the village, with a market of crafts and local products, a ricotta and cheese-making workshop and live music.

On Saturday it opens at 10:00 AM: lunch is served from 12:00, Halloween for children starts at 3:00 PM and dinner from 6:00 PM. On Sunday lunch is from 12:00 and the festival closes at 6:00 PM. The menu includes polenta, legume soups, mosciarelle (dried chestnuts), roast chestnuts, fried pizza and new wine. Admission is free; parking and the shuttle are free and card payments are accepted at the stands.
