From 20 to 22 November 2026 Palazzo Rospigliosi in Zagarolo hosts the third edition of the Italia Media Art Festival (IMAF), with its Grand Exhibition, and, on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November, "On The Road", the third international conference on game design, with Italian and international figures from the video game world. The event is run by the Lazio Region and Lazio Innova, at the Spazio Attivo in Piazza Indipendenza.

The conference opens on Saturday at 10:30 am; on Sunday, from 10 am to 1 pm, there are "Meet&Greet" sessions between professionals and companies. Taking part is free, but you need to register online on the Lazio Innova portal by 1 pm on Friday 20 November: places are limited and individual sessions are subject to confirmation by the organisers.
