On Sunday 22 November 2026 the Pro Loco of Capranica Prenestina, together with the Sentieri da Vivere association and with the patronage of the Municipality, offers the "Escursione Foliage: Anello della Mentorella". The meeting point is at 9 am at the car park of the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Mentorella.

The loop crosses woods of oak, maple, hornbeam and chestnut in their autumn colours, reaches Monte Cerella and Monte Spina Santa and passes panoramic ridges and karst clearings. The group is led by AIGAE environmental hiking guide Marina Rullent. Bring hiking boots, a jacket or fleece, water and something to eat.

Taking part is free, but booking is required and there are at most 25 places: book with Marina on 349 622 6060, also on WhatsApp.
