On Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026, between Via Francesco Petrarca and Piazza Gobetti, Colleferro hosts the Festa del Peperoncino - Villaggio dei Sapori d'Italia, put on by the Tradizioni d'Italia association and included in the Pro Loco's calendar. It opens on Saturday at 9:00 AM and closes on Sunday at 9:00 PM.

On the programme: tastings and spicy specialities, talks by experts on the history and properties of chilli peppers, workshops and a spicy pasta contest with prizes. Alongside it is the Market of the Flavours of Italy, with food and wine products and crafts, and for families there are creative workshops for children, live music and entertainment. No tickets are sold.

For information: Colleferro Pro Loco, tel. +39 331 4616819, colleferro.proloco@gmail.com.
