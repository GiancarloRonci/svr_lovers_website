On Saturday 28 November 2026, at 7:15 pm, the Cathedral of Sant'Agapito in Palestrina hosts "L'Homme armé", a concert by the Cappella Musicale di Santa Maria in Via conducted by Maestro Luigi Ciuffa, part of the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina devoted to choral and sacred music.

The programme features the anonymous chanson "L'Homme armé" and, by Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, the Missa "L'Homme armé" for five voices, "Pueri Hebraeorum" for four voices and "Tu es Petrus" for six voices. The soloists are Maria Chiara Forte, Marilisa Gallori, Edoardo Boccoli, Paolo Perone and Alessandro Tenaglia. For information on admission, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
