On Saturday 28 November 2026 the Cathedral of Sant'Agapito in Palestrina hosts a concert by the Cappella Musicale di Santa Maria in Via, part of the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina devoted to choral and sacred music.

The start time has not been announced yet: for information on time and admission, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
