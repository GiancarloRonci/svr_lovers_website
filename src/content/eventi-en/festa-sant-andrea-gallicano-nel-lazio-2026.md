From Saturday 28 to Monday 30 November 2026 Gallicano nel Lazio celebrates its patron saint, Saint Andrew the Apostle. On Saturday the Rosary is at 5:30 pm, followed by Mass at 6 pm; on Sunday 29 Masses are at 9:30 and 11 am, the latter celebrated by Bishop Mauro Parmeggiani, and the Christmas market is open from 10 am to 1 pm.

On Monday 30, the feast day, the church bells ring out at 9 am; the Christmas market is open from 4 to 8 pm. The Rosary is at 5 pm, Mass at 5:30 pm and the procession with the parish band at 6:30 pm. The day ends at 7:15 pm, in the church, with the Saint Andrew Concert by the Banda Parrocchiale conducted by Nicola Chesti and the Cantabanda choir.
