On Saturday 3 October 2026 at 4:00 PM, the series "Tracciati. Luoghi, storie e culture attraversando il Museumgrandtour" stops in Cave with the talk "On the trail of the Rome-Fiuggi. Tracks, Liberty villas and communities", in the Multipurpose Hall of the Lorenzo Ferri Museum.

The speakers are Giuseppe Nicola de Grisantis and Letizia Mula. Organised by the Lorenzo Ferri Museum together with the Colonna Station Railway Museum, the event tells the story of the historic line that linked Rome to Fiuggi through the Prenestine villages, and the mark it left on local architecture and communities. Participation is free.
