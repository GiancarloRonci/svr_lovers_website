On Sunday 15 November 2026, at 6 pm, the Council Chamber of Colleferro Town Hall hosts "Metamorphoses: Folk songs and virtuoso transcriptions", a solo harp concert by Sofia Marzetti, blending folk songs and virtuoso transcriptions. The concert is part of the twelfth season of the "Incontriamoci" series.

The Pro Loco's page does not give the ticket price or how to book: it's best to check with the organisers beforehand.
