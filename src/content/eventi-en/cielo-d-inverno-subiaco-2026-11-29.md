On Sunday 29 November 2026 ASTRIS Astronomia brings "Storie e scienza del cielo d'inverno nei Monti Simbruini" ("Stories and science of the winter sky") to Subiaco. In the hall of the Monastery of Santa Scolastica, Luca Spuntoni talks about "C/2026 A2 (Bok), the Christmas comet"; weather permitting, there is also daytime solar observation and night-sky viewing. It is repeated on [Sunday 20 December](/en/eventi/cielo-d-inverno-subiaco-2026-12-20).

The Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar does not give the time or cost, so it is worth calling ahead. Information and booking: 347 480 9153, astris@astrisroma.org.
