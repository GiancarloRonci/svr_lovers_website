On Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September 2026, Guadagnolo - the highest non-mountain inhabited place in Lazio - hosts the eagerly awaited 2026 edition of the "Festa delle More" (Blackberry Festival), two days of authentic flavours, music, comedy and nature.

In this village of the Monti Prenestini, nature and the delights of the undergrowth take centre stage: food stalls will offer blackberry jams and biscuits, while the evenings come alive with the "Impressioni d'Autore" concert and the rousing comedy of the "Geppo Show".

The event, organised by the Pro Loco Guadagnolo with the support of UNPLI (National Union of Italian Pro Loco) and the "I Borghi più belli d'Italia" (Most Beautiful Villages of Italy) label, will bring the central Piazza Dante Alighieri to life, offering a perfect weekend to combine wild berry tastings, fresh air and evening shows. Matteo Pizzolorusso will host and conduct the event's interviews.
