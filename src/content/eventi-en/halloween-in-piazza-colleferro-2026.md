On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 4:30 PM, Piazza Italia in Colleferro hosts "Halloween in piazza" (Halloween in the Square), an afternoon of celebrations organised by the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro with the association Piazza Italia e dintorni.

For children there are games, face painting and spooky stories, with a "K-Pop Demon Hunters" theme; from 4:30 to 6:30 PM, in Piazza dei Cosmonauti, bouncy castles for the little ones (ages 3 to 5). Free doughnuts for everyone. For information you can contact the Pro Loco of Colleferro on 331 4616819.
