On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 5:00 PM, Piazza Italia in Colleferro hosts "Halloween in piazza" (Halloween in the Square), an event listed in the calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro.

The detailed programme of the afternoon has not been announced yet. For information you can contact the Pro Loco of Colleferro on 331 4616819.
