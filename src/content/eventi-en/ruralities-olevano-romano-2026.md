On Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December 2026 "Ruralities" returns to Olevano Romano for its second edition: two days devoted to olive oil, to the flavours and stories of the town and to Panpepato, a spiced honey-and-nut cake that is one of the most typical sweets of Christmas in Olevano. The project brings together several local organisations to promote traditions, local products and the people who keep them alive.

It takes place at SpaziOlevano, in the former Bonuglia olive mill: from the new oil to the "Panpepato d'Oro" (Golden Panpepato), with stories, tastings and other activities. The full programme of the two days has not been published yet: for times and details, follow the Facebook page of the Pro Loco of Olevano Romano.
