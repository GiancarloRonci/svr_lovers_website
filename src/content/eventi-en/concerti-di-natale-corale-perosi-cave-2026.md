On Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December 2026 the Corale Lorenzo Perosi choir gives its traditional Christmas concerts in Cave, on the last weekend before the holidays.

The concerts are in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given the venue, the times or the programme: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the choir.
