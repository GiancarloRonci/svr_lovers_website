On Sunday, September 27, 2026, from 8:30 am to 1:30 pm, the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park is organizing a guided e-bike tour starting from Monte Livata, through centuries-old beech forests, flow trails and panoramic views over the whole valley. Meeting point at the Park's Visitor Centre, for an outing suitable for both families and more experienced riders.

The tour takes a minimum of 4 and a maximum of 10 participants, at a cost of 40 euros per person; each participant must bring a backpack with water, snacks and a rain jacket. For information and registration, contact Danilo Camusi (Pachamama Adventure) at +39 328 4897969, or the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park.
