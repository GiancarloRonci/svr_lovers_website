At Teatro Narzio, Friday 16 October brings "Paolo Borsellino – The Guardian of Courage".

Paolo Borsellino knew well that time was closing in around him, that any day could be his last. Yet he kept working, kept speaking, kept searching for the truth. He was afraid, but he had also decided that fear would not choose in his place.

"Paolo Borsellino – The Guardian of Courage" does not aim to turn a man into a statue. It brings him back to his most authentic dimension: that of a magistrate, a father, a citizen aware of the danger and yet unable to look away.

On stage, Cristiano Arsì gives body and voice to this story with great intensity, because remembering Borsellino does not simply mean speaking his name, but asking what remains of his example once the commemorations end, once the lights go out, once courage becomes a personal choice again.

We will tell you about a man, not a monument. A man who knew fear and, precisely because of that, knew how to set its limits.
