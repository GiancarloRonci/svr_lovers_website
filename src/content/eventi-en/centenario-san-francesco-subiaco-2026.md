On Saturday 3 October 2026 at 4:00 PM, the Porta del Parco in Subiaco hosts an event dedicated to the centenary of Saint Francis's presence in Subiaco, organised by the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park.

The initiative recalls the bond between the saint of Assisi and the town of Saint Benedict, where, according to tradition, Francis stayed on pilgrimage to the Sacro Speco. The event falls on the eve of the feast of Saint Francis, on 4 October.
