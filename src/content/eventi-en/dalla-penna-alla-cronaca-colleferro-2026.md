On Saturday 7 November 2026, at 5:00 PM, the Biblioteca Comunale "Riccardo Morandi" in Colleferro opens "Dalla penna alla cronaca: dove finiscono i silenzi" ("From the pen to the news: where silences end"), the November series promoted by the Municipality of Colleferro and devoted to violence against women.

The guest of the first meeting is Roberta Cassetti, psychologist and psychotherapist, author of "Sotto pelle. Il dolore invisibile delle relazioni tossiche", in conversation with Diana Stanzani, deputy mayor and councillor for Culture. The meeting includes music by the Neos Kronos association, with Giuseppina Mazzei on piano and Giada Zaccari on guitar.

The series includes two more meetings during the month, whose dates and guests have not been announced yet.
