On Friday 20 November 2026, at 4:30 pm, UNITRE – the University of the Third Age of Palestrina – presents "Cartoline d'amore – La corrispondenza amorosa dei nostri nonni" (Love postcards – Our grandparents' love letters), a talk by Angelo Pinci: a journey into the feelings of the past through the letters and postcards our grandparents exchanged.

It takes place in the ITOP conference hall, at Via Prenestina Nuova 307 in Palestrina. Cost and how to attend are not stated: for information, contact UNITRE Palestrina.
