On Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November 2026 Canterano celebrates autumn with the festival organised by the Pro Loco in the lanes of the village. On Saturday lunch and dinner are served, starting from 12:00 noon; on Sunday lunch, also from 12:00 noon.

The menu includes chestnut bread made by the Pro Loco with local salami and cheeses, pasta and beans with chopped chestnuts, a stuffed pocket with meatballs in sauce, new this year, and Abruzzo-style arrosticini skewers. To finish there are Mont Blanc and zuppa inglese, with roast chestnuts and mulled wine.

On Saturday there is also Halloween entertainment for children, with live music from Jimmy Pelli and Red Sax 77.
