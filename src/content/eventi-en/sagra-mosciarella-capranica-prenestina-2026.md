On Sunday 6 December 2026 Capranica Prenestina hosts the 45th Sagra della Mosciarella, devoted to the dried chestnut that is the village's signature product. It is a day of traditional flavours, folklore and discovering the area, set in the cellars of the historic centre.

Times, menu and programme have not been published yet, and for now the news comes from the local press: for updates, follow the channels of the Municipality of Capranica Prenestina and of the Pro Loco.
