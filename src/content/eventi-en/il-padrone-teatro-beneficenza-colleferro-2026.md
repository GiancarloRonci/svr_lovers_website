On Saturday 17 October 2026, from 9:00 PM, the Teatro Comunale Vittorio Veneto in Colleferro hosts "Il Padrone" (The Boss), a play based on a text by Gianni Clementi and staged by the Compagnia Teatro Nuovo, directed by Luciano Fontana. On stage are Fontana himself, Gaetana Carta and Mario Girardi, with Luigi Capozi and Caterina Vincenzi; the evening is supported by FITA.

It is a charity evening: all the proceeds support the inclusive projects and workshops of Anffas Monti Lepini e Valle del Sacco, for young people and families in the area. Seats are few, so booking is recommended on 328 075 4622, also via WhatsApp.
