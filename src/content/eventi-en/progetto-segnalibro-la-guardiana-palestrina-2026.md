On Sunday 8 November 2026, at 5:30 PM, the Biblioteca Comunale Fantoniana in Palestrina welcomes back Progetto Segnalibro ("Bookmark Project"), the series of meetings with authors run by the association Il Principe APS, now in its second edition.

The guest of the first meeting is Caterina Battilocchio, who presents "La guardiana" (Garzanti), her debut novel, in conversation with Elisa Saioni. Entry is free and no booking is needed, and refreshments will be served at the end.
