On Tuesday 8 December 2026 the twenty-second edition of the life-size art nativity scene, curated by the town's Pro Loco, opens in Castel San Pietro Romano. On Saturday 2 and Sunday 3 January 2027 the sixth edition of the living nativity is also planned.

Opening days and times of the nativity scene have not been published yet: the Pro Loco will announce them on its social media, which are worth checking before setting off.
