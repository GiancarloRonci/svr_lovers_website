On Tuesday 8 December 2026, at 6 pm, the twenty-second edition of the life-size art nativity scene, curated by the town's Pro Loco together with the Municipality, opens in Castel San Pietro Romano. It can be visited until 6 January 2027, every day from 10 am to 7 pm, with free entry.

On Saturday 2 and Sunday 3 January 2027 the sixth edition of the living nativity, "Il Presepe prende vita" (The Nativity comes to life), is also planned, organised by the cultural association L'Allegra Brigata.
