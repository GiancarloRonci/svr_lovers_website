On Sunday 20 December 2026, at 5:30 PM, the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano rounds off its pre-Christmas programme with "Il Referendum di Natale" ("The Christmas Referendum"), the comedy with Marco Bandiera and Carmine Paraggio. It is one of the shows of the new 2026/27 theatre season, which is devoted mainly to comedy.

The season is organised by "Insieme per il Teatro" with the Pro Loco and the Municipality of Genazzano. For information and bookings: 392 945 9132 (Mara), 339 656 1813 (Francesca).
