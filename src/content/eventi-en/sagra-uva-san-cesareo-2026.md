From 25 to 27 September 2026, San Cesareo hosts the 56th edition of the Grape and Local Products Festival ("Sagra dell'Uva"), a long-running event organised by the local Pro Loco in the town centre.

Three days dedicated to the flavours and traditions of the area, with grapes as the star of the show, food stalls, local products and festive moments celebrating country culture, wine and conviviality.

*Source: [Comune di San Cesareo](https://www.comune.sancesareo.rm.it/novita/comunicati_stampa/novita_2829.html)*
