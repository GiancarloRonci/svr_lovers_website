On Saturday 12, Sunday 13, Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December 2026 the Auditorium Pierluigi in Palestrina hosts "Rapunzel – Il Musical", the big family production by the cultural association "Arte Madre", back on stage by popular demand.

Music, sets, costumes, choreography and fairy-tale characters bring to life a show designed to move audiences of every age. It is the third title in the Auditorium's season preview, after "La guerra di Martin" and "A Midsummer Night's Dream".

The show runs only on the two weekends listed. Performance times, ticket prices and booking details have not been announced yet.
