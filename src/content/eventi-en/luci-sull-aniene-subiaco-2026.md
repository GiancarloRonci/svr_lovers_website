On Friday 11 December 2026 the Collegio del Palio association presents "Luci sull'Aniene" ("Lights on the Aniene. Christmas lights up Subiaco's roots") in Subiaco, an event about the bond between the San Francesco bridge, the river Aniene and the town's traditions.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information and booking: 338 873 7964, info@paliosanlorenzo.it.
