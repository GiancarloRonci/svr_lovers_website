On Saturday 31 October 2026 Olevano Romano celebrates Halloween with two events. From 5:00 PM, in Piazza Laudenzi, the Circus Karakasciò puts on a show for all ages, with juggling, soap bubbles and magic.

From 10:00 PM the party moves indoors to Salti di Gioia, on Via Maremmana Superiore, for the Halloween Party with DJ sets by CØRI-G, X-UBERANT and AYKO. The town council offers a 500-euro prize for the best costume. The first 100 tickets bought online cost 10 euros, entry and a drink included; after that, entry at the door costs 15 euros.
