On Saturday 9 and Sunday 10 January 2027 Cosmofauna APS holds "Sulle tracce dei grandi mammiferi" ("On the trail of large mammals - from knowledge to ethical photography") in Jenne. The subject is the large animals of the Simbruini Mountains Park: the bear, which is coming back, the wolf and the deer. Where they live, how they behave, and how to photograph them without disturbing them.

The Park calendar does not yet give times, programme or cost: ask the organiser. Information: 331 857 5562, cosmofaunaaps@gmail.com.
