On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 7:30 PM, the Parco Comunale (municipal park) of Capranica Prenestina hosts the Halloween Party.

The event closes the October calendar in the Monti Prenestini put together by Visit Monti Prenestini. The detailed programme of the evening has not been announced yet.
