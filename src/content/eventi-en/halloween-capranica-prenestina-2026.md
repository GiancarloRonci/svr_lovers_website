On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 7:30 PM, the Parco Comunale (municipal park) of Capranica Prenestina hosts the Halloween Party: an evening with dinner, music, cocktails and costumes.

The event closes the October calendar in the Monti Prenestini put together by Visit Monti Prenestini. Closing time, prices and booking details have not been announced yet.
