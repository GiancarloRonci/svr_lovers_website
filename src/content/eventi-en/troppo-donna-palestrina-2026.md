On Thursday 19 and Friday 20 November 2026, from 6:00 to 8:00 PM, the Albergo Ristorante Stella in Palestrina, at Piazza della Liberazione 3, hosts "Troppo Donna – Ritrova te stessa" ("Too Much Woman – Find Yourself Again"), two sessions of the "Tu Donna" project devoted to women's self-awareness, personal growth and the prevention of violence against women, in the week leading up to 25 November.

On Thursday counsellor Martina Riccio leads a journey through psychology, art and female archetypes, to recognise the habits that lead women to sacrifice too much and always put others first. On Friday mental coach Monia Cupellini works on the "logical levels of change", a coaching tool to see how far one's life matches the woman one wants to become.

Attendance is free and booking is recommended: 379 101 4912, attraversonuoviocchi@gmail.com. The sessions are organised by the Circolo Culturale Prenestino Roberto Simeoni APS and the Associazione Attraverso Nuovi Occhi, under the patronage of the Lazio Region and the Municipality of Palestrina. They are held in Italian.
