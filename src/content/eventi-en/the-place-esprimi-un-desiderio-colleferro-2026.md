On Saturday 17 October 2026, at 9:00 PM, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro, in Piazza Occupazione di Fabbrica, hosts the play "The place - esprimi un desiderio" (The place - make a wish). The show is part of the events calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro and lasts about two hours.

The company, ticket prices and booking details have not been announced yet. For information you can contact the Pro Loco of Colleferro on 331 4616819.
