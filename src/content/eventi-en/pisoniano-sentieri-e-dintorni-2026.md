On Sunday 18 October 2026, from 10 AM, the Comune di Pisoniano and Officina dei Mestieri ETS invite residents and visitors to "Pisoniano… Sentieri e Dintorni – Percorsi del Gusto, della Natura e dello Sport" (Trails and Surroundings – routes of food, nature and sport), a free event funded by ARSIAL and the Lazio Region to promote local food and wine and to showcase the area.

The day takes place in the village and offers several experiences devoted to discovering the inland areas, with a focus on outdoor life and healthy eating, including a visit to the Civic Hemp Museum (Museo Civico della Canapa). There is also a market of local farms, where visitors can taste and buy local food and wine.

Participation is free, but booking is required by email at officinadeimestieriaps@gmail.com.

*Source: Comune di Pisoniano*
