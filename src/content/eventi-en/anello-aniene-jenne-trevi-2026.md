On Saturday 21 November 2026, from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM, environmental hiking guide Samuele Giancarlini (Escursioni del Sam) leads "Tra pietra e acqua: l'anello dell'Aniene da Jenne a Trevi" ("Between stone and water: the Aniene loop from Jenne to Trevi"), a loop hike of about 14 km in the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park.

From the village of Jenne the route drops down to the bottom of the Aniene gorge, passes the historic Mola Vecchia mill, follows the river past the outer walls of the Inferniglio Caves and reaches the Trevi waterfall, where there is a stop. Along the way the guide talks about the plants, wildlife and geology of the area and the life of the wild animals of these woods, from the wolf to the bear.

The fee is 20 euros per person and covers the guide only. The hike goes ahead once a minimum number of participants is reached: those who sign up receive confirmation and the exact meeting point. For information and bookings: 334 830 2603, infoescursionidelsam@gmail.it. The hike is led in Italian.
