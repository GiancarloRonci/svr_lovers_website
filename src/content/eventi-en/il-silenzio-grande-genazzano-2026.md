On Sunday 6 December 2026, at 5:30 PM, the TrePerCaso theatre company brings "Il silenzio grande" ("The Great Silence"), the play by Maurizio de Giovanni, to the stage of the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano. It is one of the shows of the new 2026/27 theatre season, which alongside comedy also makes room for drama.

The season is organised by "Insieme per il Teatro" with the Pro Loco and the Municipality of Genazzano. For information and bookings: 392 945 9132 (Mara), 339 656 1813 (Francesca).
