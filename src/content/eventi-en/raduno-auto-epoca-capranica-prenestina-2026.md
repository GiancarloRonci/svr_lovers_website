On Sunday 4 October 2026 the vintage car rally of the "Amici dei Motori d'Epoca" club (affiliated with ACI Storico) returns to Capranica Prenestina for its fifth edition, organised in cooperation with the municipality.

The classic cars arrive in Piazza Pietro Baccelli around 11:30 AM for the display, followed at 1:00 PM by a typical lunch at the "Da Gaetano" restaurant. Anyone is welcome to join with their own vintage car and get to know the club.

*Source: [Capranica Prenestina e Guadagnolo Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/561160834062544/posts/3362565327255400)*
