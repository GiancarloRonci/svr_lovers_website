On Tuesday 8 December 2026, the Feast of the Immaculate Conception, Subiaco hosts "Sinfonie d'inverno nel cuore dei Simbruini" ("Winter symphonies in the heart of the Simbruini"): an event called "L'Eco del Cervo" ("The echo of the deer") that brings together cinema, music and Christmas tradition.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information and booking: Gioacchino Galiena, 339 228 7008, gioacchino.galiena@alice.it.
