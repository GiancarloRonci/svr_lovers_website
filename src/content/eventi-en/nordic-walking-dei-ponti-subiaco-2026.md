On Saturday 24 October 2026, from 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM, Subiaco hosts the "Nordic Walking dei Ponti", a walk with poles that starts from the Bridge of San Francesco, on via della Pila, and reaches the Minnone Bridge.

The initiative is part of the Pink October programme and raises funds for Komen Italia, which works on the prevention and treatment of breast cancer. Booking is required by Friday 23 October: +39 328 453 8950, ss-antares@tiscali.it.
