On Saturday 24 October 2026 Agosta, in the Aniene Valley, celebrates its 19th Festa dell'Autunno. From 5:00 PM the food stands serve roasted chestnuts, new wine and the village speciality, gnocchi with mutton sauce; craft stalls and music round off the evening. Covered seating is available.

The festival is traditionally organised by the Agosta Pro Loco. This information comes from online news outlets and event calendars and not yet from an official source: before setting off, check the channels of the Pro Loco or the Municipality of Agosta.
