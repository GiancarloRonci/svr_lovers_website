On Saturday 5 December 2026, from 5 pm to 7 pm, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro hosts a Christmas concert by Decadanza, a cover band of the Italian group Nomadi. Proceeds go to Anffas Monti Lepini e Valle del Sacco, an association of families of people with disabilities.

Entry is free, with a voluntary donation. For information: Pro Loco Città di Colleferro, 331 461 6819.
