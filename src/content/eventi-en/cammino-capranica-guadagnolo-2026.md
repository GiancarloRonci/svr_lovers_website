On Sunday 18 October 2026 there is a walk in the Monti Prenestini with the promotional hike "In cammino da Capranica Prenestina a Monte Guadagnolo", organised by the Associazione per il Cammino Naturale dei Parchi for the trail's tenth anniversary. The meeting point is at 9:00 AM in Piazza Pietro Baccelli, Capranica Prenestina, from where the route climbs towards Guadagnolo.

The route is about 8 km long, for three and a half hours of walking, with 480 metres of ascent and 200 of descent: a ridge hike through pastures and woods up to Monte Guadagnolo (1,218 metres), in a Special Protection Area. A guided visit to the Museo Civico Naturalistico dei Monti Prenestini is also included.

Participation is free, but booking is required by Thursday 15 October on the [Trekking Italia](https://www.trekkingitalia.org/trek/240298) website. In the afternoon, at 4:30 PM, the Museum hosts the talk ["Tracciati": the roads of staying](/en/eventi/tracciati-vie-della-restanza-capranica-prenestina-2026).
