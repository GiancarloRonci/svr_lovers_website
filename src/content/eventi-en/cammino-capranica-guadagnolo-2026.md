On Sunday 18 October 2026 there is a walk in the Monti Prenestini with the promotional hike "In cammino da Capranica Prenestina a Monte Guadagnolo". The meeting point is at 9:00 AM in Piazza Baccelli, Capranica Prenestina, from where the route climbs towards Guadagnolo.

The outing is part of the October programme in the Monti Prenestini. Difficulty, duration and how to sign up have not been announced yet. In the afternoon, at 4:30 PM, the Museo Civico Naturalistico dei Monti Prenestini hosts the talk ["Tracciati": the roads of staying](/en/eventi/tracciati-vie-della-restanza-capranica-prenestina-2026).
