From 2 to 4 October 2026, Zagarolo hosts the 82nd edition of the Grape Festival ("Sagra dell'Uva"), one of the area's most beloved autumn events, awarded "Sagra di qualità" (Quality Festival) status.

The historic centre, from Piazza della Fontana Nuova to Viale Ungheria, comes alive with scattered taverns and food stands, wine and local product tastings, live music, a historical parade and allegorical floats themed around the grape harvest.

The full map and programme for the three days were still being published at the time of writing: check the event's official channels for updates before visiting.
