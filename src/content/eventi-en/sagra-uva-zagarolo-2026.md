From 2 to 4 October 2026, Zagarolo hosts the 82nd edition of the Grape Festival ("Sagra dell'Uva"), one of the area's most beloved autumn events, awarded "Sagra di qualità" (Quality Festival) status.

The historic centre, from Piazza della Fontana Nuova to Viale Ungheria, comes alive with scattered taverns and food stands, wine and local product tastings, live music, a historical parade and allegorical floats themed around the grape harvest.

Sixteen taverns are set up along the route, each with its own menu. Here is the programme for the three days:

- **Friday 2 October:** children's entertainment with Filobus at 5pm between Via Fabrini and Piazzetta Agostini, official opening at 6pm in Piazza Indipendenza and, at 10pm in the same square, a free DJ set by Dino Brown.
- **Saturday 3 October:** the dialect theatre show "La rapina… da lu direttore" at 5:30pm in Piazza Tre Cannelle and, at 9:30pm, a **free concert by Zero Assoluto** in Piazza Santa Maria.
- **Sunday 4 October:** the exhibition "Storie e giochi tra uva e vendemmia" (Stories and games of grapes and harvest) at the Toy Museum at 11am, and another dialect theatre show at 5:30pm in Piazza Tre Cannelle.

On Saturday (7pm to 1am) and Sunday (3pm to 11pm) a shuttle bus runs every 30 minutes between the railway station and the centre.
