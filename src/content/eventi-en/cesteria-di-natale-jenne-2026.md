On Sunday 27 December 2026 Jenneland Natura, Sport e Tradizione offers a basket-weaving workshop in Jenne: participants weave Christmas baskets from rattan, and the day continues with lunch and a tasting of the town's local products.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 349 229 5518, info@jenneland.com.
