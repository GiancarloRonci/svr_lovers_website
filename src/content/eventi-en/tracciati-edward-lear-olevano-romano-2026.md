On Saturday 17 October 2026 at 4:30 PM, the series "Tracciati. Luoghi, storie e culture attraversando il Museumgrandtour" stops in Olevano Romano with the talk "Travelling the Grand Tour routes: the work of Edward Lear", at SpaziOlevano.

The speaker is Francesca Tuscano. Organised by the "Domenico Riccardi" Civic Art Museum, the event is dedicated to the 19th-century English writer and painter and his travels along the Grand Tour routes, which also brought many artists to Olevano and the Monti Prenestini. Participation is free.
