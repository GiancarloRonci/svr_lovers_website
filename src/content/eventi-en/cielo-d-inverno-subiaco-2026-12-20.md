On Sunday 20 December 2026 the ASTRIS Astronomia association brings "Storie e scienza del cielo d'inverno nei Monti Simbruini" ("Stories and science of the winter sky") to Subiaco: Luca Spuntoni's talk "C/2026 A2 (Bok), the Christmas comet" and, weather permitting, daytime solar observation and night-sky viewing. It is repeated on Tuesday 29 December.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 347 480 9153, astris@astrisroma.org.
