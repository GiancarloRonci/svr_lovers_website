On Sunday 1 November 2026, at 6:00 PM, Basket Palestrina 1962 plays at home against Velletri in the regional league. With this game the Palestrina team returns to the PalaIaia in front of its home crowd, and for the occasion the players wear a special shirt.

Those who cannot make it to the sports hall can watch the game live on Basketincontro.
