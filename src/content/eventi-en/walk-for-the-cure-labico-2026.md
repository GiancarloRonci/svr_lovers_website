On Sunday 4 October 2026 Labico hosts the "Walk for the Cure", a non-competitive walk promoted by Komen Italia with the support of the town council, raising funds for breast cancer research, prevention and early diagnosis.

The walk starts at 9:30 AM from the Piazzale Comunale in via G. Matteotti. Participation requires a minimum donation of 10 euros, which includes the event's official T-shirt. Registration is open at Ginnastica Labico on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays from 5:00 to 8:00 PM, or by calling Enrica on 339 275 2548.
