On Saturday 10 October 2026 at 11:00 AM, the series "Tracciati. Luoghi, storie e culture attraversando il Museumgrandtour" comes to Valmontone with the study meeting "Along the Via Latina", at the Palazzo Doria Pamphilj Museum.

The speakers are Angelo Luttazi, Massimiliano Valenti and Elena De Sanctis. The event is jointly promoted by the museums of Artena, Colleferro, Valmontone and Zagarolo, and traces the history of the ancient road that crossed the Sacco valley. Participation is free.
