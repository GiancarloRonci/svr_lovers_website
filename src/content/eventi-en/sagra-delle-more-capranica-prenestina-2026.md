On 27 September 2026, starting at 11:00 AM, Piazza Pietro Baccelli in Capranica Prenestina hosts the tenth edition of the Blackberry Festival ("Sagra delle More"), with food stands devoted to the wild berries typical of the Monti Prenestini, live music and the traditional tombola.
