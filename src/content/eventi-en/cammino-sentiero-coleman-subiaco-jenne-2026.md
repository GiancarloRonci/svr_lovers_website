On Sunday 18 October 2026 the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park offers a walk towards the Coleman Trail, as part of the "Custodire e raccontare il territorio" ("Protecting and telling the story of the land") initiative, dedicated to the biodiversity, traditions and flavours of the Park's villages.

The meeting point is at 8:00 AM at the Ruins of Nero's Villa in Subiaco. After the hike, a tasting of local products is planned at 1:00 PM in Jenne; the return is at around 3:30 PM.

Participation is free. For information: +39 349 2295518.
