On Monday 7 September 2026, at 6:00 PM, Pope Leo XIV returns to Genazzano to celebrate Holy Mass at the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Good Counsel, on the eve of the feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary.

This will be his second visit to the sanctuary, after the one on 10 May 2025. The event is particularly significant since the Augustinians are the custodians of the Sanctuary of Genazzano, one of the most venerated Marian shrines in Lazio.

Local authorities, the Vatican Gendarmerie and the Police Forces have arranged a welcome and security plan. Access will be granted to residents and pilgrims; logistical details and traffic information will be announced in the days beforehand.
