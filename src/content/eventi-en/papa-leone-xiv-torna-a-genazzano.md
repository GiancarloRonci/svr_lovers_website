On Monday 7 September 2026, Pope Leo XIV returns to Genazzano to celebrate Holy Mass at the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Good Counsel, on the eve of the feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary. This will be his second visit to the sanctuary, after the one on 10 May 2025: the Holy Father returns to a place deeply tied to his Marian devotion.

**Schedule of the day**

- **4:30 PM** - Arrival in Piazzale della Pace and transfer to the Sanctuary in the popemobile, blessing the faithful along the way
- **5:00 PM** - Official welcome at the Sanctuary by the city authorities, the Augustinian Community and Bishop Mauro Parmeggiani
- **5:30 PM** - Unveiling of the painting depicting "Pope Leo XIV in prayer before the image of the Madonna"
- **6:00 PM** - Celebration of Holy Mass for the vigil of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary
- **7:30 PM** - Conclusion, with a popemobile procession through the historic centre and Piazza della Repubblica

**How to attend**

Entry to the Basilica requires a pass from 4:00 PM. Faithful without a pass will be able to follow the Mass on a large screen set up in Piazza della Repubblica. Other areas indicated for greeting the Pope are Piazzale Clementi, Piazza San Paolo and Piazzale Matteotti.

The event is particularly significant since the Augustinians are the custodians of the Sanctuary of Genazzano, one of the most venerated Marian shrines in Lazio.
