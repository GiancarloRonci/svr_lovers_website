On Sunday 11 October 2026, from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Castel San Pietro Romano goes back in time with the sixth edition of "Pranzo nel Borgo" (Lunch in the Village), organised by the town's Museo Diffuso. The day opens at 11:00 AM with a medieval reenactment at the Rocca dei Colonna, free for lunch ticket holders; at 1:00 PM there is a traditional lunch with local specialities and, at 4:00 PM in Piazza San Pietro, the grape harvest and old crafts, with handmade pasta and dessert workshops. Live music accompanies the whole day.

The lunch ticket costs 22 euros and can only be bought in person, from Saturday 10 October, at the Mu.Di. info point in Piazza San Pietro (9:00 AM-1:00 PM and from 3:00 PM until sold out): there is no online booking. The ticket also gives reduced museum admission and a 10% discount at the bookshop. A free shuttle runs from 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM and from 3:00 to 8:00 PM.
