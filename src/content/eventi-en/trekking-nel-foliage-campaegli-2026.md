On Saturday 17 October 2026 Campaegli Experience organises the "Trekking nel foliage", a guided walk on the trails of the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park, at the time of year when the beech woods of the plateau light up with the colours of autumn.

The meeting point is at 10:00 AM at the Resort Campaegli. Route, duration, difficulty, cost and booking arrangements are not given on the event page: for all details, contact the organiser on +39 347 6459 404.
