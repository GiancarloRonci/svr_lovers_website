On 26 September 2026, San Cesareo and Zagarolo host the next appointment of the "Itinerari del Museumgrandtour", the guided-tour series of the Territorial Museum System of the Roman and Prenestine Castles, with the itinerary "Along the routes of travel: railways, ancient roads and paths".

The morning is devoted to visiting the Railway Museum at Colonna Station, which tells the story of 110 years of local railway history; in the afternoon, the itinerary continues with a guided walk through the historic alleys of Zagarolo, along the town's ancient communication routes.

Participation is free with limited spots: booking is recommended by writing to info@museumgrandtour.org.
