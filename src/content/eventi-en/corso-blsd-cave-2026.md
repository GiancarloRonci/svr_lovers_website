On Saturday 10 October 2026, at 2:00 PM, the Council Chamber of Cave's Town Hall hosts a BLSD course open to the public: participants learn cardiopulmonary resuscitation and how to use a semi-automatic defibrillator (AED). The course is organised by the Comitato Monti Prenestini, in collaboration with the Municipality of Cave.

The course is open to everyone and requires no previous training. To sign up, fill in the online form linked in the NumeroZero article: the organisers then contact those who registered to confirm their place. For information: +39 344 083 8037, +39 06 953 5021.
