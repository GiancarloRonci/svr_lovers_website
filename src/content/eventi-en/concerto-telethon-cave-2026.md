On Sunday 13 December 2026 the Rete del Cuore and the Banda Città di Cave, the town band, present the "Concerto Cave per Telethon", an evening of music in support of research into rare genetic diseases.

The concert is in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given the venue, the time or how donations will be collected: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the band.

*Source: [Pro Loco Città di Cave – Events 2026](https://www.prolococaveaps.it/eventi-2026/)*
