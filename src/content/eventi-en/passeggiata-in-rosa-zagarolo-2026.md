On Sunday 20 September 2026, Zagarolo turns pink for the "Passeggiata in Rosa", a non-competitive walk held as part of Susan G. Komen Italia's Walk for the Cure in support of breast cancer prevention.

Participants meet at 9:00 am at the Villa Pierina car park, with the walk starting at 9:30 am. Registration is open until 19 September at the Pro Loco di Zagarolo, in Piazza Indipendenza, with a minimum donation of 10 euros entitling participants to the event's official t-shirt.

*Source: [Comune di Zagarolo](https://comune.zagarolo.rm.it/vivere-il-comune/eventi/passeggiata-in-rosa-a-zagarolo-walk-for-the-cure-domenica-22-settembre-dalle-ore-9-30/)*
