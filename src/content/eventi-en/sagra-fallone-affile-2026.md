On Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026 Affile celebrates the 40th Sagra del Fallone and Palio delle Contrade, organised by the Pro Loco and the Municipality in Piazza San Sebastiano. The star is the fallone, an oven-baked focaccia stuffed with cheese, ham or vegetables. Admission is free.

On Saturday at 4:00 PM the historical parade "Qui Effide dicitur", with flag-wavers, makes its way from the old town down to Piazza San Sebastiano; from 6:00 PM falloni and sandwiches are on sale. At 8:00 PM there is the dinner "A cena con Ildemondo", with fallone stuffed with vegetables and black pork sausage, cannelloni with sheep's ricotta and mushrooms, and desserts (€25 adults, €15 children; booking was requested by 20 October), followed at 9:00 PM by music from the Brigallè.

Sunday starts at 7:00 AM with an Avis blood drive; at 12:00 PM Avis donates a defibrillator to the community and at 1:00 PM there is lunch with polenta and pork ribs, with music by Claudio Frosoni and Paolino (€15 adults, €10 children and Avis donors; limited seats, booking recommended). At 4:00 PM the Contest of the Contrade closes the Palio. Updates on the Facebook page "Affile Feste, Sagre, Eventi".
