On Sunday 29 November 2026 the TrePerCaso theatre company brings "Il silenzio grande" ("The Great Silence"), the play by Maurizio de Giovanni, to the stage of the Teatro Comunale "Milco Paravani" in Cave. The same company performs it again on Sunday 6 December at the [Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano](/en/eventi/il-silenzio-grande-genazzano-2026).

The show is in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given the time or ticket prices: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the theatre. The play is in Italian.
