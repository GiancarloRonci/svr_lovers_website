With "Non chiamatelo amore" (Don't call it love) Valmontone devotes several days to raising awareness against violence against women, ahead of the International Day on 25 November. The events are run by the Municipality, with the Metropolitan City of Rome, the Valmontone branch of the Red Cross, the ASL Roma 5 health authority and the secondary school in via Gramsci.

On Saturday 21 November 2026, at 5 pm, the photo exhibition "Mai più silenzio" (No more silence) by Vanessa Renzi opens in the Stanza dell'Aria of Palazzo Doria Pamphilj; at 6 pm, in the palace auditorium, the association "Il Giardino delle Parole" presents the theatre reading "Quello che resta. Per Giulia, per tutte" (What remains. For Giulia, for all women). On Wednesday 25 November, at 5 pm, again in the Stanza dell'Aria, the exhibition "Les Tableaux Vivants" by the students of the via Gramsci school opens.

Cost is not stated: contact the Municipality of Valmontone for information.
