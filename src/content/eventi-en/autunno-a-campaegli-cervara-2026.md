On Sunday 25 October 2026 the hiking guide Stefania Calzini leads an autumn hike in the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park. The meeting point is at 9:00 AM in Cervara di Roma and the return is expected at around 4:00 PM, with 5-6 hours of walking not counting stops and the lunch break.

The route is a loop of about 12 km with 400 metres of elevation gain: it climbs through the woods, crosses small valleys and meadows, stops on the Campaegli plain and returns to Cervara along the Sentiero Italia, with views over the Aniene Valley. The difficulty is easy and the hike is also suitable for young people aged 10 and over who are used to walking; the pace is slow and the group waits for everyone. Dogs are welcome.

The fee is 15 euros for adults and 10 euros for under-14s, and includes the guide and insurance. Booking is required by Saturday 24 October, via WhatsApp at +39 340 408 4321, giving your first name, surname and Italian tax code. Bring layered clothing, ankle-high hiking boots, a packed lunch, at least a litre and a half of water and a waterproof jacket.
