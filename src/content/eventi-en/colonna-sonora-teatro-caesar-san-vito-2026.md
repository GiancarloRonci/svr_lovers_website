On Sunday 15 November 2026, at 5 pm, the Teatro Caesar in San Vito Romano hosts "Colonna Sonora – La storia in musica della famiglia Colonna" (the history of the Colonna family in music), a concert-show retracing the events and the leading figures of the Colonna family on a journey from the Middle Ages through the Renaissance to the modern era. The historical account of the places is woven together with a psychoanalytic reading, linking the memory of our past to self-awareness.

It is a work of original music: 26 songs joined by a single thread, blending singer-songwriter music, classical and traditional sounds, performed live with voices, guitars, piano, flute and percussion. It is also a look at our places and our roots, at the towns that still keep the memory of those origins, their buildings and their customs.

Admission is a single ticket of 10 euros. Booking at +39 380 180 5894.
