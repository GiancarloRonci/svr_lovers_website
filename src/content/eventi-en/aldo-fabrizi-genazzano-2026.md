On Sunday 29 November 2026, at 5:30 PM, the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano hosts "Aldo Fabrizi", the show with Fabrizio Gaetani dedicated to the great Roman actor, directed by Fabrizio Giannini. It is one of the shows of the new 2026/27 theatre season, which is devoted mainly to comedy.

The season is organised by "Insieme per il Teatro" with the Pro Loco and the Municipality of Genazzano. For information and bookings: 392 945 9132 (Mara), 339 656 1813 (Francesca).
