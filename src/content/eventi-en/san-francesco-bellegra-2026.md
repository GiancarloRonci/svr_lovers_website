From 29 September to 4 October 2026, Bellegra celebrates the feast of Saint Francis in the San Francesco area, with the lighting of the "Lamp of Fraternal Peace".

The Pro Loco's calendar does not give further details on the schedule for individual days: for up-to-date information, contact the Bellegra Pro Loco (tel. 334 5719720, email prolocobellegra@tiscali.it).
