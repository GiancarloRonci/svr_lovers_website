On Sunday 22 November 2026, at 5 pm, the exhibition "Oltre il silenzio – Contro la violenza sulle donne" (Beyond silence – Against violence against women) opens in Colleferro, in the exhibition space of the former BPD headquarters, at Corso Garibaldi 22. It stays open until 28 November and is part of the Municipality's events for the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on 25 November.

Opening hours and cost are not stated: contact the Municipality of Colleferro for information.
