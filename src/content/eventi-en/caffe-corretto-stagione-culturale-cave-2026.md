On Sunday 8 November 2026 the cultural season of Caffè Corretto starts again in Cave. The association organises meetings with authors, reading and writing courses, literary walks and literary prizes. The day begins in the theatre foyer, with membership renewals and new sign-ups (members receive a collectible cup) and applications for the prize juries.

In the hall the year's activities are presented, including the 17th Caffè Corretto Città di Cave Literary Prize, for unpublished works, and the 2nd "Caffè e Latte" Literary Contest for children and young people, which this year adds a new section, "Storie deliziose", devoted to stories about food. The guest of the day is the writer Vincenza Alfano, who presents her novel "La guerriera dei libri" (Solferino), inspired by a true story, with an interview, readings, choreography and projected images.
