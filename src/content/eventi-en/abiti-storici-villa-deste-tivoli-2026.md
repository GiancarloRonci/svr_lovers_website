On Sunday 27 September 2026, from 10:30 AM, in the gardens of Villa d'Este in Tivoli, visitors are welcomed by musicians in historical dress near the Owl Fountain. A parade along the garden avenues follows, interspersed with stories and anecdotes from the sixteenth century, as part of the European Heritage Days.

Participation is free but requires the purchase of the regular admission ticket (15 euros, unless reductions or free entry apply) and is by reservation, while places last: to book, call +39 0774 330329.
