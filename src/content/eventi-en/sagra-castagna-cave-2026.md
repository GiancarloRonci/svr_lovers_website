From October 23 to 25, 2026, Cave hosts the 92nd edition of the Chestnut and Local Products Festival, a long-standing event celebrating the town's chestnut-growing tradition and its surrounding woods.

The alleys of the historic center fill with roasted chestnuts, local specialties and traditional dishes such as "gnocchetti a sassetto" and the "serpetta" sweet. The program includes more than 40 food and craft stalls, live music, guided tours of the Lorenzo Ferri Museum, and the traditional "Bicicastagnata", a 45 km cycling ride. Dedicated parking areas and a shuttle service are available.

*Source: [Sagra della Castagna di Cave](https://www.sagradellacastagnacave.it/)*
