From 14 to 28 November 2026 the Campaegli mountain hut, on the plateau above Cervara di Roma in the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park, hosts a ceramics workshop organised by Campaegli Wild.

The Park's page does not give the days and times of the sessions, the fee or how to sign up: for information and bookings, call the organisers' info line on +39 350 115 7226.

*Source: [Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=143591)*
