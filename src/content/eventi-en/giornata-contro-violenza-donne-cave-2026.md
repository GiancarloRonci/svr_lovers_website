On Wednesday 25 November 2026, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, the Dimensione Donna association holds an event at the Teatro Comunale "Milco Paravani" in Cave.

The event is in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given the programme or the time: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the association.
