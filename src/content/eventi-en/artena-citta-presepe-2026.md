From 8 December 2026 to 6 January 2027 "Artena Città Presepe" (Artena, Nativity Town) returns: the historic centre of Artena, one of the largest pedestrian villages in Europe, becomes a trail devoted to the Nativity, crafts and Christmas traditions. Over 50 cellars are turned into small Christmas worlds, with nativity scenes, handicrafts, local products and small markets, and there is also a judged nativity-scene exhibition and competition, with the awards planned for 6 January.

Entry is free. Openings are concentrated on weekends and public holidays, but the detailed calendar of each day has yet to be set by the organisers. For parking, the reference point is Piazza Genocchi.

The dates come from the local press and the opening calendar has not been published yet: check them on the event's channels before setting off.
