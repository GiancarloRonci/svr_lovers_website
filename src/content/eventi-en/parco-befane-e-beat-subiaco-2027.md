On Saturday 2 January 2027 the Pro Loco di Subiaco puts on "Parco, Befane e Beat", a day in town that brings together Epiphany traditions, contemporary music, crafts and small local producers.

The event is in the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet say where it takes place or at what time: ask the Pro Loco for details. Information: 347 596 5637, proloco.subiaco@virgilio.it.
