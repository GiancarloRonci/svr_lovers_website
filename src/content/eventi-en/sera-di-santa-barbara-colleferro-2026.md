On Friday 4 December 2026, Saint Barbara's day, from 8 pm to 11 pm the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro holds "La sera di Santa Barbara" (Saint Barbara's evening) at the Covered Market.

The programme has readings and stories on the town's history by the writers' group 00034 Writers' Square, and a concert by local musician Stefano Fioretti dedicated to Frank Sinatra and the great crooners. The Pro Loco's page lists no tickets for sale. For information: 331 461 6819.
