On Tuesday 29 December 2026 ASTRIS Astronomia repeats "Storie e scienza del cielo d'inverno nei Monti Simbruini" ("Stories and science of the winter sky") in Subiaco, with Luca Spuntoni's talk "C/2026 A2 (Bok), the Christmas comet" and, weather permitting, daytime solar observation and night-sky viewing. The first date is Sunday 20 December.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 347 480 9153, astris@astrisroma.org.
