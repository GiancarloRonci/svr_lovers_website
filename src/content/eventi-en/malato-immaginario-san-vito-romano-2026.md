On Sunday 13 December 2026, at 5 pm, the Teatro Caesar in San Vito Romano hosts Molière's "Il malato immaginario" ("The Imaginary Invalid"), with Edoardo Siravo. On stage with him are Andrea Bezzi, Francesca Bianco, Francesca Buttarazzi, Gabriella Casali, Giuseppe Cattani, Alessandro Pizzuto, Susy Sergiacomo, Roberto Tesconi and Tonino Tosto. The show is in Italian.

It is the second event of the free "AmiAMO il Teatro" season, after ["Trilussa e il vino"](/en/eventi/trilussa-e-il-vino-san-vito-romano-2026) on 29 November. Admission is free, with unreserved seating; 2027 season subscribers have a reserved seat (information on 380 180 5894 or 338 527 4636).
