On Sunday 13 December 2026 Trevi nel Lazio hosts "Sinfonie d'inverno nel cuore dei Simbruini" ("Winter symphonies in the heart of the Simbruini"): an event called "L'Eco del Cervo" ("The echo of the deer") that brings together cinema, music and Christmas tradition.

The Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park first listed it for Tuesday 8 December in Subiaco; the park's page now gives Sunday 13 December in Trevi. Time, exact venue and cost have not been announced yet. Information and booking: Gioacchino Galiena, 339 228 7008, gioacchino.galiena@alice.it.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144178)*
