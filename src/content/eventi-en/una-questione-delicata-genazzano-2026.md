On Sunday 15 November 2026, at 5:30 PM, the Teatro Cinema Italia in Genazzano stages "Una questione delicata - Madre certa, padre meno" ("A Delicate Matter - Mother Certain, Father Less So"), the second show of the new 2026/27 theatre season, which is devoted mainly to comedy. The theatre has recently been renovated, both its spaces and its technical equipment.

The season is organised by "Insieme per il Teatro" with the Pro Loco and the Municipality of Genazzano. For information and bookings: 392 945 9132 (Mara), 339 656 1813 (Francesca).
