On Saturday 14 and Sunday 15 November 2026 Castel Madama celebrates the new oil with the twentieth edition of OliOlive, the festival devoted to extra virgin olive oil and table olives, one of the town's best-known products. The festival is in Via Sant'Anna and entry is free; on Friday 13 it opens with a meeting with local schools at the Castello Orsini.

**Saturday 14:** at 10:30 am the stands open and children's entertainment begins; at 11 am, in the Sala Baronale of the Castello Orsini, the town signs a friendship pact with the Comune of Rocca Imperiale, and at 11:30 am the flag-wavers and musicians of Castel Madama perform. At 5 pm the Smilf Street Band plays, at 7:30 pm the food stalls open, at 8 pm there's a DJ set and at 9:30 pm the live show of [2000 in Spinta](/en/eventi/2000-in-spinta-castel-madama-2026).

**Sunday 15:** stands and children's entertainment from 10:30 am, at 11 am a concert by Ever Rock, at 12 noon a show by ASD Play, at 4 pm more children's entertainment and at 4:30 pm a concert by the 883 tribute band "Generazione 1000 Watt". At 5:45 pm the official shirt for the thirtieth anniversary of the Palio Madama Margarita d'Austria is presented, and at 6 pm the festival closes with the [Luca Dirisio concert](/en/eventi/luca-dirisio-castel-madama-2026).
