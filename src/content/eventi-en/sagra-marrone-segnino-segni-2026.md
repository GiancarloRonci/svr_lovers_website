From Thursday 22 to Sunday 25 October 2026, Segni, in the Lepini Mountains, hosts the 69th edition of the Sagra del Marrone Segnino, the autumn festival devoted to the chestnut harvested in the woods surrounding the town. The dates were announced by the Municipality of Segni, which has approved the festival's regulations and published the forms for those who wish to take part with stalls and food stands.

The festival takes place in the streets and squares of the historic center, with a food area in Piazza Risorgimento. The detailed programme, with times and events for each day, has not been published yet: it is worth checking the Municipality's website in the days before the festival.

*Source: [Municipality of Segni](https://www.comune.segni.rm.it/novita/notizie/novita_115.html)*
