From Thursday 22 to Sunday 25 October 2026, Segni, in the Lepini Mountains, hosts the 69th edition of the Sagra del Marrone Segnino, the autumn festival devoted to the chestnut harvested in the woods surrounding the town. The dates were announced by the Municipality of Segni. The festival takes place in the streets and squares of the historic center, where the fraschette, the traditional taverns, serve roasted chestnuts and dishes made with marroni.

The programme day by day:

- **Thursday 22:** at 5:00 PM the exhibition of historical costumes "Fili… di memoria" at the Town Hall, at 6:30 PM a choir concert in the Cathedral and from 7:30 PM the tasting route "Tra Vicoli e Calici" ("Among Alleys and Wine Glasses"), with AIS Lazio and the Pro Loco.
- **Friday 23:** exhibitions and talks in the afternoon; from 7:30 PM the wine cellars and food stands open and at 9:00 PM, in Piazza Santa Maria Assunta, the Gruppo Folk Parquaria plays folk music.
- **Saturday 24:** at 10:00, 11:00 and 12:00 guided walks through the historic center starting from Porta Maggiore, and at 11:30 a guided tour of the Nymphaeum; food stands open from 12:00 and from 7:30 PM. From 6:00 PM the SMILF Street Band plays through the streets up to the main square, followed by a DJ set.
- **Sunday 25:** guided walks in the morning, at 11:30 the Città di Segni town band in Piazza Santa Maria Assunta and at 4:00 PM the historical Copéta parade with the flag-wavers of Cori. The festival closes at 9:00 PM with a concert by Hernicantus, followed by a DJ set.

Admission to the festival is free. For the guided tours of the Nymphaeum, contact the Amici del Museo di Segni association (06 97260072). The programme comes from a Segni website that reproduces the organisers' official material, not from a page of the Municipality: before setting off, check the channels of the Municipality and the Pro Loco.
