From Thursday 22 to Sunday 25 October 2026, Segni, in the Lepini Mountains, hosts the 69th edition of the Sagra del Marrone Segnino, the autumn festival devoted to the chestnut harvested in the woods surrounding the town. The dates were announced by the Municipality of Segni, which has approved the festival's regulations and published the forms for those who wish to take part with stalls and food stands.

The festival takes place in the streets and squares of the historic center, with a food area in Piazza Risorgimento. Thursday 22 opens with "Tra Vicoli e Calici" ("Among Alleys and Wine Glasses"): tasting routes guided by AIS Lazio, live music, local crafts and the historical Copéta parade, with flag-wavers and drummers in costume (the guided tastings have limited places). From Friday to Sunday the fraschette, the traditional taverns of the old town, open their doors, with roasted chestnuts, marroni, concerts and guided tours.

The Municipality has not yet published the official programme with the times for each day: before setting off, check the channels of the Municipality and the Segni Pro Loco.
