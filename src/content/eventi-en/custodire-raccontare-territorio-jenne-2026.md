On Friday 2 October 2026 the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park and the astronomy association Astris offer an evening of nature and sky as part of "Custodire e raccontare il territorio" (Caring for and telling the land). At 5:00 PM there is the official opening, "Bonifica straordinaria: ripartiamo dal territorio", followed at 5:30 PM by solar observation in Via della Bandita at Livata, on the small hill opposite the Park Visitor Centre.

At 7:30 PM there is a tasting of local products, at 8:30 PM a session on the red deer's rutting call at Fondi di Jenne, and at 9:30 PM, also at Fondi di Jenne, guided observation of planets and deep-sky objects. Participation is free, with booking by email to astris@astrisroma.org. Participants make their own way between the different locations.

The programme published by the Park gives 3 October as the date in one place: before setting off, it is worth asking Astris to confirm the day, at the same email address.
