On Friday 2 October 2026 the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park and the astronomy association Astris offer an evening of nature and sky as part of "Custodire e raccontare il territorio" (Caring for and telling the land). At 5:00 PM there is the official opening, "Bonifica straordinaria: ripartiamo dal territorio", followed at 5:30 PM by solar observation at Fonti di Jenne.

At 7:30 PM there is a tasting of local products, at 8:30 PM a session on the red deer's rutting call at Frassigno, and at 9:30 PM guided observation of planets and deep-sky objects. Participation is free, with booking by email to astris@astrisroma.org. Participants make their own way between the different locations.

*Source: [Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=142808)*
