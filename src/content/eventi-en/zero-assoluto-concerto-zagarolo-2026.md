On Saturday 3 October 2026, at 9:30 PM, Zero Assoluto take the stage in Piazza Santa Maria, in the historic centre of Zagarolo. The Roman pop duo of Thomas De Gasperi and Matteo Maffucci, known for hits such as "Semplicemente", "Svegliarsi la mattina" and "Grazie", is the headline act of the 82nd Sagra dell'Uva (Grape Festival).

Admission to the concert is free. For those arriving by train, on Saturday a shuttle links the station to the town centre from 7:00 PM to 1:00 AM, running every 30 minutes.
