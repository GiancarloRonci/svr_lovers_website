On Saturday 14 November 2026 at 9:30 pm "2000 in Spinta" stops in Castel Madama for the first time. It is a music show touring Italy with the dance and pop hits of the 2000s, complete with confetti, streamers, flames, bubble machines, entertainment and smoke effects, and plenty of free gadgets for the audience.

The show is at Via Sant'Anna 5 and closes the first day of [OliOlive](/en/eventi/oliolive-castel-madama-2026), the new olive oil festival organised by the Municipality; the next day, in the same place, there is the [Luca Dirisio concert](/en/eventi/luca-dirisio-castel-madama-2026). Entry is completely free. After the show the evening goes on with a DJ set.
