On Saturday 5 December 2026 "Tracce d'inverno" ("Winter tracks"), by the Collettivo L'Aquila Reale ETS association, returns: nature walks and creative workshops on biodiversity and Christmas traditions, in the areas of Subiaco, Filettino and Vallepietra. The first date is Saturday 28 November.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Booking: 349 689 8842, contact@laquilarealelicenza.org. It is worth asking which town the day takes place in.
