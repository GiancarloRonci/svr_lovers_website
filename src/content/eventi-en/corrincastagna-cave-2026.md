On Sunday 25 October 2026, the last day of the [Chestnut Festival](/en/eventi/sagra-castagna-cave-2026), Cave hosts the 5th Corrincastagna, a 9.6 km competitive road race run with Atletica Cave. The start is at 9:15 AM from Piazza Santa Croce.

Registration costs 12 euros and is done on the Race Service website; the first 500 entrants receive a race pack. Prizes go to the top three men and women overall, the top six in each category and the top ten clubs. For information: Davide 331 808 3333, Andrea 393 043 4738, Rossano 338 872 8616.

*Source: [Monti Prenestini](https://montiprenestini.info/cave-si-prepara-alla-5a-corrincastagna-domenica-25-ottobre-torna-la-gara-podistica-competitiva-di-96-chilometri/)*
