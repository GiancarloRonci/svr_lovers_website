On Sunday 6 December 2026, at 6 pm, the Teatro Comunale "Milco Paravani" in Cave hosts "Possessione" ("Possession"), a show by the C.A.M.I. 2020 association and the Off Rome Company freely inspired by Euripides, directed by Igor Geat. It is part of the theatre's 2026/27 season, in the middle of the [Christmas in Cave](/en/eventi/natale-a-cave-2026) celebrations. The show is in Italian.

Tickets are on sale in Cave at Dino's Photo (via V. Sessi 21), Silvana Santi (viale Pio XII 64) and Caffetteria San Carlo (via G. Clementi 8). Information and booking: 338 973 8924.
