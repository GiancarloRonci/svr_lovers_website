On Sunday 6 December 2026 the C.A.M.I. 2020 association and the Off Rome Company present "Possessione" ("Possession") in Cave, in the middle of the [Christmas in Cave](/en/eventi/natale-a-cave-2026) celebrations.

The show is in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given the venue, the time or ticket prices: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the organisers. The show is in Italian.
