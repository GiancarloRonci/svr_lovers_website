The Youth Mountaineering (Alpinismo Giovanile) group of the CAI Palestrina section proposes a hike in the Prenestini Mountains starting from the Mentorella Sanctuary, one of the most striking places of faith and nature in the area between Guadagnolo and Capranica Prenestina, up to Monte Cerella. A hiking-grade (E) route designed for children and teenagers, accompanied by Youth Mountaineering educators.

The activity is open to CAI members and their families. For details on registration, meeting point and times, contact the CAI Palestrina section (Piazza di Porta San Martino 11, Palestrina) or check the official website.
