On Saturday 24 October 2026 "Boschi in movimento – Il ritmo del bosco, il respiro del movimento" (Woods in motion) returns, a day that combines walking and yoga in the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park. The meeting point is at 9:30 AM at the Rifugio Montano di Campaegli, with departure at 10:00 AM and return at around 4:30 PM.

The route is a loop of about 13 km, with 200 metres of elevation gain and medium difficulty, leading from Campaegli to the clearings of Camposecco through centuries-old beech woods and rocky stretches, at altitudes between 1,450 and 1,600 metres. There are stops along the way to observe the landscape and wildlife tracks; at Camposecco the teacher Flavia Negozio leads a Sensory Hatha Yoga session, with sounds and essential oils. The group is accompanied by the hiking guide Samuele Giancarlini.

The fee is 25 euros per person and includes the guide, the yoga session and assistance. Registration is through the online form linked on the event page. Bring hiking shoes, layered clothing, a windproof jacket, a yoga mat and at least a litre and a half of water. Information: +39 334 8302603 (Samuele), +39 320 4108053 (Flavia), infoescursionidelsam@gmail.com.
