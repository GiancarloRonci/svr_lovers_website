On Saturday 7 November 2026 at 10 am the Biblioteca Comunale Fantoniana in Palestrina hosts the second meeting of "SpazioAI", the series through which the Municipality, as part of its Strategic Plan for digital innovation, introduces residents to artificial intelligence.

The meeting starts from the basic concepts, explained simply, and moves on to the impact of AI on the job market, schools and society. It ends with an open discussion with the audience on its advantages and risks. Entry is free.
