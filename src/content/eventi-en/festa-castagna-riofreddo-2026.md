On Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November 2026 Riofreddo hosts the 42nd Festa della Castagna (Chestnut Festival), organised by the Pro Loco of Riofreddo together with the Pro Loco of Civita d'Oricola and the village's senior centre. The festival is a food walk through the streets of the old village, with roaming bands and street performers.

On both days the food stands open at 12:00 noon. The full menu costs 18 euros and includes bruschetta, polenta (or fettuccine with mushrooms and sausage), grilled arrosticini and sausage, bread, a drink, dessert and roast chestnuts. For children there is a 5-euro menu with a hot dog and a drink.

Information: proloco.riofreddo@libero.it, [www.prolocoriofreddo.it](http://www.prolocoriofreddo.it) and the ProLocoRiofreddo Facebook page.
