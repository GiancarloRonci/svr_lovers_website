From Friday 9 to Sunday 11 October 2026, Palazzo Rospigliosi in Zagarolo hosts "Disegni, graffi e altri fastidi" ("Drawings, scratches and other nuisances"), a solo exhibition by Igor Geat tracing around thirty years of the artist's work, from 1996 to 2026.

The show focuses on the creative process: alongside finished works it displays sketches, early ideas and pieces left unfinished, in dialogue with the history and architecture of the palace. The exhibition is curated by Letizia Mula, with a critical text by Serena Borghesani, and is organised by APS OFF ROME and C.A.M.I. 2020.

Admission is free. Opening hours are not given by the source: for information call +39 334 1933800 or +39 339 3200355.
