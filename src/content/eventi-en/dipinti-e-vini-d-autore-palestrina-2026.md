On Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November 2026 the birthplace of Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, at Vicolo Pierluigi 3, hosts the 16th edition of the exhibition "Dipinti&Vini d'Autore" (Paintings and Fine Wines), an event of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina that pairs artworks with local wines.

Fifteen artists are on show: Paolo Cannucciari, Marco Diaco, Paul De Haan, Felixandro, Giovanna Gallo, Maurizio Lupo, Sofia Nisticò, Maria Felice Petyx, Francesca Provenzano, Anna Salvati, Francesca Santarelli, Angela Scapaticci, Susy Senzacqua, Stefano Sorrentino and Anna Maria Tessaro.

The exhibition is open on both days from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. For information, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
