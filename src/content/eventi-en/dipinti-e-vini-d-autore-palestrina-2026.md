On Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November 2026 the birthplace of Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina hosts the 16th edition of the exhibition "Dipinti&Vini d'Autore" (Paintings and Fine Wines), an event of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina that pairs artworks with local wines.

Opening hours have not been announced yet: for information, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
