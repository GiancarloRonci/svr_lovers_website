From 11 to 13 September 2026, Roiate hosts the 44th edition of the "Sagra dell'Abbacchio" (Lamb Festival), three days of tradition, taste, music and entertainment in the small village of the Monti Prenestini.

**Programme**

- **Friday 11 September** - 4:30 PM public meeting on "Farming and territory: from pastoralism to value chain development"; 7:30 PM food stalls open with traditional dishes; 9:00 PM folk music with Brigallè; from 11:30 PM DJ set
- **Saturday 12 September** - 9:00 PM concert by Paolo Vallesi, followed by the Carmen Russo Show, a tribute to Raffaella Carrà directed and choreographed by Enzo Paolo Turchi; from 11:30 PM DJ set with Papy & Miha
- **Sunday 13 September** - 9:00 AM guided nature walk (an easy route of about 3 hours, 200m elevation gain, meeting at Piazzale della Libertà); 4:00 PM street entertainment with the SMILF band; 7:00 PM closing concert by Alla Riversa

*The programme may be subject to change: check the official channels of the Municipality of Roiate for updates.*
