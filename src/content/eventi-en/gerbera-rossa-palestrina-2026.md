On Saturday 21 November 2026 Palestrina hosts the second edition of "Gerbera Rossa – Tra arte, musica e parole" (Art, music and words), a public event raising awareness against all forms of gender-based violence, ahead of the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on 25 November.

The day begins at 11 am with the installation of a red bench in the Valvarino park. At 5 pm, in Piazza Santa Maria degli Angeli, there is a flash mob open to everyone; at 6 pm, in the Sala Borghesani, the exhibition "Nonostante tutto VIVO" (Alive despite everything), curated by Massimo Iermano, opens and stays open until 25 November. The event is sponsored by the Municipality of Palestrina and organised by a network of local associations, artists and residents.

Gerbera Rossa closes on Sunday 29 November at 5 pm with an evening at the Auditorium Pierluigi, with free entry.
