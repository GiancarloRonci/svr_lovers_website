On Saturday 21 November 2026 Palestrina hosts the second edition of "Gerbera Rossa – Tra arte, musica e parole" (Art, music and words), a public event raising awareness against all forms of gender-based violence, ahead of the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women on 25 November.

It starts at 5 pm in Piazza Santa Maria degli Angeli with a flash mob open to everyone. Afterwards, in the Sala Borghesani, the exhibition "Nonostante tutto VIVO" (Alive despite everything), curated by Massimo Iermano, opens. The event is sponsored by the Municipality of Palestrina and organised by a network of local associations, artists and residents.
