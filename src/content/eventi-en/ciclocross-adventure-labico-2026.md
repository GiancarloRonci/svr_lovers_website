On Saturday 12 December 2026 Labico holds the first edition of the "Ciclocross Adventure Città di Labico", a cyclocross race organised by ASD Meta Bike with the town council's patronage. The meeting point is via di Valle Fredda 45/49.

Registration and bib collection run from 8 am to 9 am. The first race (40 minutes) starts at 9:30 and the second (60 minutes) at 10:30; there are refreshments afterwards and prizes at noon, for the top five in each category and the top three teams. Entry costs 20 euros online, until noon on Friday 11 December, or 25 euros on the day. For information: 392 960 8137.
