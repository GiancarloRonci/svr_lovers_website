On Sunday, October 4, 2026, at 4 PM, the church of S. Pietro Apostolo in Sambuci hosts "Serafico in ardore", an event dedicated to Saint Francis of Assisi on the eighth centenary of his death, which took place at the Porziuncola on the evening of October 3, 1226. The initiative is promoted by the Associazione Culturale Terzo Millennio and falls on the saint's liturgical feast day.

At the heart of the event is Canto XI of Dante's Paradise, which retraces the life of Francis: his renunciation of his father's wealth, his marriage to Lady Poverty, his first followers and the recognition of his Order. The verses will be read by Henos Palmisano and Aurora Fratini and commented on by Prof. Rino Caputo, an internationally renowned Dante scholar, while soprano Michela Marconi and organist Filippo Tenisci perform pieces dedicated to the saint. It is also a chance to recall Saint Francis's ties to the Aniene Valley.
