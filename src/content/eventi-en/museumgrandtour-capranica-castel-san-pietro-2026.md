On 4 October 2026, the "Tracciati. Luoghi, storie e culture attraversando il Museumgrandtour" series stops in Capranica Prenestina and Castel San Pietro Romano with a meeting dedicated to "Landscapes of memory, between nature, photography and rebirth".

The itinerary, curated together with the directors of the museums involved, explores the relationship between the landscape of the Monti Prenestini, photography, and the natural and cultural rebirth of the two villages.

Participation is free with limited spots: booking is recommended by writing to info@museumgrandtour.org.
