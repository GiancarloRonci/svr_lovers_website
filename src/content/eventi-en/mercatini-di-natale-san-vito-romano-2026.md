The Christmas Markets return to the historic centre of San Vito Romano, organised by the Pro Loco. There are two market days: **Sunday 13 December** and **Sunday 20 December 2026**, in the streets of the old town, with stalls by local sellers, artisans and makers and a festive atmosphere. There is no market on the days in between.

The Pro Loco is still collecting applications from stallholders, so times and programme have not yet been announced: follow the Pro Loco's Facebook page for updates. Anyone who wants to take part with a stall can write to mercatini.sanvito@email.com or call +39 338 883 8931 or +39 329 228 5668.
