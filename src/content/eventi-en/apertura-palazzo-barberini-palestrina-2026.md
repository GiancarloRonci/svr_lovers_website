On Sunday 15 November 2026 at 11 am Palazzo Barberini in Palestrina opens its private wing, usually closed to visitors, for a special guided tour. The tour tells the story of the Barberini family and its bond with Palestrina, taking in the Palatine Chapel of Santa Rosalia, the private apartments of the piano nobile and the Barberini Nymphaeum.

Meet a few minutes before 11 am in front of the Portico di Fortuna, at Via Barberini 24. The ticket including the guided tour costs 15 euros, 7.50 euros for children aged 7 to 14, free under 7. Places are limited and booking is required through the [online form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf7B-VjG3qG0mgGa6ms3jeMCfcrWicPMGK0L04tL7L_3YuRNg/viewform); for information, info@palazzobarberinipalestrina.it. The previous opening, on 18 October, sold out.
