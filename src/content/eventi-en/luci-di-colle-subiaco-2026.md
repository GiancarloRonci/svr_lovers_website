On Saturday 19 December 2026 the Madonna di Pompei Rione Colle association organises "Le Luci di Colle" ("The lights of Colle: traditions, nativity scenes and flavours in the heart of the district"), with the lighting of the district's Christmas tree and the opening of its artistic nativity scene.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: 349 536 2182, madonnadipompeirionecolle@gmail.com.
