On Saturday 21 November 2026 Castello Colonna in Genazzano hosts a concert by the Corale Lorenzo Perosi and the Schola Cantorum A. Belli, two choirs brought together for an event in the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina.

The start time has not been announced yet: for information on time and admission, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
