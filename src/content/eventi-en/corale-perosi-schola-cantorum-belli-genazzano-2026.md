On Saturday 21 November 2026, at 8:45 PM, Palazzo Colonna in Genazzano hosts "Mozart: Grazia e Invenzione (La stagione giovanile)" ("Mozart: Grace and Invention – The Early Years"), a concert devoted to the young Mozart in the 2026 programme of the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina.

On stage are the Orchestra delle Cento Città, the Corale Lorenzo Perosi of Cave and the Schola Cantorum A. Belli of Genazzano, with soprano Cristina Santonico, mezzo-soprano Beatrice Mercuri, tenor Michele Della Ventura and bass Paolo Breda Bulgherini, conducted by Maestro Giovanni Proietti. The first half features the Symphony No. 29 in A major, K. 201; the second the Mass in C major, K. 317, known as the "Coronation Mass", for soloists, choir, orchestra and organ.

The concert closes the day on which Genazzano, together with the Foundation, celebrates the Virgo Fidelis: at 6 PM there is Holy Mass at the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Good Counsel, followed by a reception, and at 8:45 PM the music at Palazzo Colonna.

Booking is required for the concert, by email to fondazionepalestrina@gmail.com. For further information, contact the Foundation on 06 953 8083 or at fond.palestrina@tiscali.it.
