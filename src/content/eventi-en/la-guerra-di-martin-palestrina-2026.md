On Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 October 2026, at 6:00 PM, the Auditorium Pierluigi in Palestrina opens its season preview with "La guerra di Martin" (Martin's War), a musical comedy "for ages 6 to 90" based on the play by Francesco Silvestri. Directed by Claudio Tagliacozzo, the show is produced by the cultural association "Arte Madre" and blends music, theatre and irony for audiences of every generation.

Ticket prices and booking details have not been announced yet. The season continues with "A Midsummer Night's Dream" (21 and 22 November) and "Rapunzel – Il Musical" (12, 13, 19 and 20 December).
