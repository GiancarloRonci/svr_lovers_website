On Saturday 21 November 2026, at 11 am, the Civic Natural History Museum of the Prenestini Mountains opens its new displays in Palazzo Barberini, in Piazza Aristide Frezza in Capranica Prenestina. The renovation was backed by the town council.

The opening is attended by the mayor of Capranica Prenestina, the deputy mayor of the Metropolitan City of Rome and the museum's director. At 12:30 pm there is a tasting of local products. The event is open to the public.
