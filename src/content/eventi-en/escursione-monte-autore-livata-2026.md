On Sunday 25 October 2026, Nomos Trek leads a guided hike from Campo dell'Osso towards Monte Autore, the "roof" of the Monti Simbruini, through beech forests and high-altitude views. The meeting point is at 9:45 AM at the Taverna di Stuzzikò, on via dei Boschi in Livata, with the return expected around 3:30 PM.

The hike is also suitable for families. Bring a packed lunch, water, hiking boots and trekking poles. The fee is 20 euros per person, including the annual membership card. Bookings: +39 347 824 7257, info@nomostrek.com.
