On Sunday 20 September 2026, Piazza della Liberazione in Palestrina hosts the Festa di Fine Estate, the closing event of the "Estate Prenestina 2026" summer season, organised together with the square's local businesses.

At 8:30 pm, "T.I.R. – Tutto il Resto" takes the stage with a tribute to singer-songwriter Franco Califano, followed at 10:30 pm by singer-songwriter Jimmy Ingrassia. Free admission.
