On Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November 2026 Arcinazzo Romano, at the foot of the Simbruini Mountains, celebrates its 52nd Sagra del Marrone. It starts on Saturday at 10:00 AM and carries on until Sunday evening, with stands in Piazza Santa Lucia and along the lanes of the historic centre: roasted chestnuts and marroni from the grill, polenta made with chestnut flour, jams, homemade cakes, chestnut liqueurs and local wine.

Over the two days there are roving folk performances, live music, markets and local crafts, and a re-enactment of the Ballo della Signoraccia, a traditional dance. Guided walks through the chestnut woods and the old chestnut-drying huts are also planned.

The dates come from a local news article and not yet from an official source: before setting off, check times, walks and traffic arrangements on the channels of the Municipality and the Pro Loco of Arcinazzo Romano.
