On Friday 11 and Saturday 12 September 2026, at 6:30 PM, the Teatro Belvedere di Ponente in Castel San Pietro Romano hosts "Eracleide - Il mito di Ercole dalla Transumanza ai Giochi Olimpici" (Eracleide - The Myth of Hercules from Transhumance to the Olympic Games).

The show offers a contemporary retelling of the myth of Hercules, connecting it to the history of transhumance and athletic contests: through historical research and dramaturgy, theatre becomes a way of telling the relationship between people, land and memory.

**Creative team**

- Adaptation and direction: Igor Geat
- Text: Marco Colli and Igor Geat
- Choreography and dance: Ilenia Ronci

**Cast**

Andrea Fanfoni, Cristian Masella, Raffaella Mancini, Ugo Cardinali, Martina Capitani, Letizia Mula, Luca Cardinali.

**Useful information**

Admission €10.00, pre-sale available at the Castel San Pietro Romano InfoPoint. For information: +39 339 3200355 - +39 339 7902407, email cami2020.produzioni@gmail.com.

The show continues its tour with a stop in Palestrina on 26 September 2026.
