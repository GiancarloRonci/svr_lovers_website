From 15 October to 1 November 2026 Palazzo Morandi, the former headquarters of the BPD company and one of Colleferro's most striking historic buildings, hosts a solo exhibition by artist Maria Rosaria Colaiacomo, presented by the Comune di Colleferro.

The opening, with a ribbon-cutting ceremony attended by the town administration, is on Thursday 15 October at 4:30 PM. The exhibition is then open every day, holidays included, until Sunday 1 November (closing at 7:00 PM on the last day). Admission is free; for information: Pro Loco Città di Colleferro, +39 331 461 6819.
