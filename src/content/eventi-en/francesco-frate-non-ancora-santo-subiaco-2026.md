On Friday 6 November 2026, at 6:30 PM, the Teatro Narzio in Subiaco hosts "Francesco, un frate non ancora Santo" ("Francis, a friar not yet a Saint"), an afternoon of music, theatrical readings and reflections on the figure of Saint Francis, organised by the Associazione Carpe Diem and included in the calendar of the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park.

On stage are the Coro Polifonico Città di Subiaco conducted by Maestro Stefano Quaresima, Francesco Basanisi on piano, theatrical readings of the *Canticle of the Creatures* by the Associazione Viaggiatori Viaggianti, and the voice of Benedetto Sette. The evening is presented by journalist Valentina Renzetti.
