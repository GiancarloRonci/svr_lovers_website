On Saturday 26 September 2026 at 9:30 PM, Lillo e Greg take the stage of the Cavea del Parco del Castello in Colleferro with "Latte e i suoi Derivati", their historic comedy rock band formed in the early 1990s, known for ironic songs and musical parodies.

The concert is the final event of the second edition of the Altaluna Festival, and admission is free. At 11:30 PM the evening continues with the GIALOSAI DJ set, featuring X-Uberant and Ayko.
