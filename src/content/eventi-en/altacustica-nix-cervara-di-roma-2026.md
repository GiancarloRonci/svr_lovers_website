On Sunday 27 December 2026 the Centro Musica Subiaco APS brings "AltAcustica Nix" to Cervara di Roma, a concert of music by Antonio Vivaldi and traditional songs, part of the Simbruini Mountains Park's "Archimenti nei borghi" series. It is the second date after the concert in Jenne on Saint Stephen's Day.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, exact venue or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: 328 626 8915, centromusicasubiaco@gmail.com.
