On Sunday 20 September 2026, at 5:00 pm, the Auditorium Pierluigi in Palestrina hosts "Cantastelle", a show by and with Andrea Perrozzi where music becomes storytelling. A journey through Italian musical memory with songs by De André, De Gregori and Dalla, touching on the moments that define life: love, loss, dreams and hope. Every performance is different from the last.

The event is part of the celebrations for the 50th anniversary of the Circolo Culturale Prenestino Roberto Simeoni, which organises the evening; part of the proceeds will go to the paediatric ward of the Ospedale Coniugi Bernardini in Palestrina. Minimum contribution 10 euros (5 euros for people with disabilities), reservations at 338 3606787.
