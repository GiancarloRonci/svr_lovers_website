Every Sunday in September 2026, the "Ardito Desio" Geopalaeontological Museum in Rocca di Cave and its Planetarium open their doors to visitors and families with a calendar of guided tours and shows.

- **13 September**: museum visits at 10:30 AM, 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM; planetarium show "Dalla Terra all'Universo" ("From Earth to the Universe") at 11:00 AM and 12:30 PM.
- **20 September**: same museum hours; planetarium show "Regata Spaziale" ("Space Regatta") at 11:00 AM, workshop "Meno 3 2 1 Lancio" ("Minus 3 2 1 Launch") at 11:30 AM, and "Dalla Terra all'Universo" at 12:30 PM.
- **27 September**: museum visits at 10:30 AM and 4:30 PM; planetarium show "Viaggio nel Sistema Solare" ("Journey through the Solar System") from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM and workshop "Piccoli paleontologi in azione" ("Little Palaeontologists in Action") at 11:30 AM.

A chance to discover up close the fossils and geological history of the Monti Prenestini, with activities suitable for children too.
