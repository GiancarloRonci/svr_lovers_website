---
title: "Special Evening Opening of Villa d'Este for 1 Euro"
description: For the European Heritage Days, Villa d'Este in Tivoli opens in the evening with a symbolic 1 euro ticket.
dataInizio: 2026-09-26
luogo: Villa d'Este, Tivoli (Rome)
tipologiaEvento: Cultural Event
---

On Saturday 26 September 2026, for the European Heritage Days, Villa d'Este in Tivoli opens exceptionally in the evening, from 7:45 PM to 11:45 PM, with last entry at 10:45 PM. The ticket costs a symbolic one euro and can be bought at the entrance ticket office on the evening itself.

The other Villæ of Tivoli, Hadrian's Villa and the Sanctuary of Hercules Victor, are also open in the evening for 1 euro on the same night.
