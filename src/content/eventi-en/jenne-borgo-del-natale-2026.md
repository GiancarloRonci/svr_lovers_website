From 19 December 2026 to 10 January 2027 Jenne becomes "il Borgo del Natale" ("the Christmas village"): the Pro Loco sets up "Il Villaggio di Babbo Natale sui Monti Simbruini", a Father Christmas village in the town. Opening days and times have not been published yet.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: Pro Loco Jenne, 329 799 4529, prolocojenne@libero.it.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144186)*
