From Saturday 5 to Tuesday 8 December 2026, over the Immaculate Conception long weekend, Cave gets ready for the holidays with "Il Natale a Cave" (Christmas in Cave), four days of events organised by the Crearte APS association.

For now the event appears only in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, without programme, venues or times: follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of Crearte APS for details, which usually come out shortly before the event.

*Source: [Pro Loco Città di Cave – Events 2026](https://www.prolococaveaps.it/eventi-2026/)*
