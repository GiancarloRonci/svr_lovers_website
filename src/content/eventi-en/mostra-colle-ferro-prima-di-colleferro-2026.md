From 17 to 27 October 2026 the Archivio storico "Renzo Rossi" in Colleferro hosts "Colle Ferro prima di Colleferro" (Colle Ferro before Colleferro), a documentary exhibition that is part of the project "Un mare di Novecento". Through the original designs of architect Michele Oddini, it tells the story of the first Workers' Village, built in 1912: the factory, the houses, the church, the cinema and the everyday spaces from which the town grew.

The opening is on Saturday 17 October at 11:00 AM. Schools can book guided tours by contacting the Archive on 06 9720 3602 or 06 9720 3320.
