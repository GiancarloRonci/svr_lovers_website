On Saturday 3 October 2026, at 8:30pm, the "Livio Gratton" Astronomical Park in Rocca di Papa, in the heart of the Castelli Romani Regional Park, hosts "Occhi su Saturno" (Eyes on Saturn), a special evening devoted to the ringed planet, organised by the Associazione Tuscolana di Astronomia (ATA).

The evening opens with an introductory talk, followed by a multimedia show in the planetarium and sky observation with the park's telescopes, including a 40 cm Dobsonian. In case of bad weather, the observation is replaced by an in-depth tour of the park. The evening ends at around 11pm.

Admission is free but places are limited: booking is required by 1pm on Friday 2 October, using the form on the ATA website. For information: segreteria@ataonweb.it, +39 06 94436469.
