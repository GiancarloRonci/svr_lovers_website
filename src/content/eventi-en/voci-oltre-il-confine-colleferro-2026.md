On Saturday 24 October 2026, at 9:00 PM, the Teatro Comunale Vittorio Veneto in Colleferro hosts "Voci oltre il confine - Memorie che parlano" ("Voices beyond the border - Memories that speak"), a show that gives voice to memories and testimonies to rediscover pages of historical memory and reflect on the value of identity.

The show is based on an idea by Cristina Valeri, with artistic direction by Maestro Paolo Gatti and historical advice from Marino Micich, director of the Fiume Historical Archive Museum in Rome. On stage are Gaia De Stefano, Anna Palmisano, Francesco Sorbelli, Mauro Sorbelli, Roberto Usai, Arianna Visca and Monica Zazzera, with narration by Elisa Rossi. It is presented by Stanza 236 APS, with the support of the Lazio Region and the patronage of the Municipality of Colleferro and the Associazione Nazionale Venezia Giulia e Dalmazia.

No tickets are sold online: for information contact the Colleferro Pro Loco, tel. +39 331 4616819.
