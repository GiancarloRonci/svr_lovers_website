On Sunday 27 September 2026, Subiaco goes back to the age of the Borgias with the sixth edition of the Festival dei Cortei Storici, organised by "Comunitas Sublacensis", the town's historical parade group. The event recalls Subiaco's link with the Borgia family: Cesare and Lucrezia were born here, when Rodrigo Borgia was commendatory abbot of Subiaco.

From 10:00 AM, the Borgo degli Opifici and the Canale Barberini, right by the Aniene river, host a Renaissance village with bow-making, woodworking, historical weaponry, antique embroidery and archery, plus bookable workshops on antique embroidery, handmade paper and bookbinding. At lunchtime, participating restaurants offer Borgia-themed menus.

From 3:00 PM the performances begin, with flag-wavers, drummers, historical duels, juggling, theatre, Renaissance music, fire shows and aerial acrobatics. Historical parades from Subiaco, Anagni, Priverno, Segni, Nettuno and Ciciliano march along Via Cavour.
