On Sunday 15 November 2026 Roviano, in the Aniene Valley, celebrates San Martino in Piazza della Repubblica. The feast is organised by the Pro Loco Roviano association with the patronage of the Municipality.

The stands open at 4:00 pm, with new wine (vino novello), chestnuts, bruschetta and mulled wine (vin brulé) to keep warm on an autumn afternoon, along with plenty of music.

The Pro Loco's poster was shared by the local press: for any updates, follow the Pro Loco Roviano's channels.
