On Sunday 22 November 2026 the Pro Loco of Castel San Pietro Romano, together with the Palestrina section of CAI (the Italian Alpine Club), organises "Alla scoperta dei colori d'autunno" (Discovering the colours of autumn), a guided hike through the beech woods and views of the Prenestini Mountains. It leaves at 8 am from the car park of the Mentorella Sanctuary, at Guadagnolo, and is expected back around 1 pm.

The route, about 7 km with 300 metres of ascent, follows CAI trails 504 and 505: the Faggeta delle Meridiane beech wood, Ara della Croce, Tre Confini and Monte Spina Santa (1,060 m). Depending on the group and trail conditions, a loop around Monte Cerella (1,205 m) can be added.

The hike is free, with booking on 331 454 7183. On the same day, from the same car park, the Pro Loco of Capranica also leads the [Mentorella Loop](/en/eventi/escursione-foliage-anello-mentorella-2026): they are two different outings, with separate organisers, times and bookings.
