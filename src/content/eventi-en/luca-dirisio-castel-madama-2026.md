On Sunday 15 November 2026 at 6 pm Luca Dirisio stops in Castel Madama on his 2026 Tour. The singer-songwriter from Abruzzo, one of the names of Italian pop in the 2000s, is known for songs such as "Calma e sangue freddo" and "Usami".

The concert is in Via Sant'Anna 5 and closes the second day of [OliOlive](/en/eventi/oliolive-castel-madama-2026), the new olive oil festival organised by the Municipality, and is hosted by Claudio Testi: you can combine it with a stroll among the stands in the afternoon. The poster does not mention tickets: for confirmation and updates, follow the OliOlive 2026 Facebook page.
