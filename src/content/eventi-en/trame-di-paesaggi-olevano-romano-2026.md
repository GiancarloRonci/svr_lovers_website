From Monday 12 to Saturday 17 October 2026 SpaziOlevano, in Olevano Romano, hosts "Trame di paesaggi" (Weaves of landscapes), the exhibition that closes the integrated workshop "Pittura di Paesaggio e Arte Tessile" (Landscape Painting and Textile Art). On display are works by eleven participants: Andrea, Angela, Daniele, Francesca, Gianmarco, Leandro, Loretana, Marco, Patrizia, Rocco and Valeria.

The opening is on Monday 12 October at 10:00 AM; the exhibition is then open every day from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, with free admission. It is curated by Francesca Tuscano, director of the Museo Civico d'Arte "Domenico Riccardi", and the visual artist Federica Luzzi.

The project is by the Municipality of Olevano Romano, with its departments for Social Policies and Schools and for Culture, together with the Museo Civico d'Arte "Domenico Riccardi", the Centro Disabili Adulti and ANFFAS Subiaco ETS. For information: museo_civico@comune.olevanoromano.rm.it.
