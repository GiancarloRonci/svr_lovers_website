The Christmas Markets of Castel San Pietro Romano return for their ninth edition, organised by the Pro Loco with the support of the Lazio Region. They take place on three Sundays, 13, 20 and 27 December 2026: from 10:30 am to 7:30 pm the historic centre fills with stalls, crafts and tastings, and a free shuttle bus runs on all three days.

On Sunday 13 December, at 4 pm, the band of the B.M.F. La Forma music association of Serrone parades through the village. On Sunday 20 at 4 pm Father Christmas parades, and at 4:30 pm his house opens in via De Sica. On Sunday 27, at 5 pm, the village lights up for "Il Borgo delle Candele" (The Village of Candles), by booking and with a ticket.

On the same days you can also visit the Pro Loco's [life-size art nativity scene](/en/eventi/presepe-artistico-castel-san-pietro-romano-2026).
