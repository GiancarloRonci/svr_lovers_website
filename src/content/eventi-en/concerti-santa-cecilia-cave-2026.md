For the feast of Saint Cecilia, patron saint of musicians, the Teatro Comunale "Milco Paravani" in Cave hosts two band concerts: on Saturday 21 November 2026 the Banda Città di Cave plays, and on Sunday 22 November the Banda G. Puccini.

Both events are in the 2026 calendar of the Pro Loco Città di Cave, which has not yet given times or admission details: for information, follow the channels of the Pro Loco and of the two bands.
