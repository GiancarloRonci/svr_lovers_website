On Sunday 25 October 2026 Colleferro gets moving with "ViviLife", a non-competitive walk to the rhythm of music played through wi-fi headphones, with stops at a few fitness stations. The meeting point is the Circolo Bocciofilo at Corso Garibaldi 16: headphone check at 9:30 AM, departure at 10:00 AM and return at 11:15 AM, with an aperitif for all participants.

The route runs between Via degli Esplosivi and the cycle path. Anyone aged 10 and over can take part, four-legged friends included. Places are limited and booking is required, by calling Viviana (338 7360309) or Elisabetta (327 8744858); for information you can also contact the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro, which organises the event under the patronage of the Municipality, on 331 4616819.
