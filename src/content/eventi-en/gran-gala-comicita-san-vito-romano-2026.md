On Sunday 27 December 2026, at 5 pm, the Teatro Caesar in San Vito Romano closes the free "AmiAMO il Teatro" season with the "Gran Galà della Comicità", a comedy gala over the holidays with many guests and personalities from Italian theatre. The names have not been announced yet: follow the theatre's Facebook page. The show is in Italian.

Admission is free, with unreserved seating; subscribers to the 2027 season, which starts on 17 January, have a reserved seat (information on 380 180 5894 or 338 527 4636). The earlier events of the season are ["Trilussa e il vino"](/en/eventi/trilussa-e-il-vino-san-vito-romano-2026) on 29 November and ["Il malato immaginario"](/en/eventi/malato-immaginario-san-vito-romano-2026) on 13 December.
