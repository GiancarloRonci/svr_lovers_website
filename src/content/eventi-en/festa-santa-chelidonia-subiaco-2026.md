On Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 October 2026 Subiaco celebrates Saint Chelidonia, the town's co-patron saint, with two days dedicated to faith, history and local traditions, with moments of prayer and sharing. The feast, organised by the Town of Subiaco, is tied to the places of the hermit saint in the Simbruini Mountains, starting with the hermitage that bears her name.

The detailed programme, with the times of the celebrations, is not yet available from the sources consulted: to find out more, follow the channels of the Municipality of Subiaco (tel. +39 0774 816200).
