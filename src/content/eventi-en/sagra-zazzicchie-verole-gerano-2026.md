On Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026 Gerano celebrates the 46th edition of the Zazzicchie and Verole Festival. The stars are the zazzicchia, the local sausage served in a sandwich with broccoli rabe, and the verole, roasted chestnuts. The menu also includes truffle bruschetta, pasta e fagioli and French fries, with gluten-free options and a children's menu.

The food stands open on both days at 12:00 PM and stay open until the evening, with covered seating. On Saturday at 9:30 PM in Piazza della Vittoria there is the concert "Idol Pour L'Amour Tour", a tribute to the songs of Achille Lauro; on Sunday the vintage cars of the club I Ferraristi dell'Appia Antica parade through the streets of the town.

The programme has not yet been published by an official source: before setting off, check for updates on the Facebook page of the [Gerano Pro Loco](https://www.facebook.com/ProLocoGerano2018).
