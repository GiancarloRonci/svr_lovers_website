On Sunday 29 November 2026, at 5 pm, the Teatro Caesar in San Vito Romano opens its "Rassegna Teatrale 2026 – AmiAMO il Teatro" with "Trilussa e il vino" ("Trilussa and wine"), written and directed by Michele La Ginestra, with Sergio Zecca. The show is in Italian.

Admission is free, with unreserved seating. The season, promoted by the Comune of San Vito Romano with the patronage of the Lazio Regional Council, continues on 13 December with ["Il malato immaginario"](/en/eventi/malato-immaginario-san-vito-romano-2026) and on 27 December with the ["Gran Galà della Comicità"](/en/eventi/gran-gala-comicita-san-vito-romano-2026). Subscribers to the 2027 season also get a reserved seat at the free shows: subscriptions can be booked on 380 180 5894 or 338 527 4636, also on WhatsApp, and collected at the theatre during the free shows.
