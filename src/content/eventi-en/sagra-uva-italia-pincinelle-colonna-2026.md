From Friday 9 to Sunday 11 October 2026, Colonna celebrates the 64th Italia Grape Festival and the 32nd Pincinelle Festival, named after the town's typical pasta made with just water and flour. The event is organised by the Municipality of Colonna and the Pro Loco.

It opens on Friday at 6:00 PM with the inauguration, followed by a Queen tribute band and the show "La Voce della Piazza". Saturday features children's entertainment from 4:00 PM, the Bandhits, a concert by Banana Republic and a DJ set. On Sunday morning there is a gathering of bands, at 3:00 PM a parade of flag-throwers and allegorical floats, and at 8:30 PM the festival closes with a show by comedian Biagio Izzo.

For food there is the Food and Wine Tour at 25 euros, including a starter, pincinelle, a main course, water and two glasses of house wine, plus tastings at three local wineries: Cantina Cìfero, Cantine Eredi dei Papi and Tenuta Le Quinte. A free shuttle runs from the sports ground and the railway station.
