On Sunday 13 December 2026 the parish community of Subiaco organises "La Via dei Presepi" ("The way of the nativity scenes"), a walk through the town's districts in the name of faith, community and local flavours. On the same day Subiaco also hosts ["Natale nel cuore"](/en/eventi/natale-nel-cuore-subiaco-2026).

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: 339 407 4673, gianluca.zelli@alice.it.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144181)*
