On Saturday 21 November 2026, at 8 pm, La Muracciola restaurant in Palestrina hosts an evening of Zucchero's music: Elio Ericina, known as Mr Pepper Blue, and the Sugar Soul Band play the Italian singer-songwriter's greatest hits, with a menu created for the occasion. It is the restaurant's latest dinner show, after the sold-out evening devoted to Franco Califano in September.

Dinner and show cost 40 euros per person. Booking on +39 06 953 8924.
