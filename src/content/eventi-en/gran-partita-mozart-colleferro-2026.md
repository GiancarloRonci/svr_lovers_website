On Sunday 25 October 2026, from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro hosts a concert entirely devoted to Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart's Serenade for winds No. 10 in B-flat major K361, the famous "Gran Partita". It is performed by an ensemble of 13 clarinets under the artistic direction of Maestro Rodolfo La Banca.

The concert is part of the twelfth edition of the "Incontriamoci" season (2026-2027), promoted by Farandola APS with the patronage of the Comune di Colleferro and in collaboration with the "Licinio Refice" Conservatory of Frosinone. Admission is free.
