On Sunday 13 December 2026 the Subiaco Solidale association presents "Natale nel cuore" ("Christmas in the heart") in Subiaco. The programme has not been published yet. On the same day the town's districts also host ["La Via dei Presepi"](/en/eventi/via-dei-presepi-subiaco-2026).

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: 328 373 7388.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144183)*
