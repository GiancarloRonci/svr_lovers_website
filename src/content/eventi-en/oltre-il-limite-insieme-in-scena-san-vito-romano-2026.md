On Saturday 21 November 2026, from 17:00 to 18:30, the Teatro Cinema Caesar in San Vito Romano hosts a stop of "Oltre il limite, Insieme in Scena" ("Beyond the limit, on stage together"), the theatre project of the young people of the C.I.S. – Centro Integrazione Sociale O.D.V. of Tivoli, funded by the Lazio Region. The show, "Fermati, ascolta il tuo cuore" ("Stop, listen to your heart"), is about emotions, listening and sharing, where difference becomes a strength.

On stage with the young people are Magiche Fantasie for the theatre, Leonardo Pucci's music therapy, HUB Dance Studio for the dance, the Coro Mani Bianche of Charmonie APS of Tivoli with music in Italian Sign Language, and the Lollobrigida physiotherapy studio. The host is Matteo Pizzolorusso. Admission is free and open to all; for information: 328 2158341. The show is in Italian.
