On Sunday 18 October 2026, from 1:00 PM, the Ottobrata Prenestina returns to Palestrina: a community lunch in Piazza Santa Maria degli Angeli to celebrate autumn together, combining tradition and good company.

The fixed-price menu features typical dishes from the Prenestine area and Lazio, prepared by volunteers with seasonal, locally sourced ingredients: traditional first courses, local meat cooked to old recipes and autumn side dishes, all accompanied by local wine and folk music.
