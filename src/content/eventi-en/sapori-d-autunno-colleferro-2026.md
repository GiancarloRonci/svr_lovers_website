On Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026 the Covered Market of Colleferro hosts "Sapori d'Autunno – Un viaggio tutto da gustare" (Flavours of Autumn – A journey to savour), the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro's event dedicated to seasonal food: polenta, chestnuts, mulled wine, platters of cold cuts and cheese, pumpkin, traditional sweets and more.

On Saturday it runs from 6 pm to midnight; on Sunday it is open from 11 am to 9 pm. For information: Pro Loco Città di Colleferro, 331 461 6819, colleferro.proloco@gmail.com.
