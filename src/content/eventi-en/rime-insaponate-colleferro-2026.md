On Saturday 28 November 2026, at 5:00 PM, the Teatro Comunale Vittorio Veneto in Colleferro hosts "Rime insaponate" ("Soapy Rhymes"), the first show of the children's theatre series of the 2026-2027 season. It is a show for all ages that combines words, rhymes and play, written by Alekos Ottaviucci and Gabriele Duma, with Alekos Ottaviucci on stage, and produced by Opificio d'Arte Scenica.

The season is organised by the Municipality of Colleferro with ATCL. The box office, at Via Artigianato 47, is open from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM on the days before the show and from 4:00 PM on the day itself. The ticket price for this show has not been stated: for information, call 345 798 3567 or 06 870 85371, or write to teatrocomunalecolleferro@gmail.com.

The programme comes from the local press: it is worth checking with the theatre before you set off.
