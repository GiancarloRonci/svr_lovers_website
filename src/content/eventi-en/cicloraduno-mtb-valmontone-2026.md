On Sunday 4 October 2026 the Valmontone Cycling Rally returns, a stage of the MTBTour circuit organised by the Ciclo Club Valmontone. The meeting point is Piazza della Repubblica, with the event running from 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

The entry fee is 20 euros, and registration is done through the Ciclo Club Valmontone website. For information: +39 393 124 4502, ccv@cicloclubvalmontone.it.
