On Sunday 20 September 2026, from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Zagarolo hosts the first edition of the "Festa dello Sport" (Sports Festival), an afternoon dedicated to the town's sports organisations along the route from Piazza Santa Maria to Piazza Indipendenza.

Local sports associations will animate the squares and streets of the centre with practical demonstrations of various disciplines, bringing young and old closer to sport as an occasion for participation, wellbeing, inclusion and sharing.

The initiative is promoted by the town council, with Mayor Emanuela Panzironi and Sports Councillor Mariangela Protani, together with numerous volunteers and local sports associations.

For information: the event's official Facebook page.
