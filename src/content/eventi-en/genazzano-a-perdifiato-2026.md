On Sunday, November 8, 2026, "Genazzano a Perdifiato" ("Genazzano Out of Breath") returns: a road race organized by A.S.D. Atletica Genazzano and a stage of the OPES "In Corsa Libera" circuit. Runners meet at 8:00 am at Castello Colonna, Piazza San Nicola 1: the organizers ask everyone to be on time, especially for the youth categories. The main race starts at 10:00 am.

The course is the traditional one, 9.5 km of constant ups and downs: from Castello Colonna it crosses the old town and the Parco degli Elcini, reaches the remains of the Roman aqueduct and Colle Pizzuto, with its view over the town, then passes Bramante's Nymphaeum and the Sanctuary of the Madre del Buon Consiglio before returning to the finish inside the castle. There are also youth races and a non-competitive walk of about 4.5 km with the Antichi Sentieri association. Every finisher gets a medal, and there is a buffet at the end.

Race entry costs 12 euros until 8:00 pm on Friday, November 6, and 15 euros on race day; registration is online, with the links on Atletica Genazzano's page. Free parking is available in Piazzale Madre Teresa di Calcutta, in the car park on Via del Mattatoio and in Piazza della Repubblica.
