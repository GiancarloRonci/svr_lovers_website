On Friday 9 October 2026, at 7:00 PM, the 64th Italia Grape Festival and 32nd Pincinelle Festival in Colonna opens with music from Queenaedo, a tribute band bringing the rock of Queen and the songs of Freddie Mercury to the stage.

The concert follows the festival's opening ceremony, scheduled for 6:00 PM, and is the first musical event of the weekend; the evening continues at 9:30 PM with the show "La Voce della Piazza". On Saturday 10 it is the turn of [Banana Republic](/en/eventi/banana-republic-tributo-dalla-de-gregori-colonna-2026), a tribute to Dalla and De Gregori. During the festival, the Food and Wine Tour is available at 25 euros, with pincinelle pasta and wine from local wineries.
