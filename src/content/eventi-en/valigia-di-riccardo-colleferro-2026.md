On Wednesday 14 October 2026, from 4:30 to 8:00 PM, the Parco dei Donatori in Colleferro hosts "La Valigia di Riccardo – Storie, giochi e merenda insieme" (Riccardo's Suitcase – Stories, games and a snack together), an afternoon organised by the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro for children and their families, open to the whole community.

The programme includes stories, games, music and a shared snack: it is also a chance to wish a good journey to Riccardo and his family, who are about to leave. No tickets are required. For information you can contact the Pro Loco of Colleferro on 331 4616819.
