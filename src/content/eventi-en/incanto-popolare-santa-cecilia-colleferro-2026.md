On Sunday 22 November 2026, at 5:30 pm, the Auditorium Fabbrica della Musica in Colleferro celebrates Saint Cecilia, patron saint of musicians, with "Incanto Popolare – Cantiamo a Santa Cecilia le musiche della nostra terra!" (Folk Enchantment – Singing the music of our land for Saint Cecilia), a concert devoted to traditional folk songs and music. On stage are the Compagnia Musica Nostra and Ars Nova Accademia Musicale.

The concert is part of the Municipality of Colleferro's weekend programme. Cost and admission details are not stated: contact the organisers for information.
