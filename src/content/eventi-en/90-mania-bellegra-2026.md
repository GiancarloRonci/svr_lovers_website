On Friday 2 October 2026, at 9:30 PM, Piazza del Municipio in Bellegra comes alive with "90' Mania", a show bringing back the dance and pop hits of the 1990s. It is the headline act on the opening night of the 18th Sagra delle Tacchie ai Funghi Porcini, which the same day also welcomes Michelin-starred chef Marco Bottega.

Before or during the show you can enjoy tacchie pasta with porcini mushrooms and the festival's other dishes at the Pro Loco stands.
