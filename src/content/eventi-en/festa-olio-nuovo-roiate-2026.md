On Sunday 8 November 2026 the Festa dell'Olio Nuovo (New Olive Oil Festival) returns to Roiate for its fifteenth edition. It is organised by the Roiate in Progress association together with the Municipality: the food stand opens at 12:30 pm.

The full menu costs 15 euros and includes two bruschette with olive pâté and new oil, gnocchi with white ragù, abbacchio alla cacciatora (hunter-style lamb), a glass of wine or water and ciambelline al vino (wine biscuits). Dishes can also be ordered separately: bruschette 2 euros, gnocchi 6, lamb 10 and biscuits 2.

The venue, Piazzale della Libertà, is given by RomaToday but not on the poster: for confirmation and updates, including in case of bad weather, follow the Roiate in Progress Facebook page (tel. 338 259 3325).
