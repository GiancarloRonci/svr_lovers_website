On Monday 28 December 2026, between Christmas and New Year, ASD Vivere la montagna is back at Monte Livata with "Esperienze di territorio" ("Local experiences"), a day outdoors to explore Subiaco's mountain at an easy pace, even in winter. The other two dates are [5](/en/eventi/esperienze-di-territorio-monte-livata-2026-12-05) and [19 December](/en/eventi/esperienze-di-territorio-monte-livata-2026-12-19).

The event is in the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: ask the organiser. Information and booking: 320 968 1006, viverelaniene@gmail.com.

*Source: [Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park](https://www.parcomontisimbruini.it/iniziativa.php?id=144232)*
