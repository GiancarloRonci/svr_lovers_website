On Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026 the Sambuci Pro Loco organises the 28th Polenta Festival, in Piazza di Corte and in the park of Theodoli Castle, with outdoor tables and covered areas in case of bad weather. Polenta comes in two versions: the classic one with a sauce of pork ribs and sausages, or "in bianco" with Roman broccoli; there are also French fries and fried pizzas.

On Saturday the food stands open at 7:00 PM for dinner, with a night show by the flag-wavers and musicians of Ciciliano followed by a DJ set. On Sunday they open at 12:00 PM for lunch, with live music by Carlo Gizzi and, in the afternoon, the flag-wavers parading through the town.

On Sunday you can also visit Theodoli Castle, with paid guided tours led by ladies in period dress from the Associazione Culturale Terzo Millennio. Times and prices are on the Facebook and Instagram pages of the Sambuci Pro Loco.
