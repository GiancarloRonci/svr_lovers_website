On Friday 18 December 2026, from 20:00, San Cesareo hosts its Presepe Vivente (Living Nativity), organised by the San Giuseppe parish, the Pro Loco San Cesareo 2026 and the town's associations: a re-enactment of the Nativity with scenes, traditional crafts and performers in period costume.

The organisers are looking for about 40 performers of all ages among local residents: anyone who wants to take part or needs information can call Luca (328 2710964) or Federica (388 9527004). The exact location of the performance has not yet been announced.
