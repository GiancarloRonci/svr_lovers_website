On Saturday 17 October 2026, from 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM, the "Ricca" public library in Colleferro hosts the presentation of "40 secondi" (40 seconds) by Federica Angeli (Baldini+Castoldi), a reflective meeting dedicated to the memory of Willy Monteiro Duarte: a book about how a single instant can mark a life, a family and an entire community forever.

After a welcome and introduction by Colleferro's deputy mayor Diana Stanzani, the author talks with Pierluigi Sanna, deputy mayor of the Metropolitan City of Rome. Admission is free; the event is organized by the Pro Loco Città di Colleferro.
