On Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November 2026 the Auditorium Pierluigi in [Palestrina](/en/luoghi/palestrina) hosts "Sogno di una notte di mezz'estate" (A Midsummer Night's Dream), the second show in the season preview after "La guerra di Martin".

On stage are the students of the adult course of the cultural association "Arte Madre": the show closes their academic year and is the culmination of a journey of study, rehearsals and passion for the theatre.

Performance times, ticket prices and booking details have not been announced yet. The season continues in December with "Rapunzel – Il Musical" (12, 13, 19 and 20 December).
