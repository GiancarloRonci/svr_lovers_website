On Saturday 26 December 2026, Saint Stephen's Day, the Centro Musica Subiaco APS brings "AltAcustica Nix" to Jenne, a concert of music by Antonio Vivaldi and traditional songs, part of the Simbruini Mountains Park's "Archimenti nei borghi" series.

The event is part of the Simbruini Mountains Regional Nature Park calendar, which does not yet give the time, meeting point or cost: for information and booking, contact the organiser. Information: 328 626 8915, centromusicasubiaco@gmail.com.
