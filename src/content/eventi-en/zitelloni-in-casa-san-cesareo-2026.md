On Sunday 25 October 2026, at 5:30 PM, the town auditorium (Auditorium Comunale) of San Cesareo hosts "Zitelloni in Casa" (Old Bachelors at Home), staged by the LIFC Officium theatre company, linked to the Italian Cystic Fibrosis League (Lega Italiana Fibrosi Cistica).

Admission is by free-will donation, and all proceeds go to the LIFC to fund care projects for cystic fibrosis patients treated at the Bambino Gesù Children's Hospital. For information and bookings: tel. +39 334 7011908, officiumroma@gmail.com.
