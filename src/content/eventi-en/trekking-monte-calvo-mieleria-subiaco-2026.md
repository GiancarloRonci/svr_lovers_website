On Sunday 15 November 2026 Campaegli Wild and the Mieleria del Bosco offer a trek to Monte Calvo, near Subiaco, inside the Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park, with a stop at the Mieleria del Bosco to see the work of local beekeepers up close.

The Park's page does not give the time, meeting point, difficulty or fee: for these details and to book, call the organisers' info line on +39 350 115 7226.
