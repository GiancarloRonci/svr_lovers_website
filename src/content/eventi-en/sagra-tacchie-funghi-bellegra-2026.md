Bellegra, a village in the Monti Prenestini mountains, once again hosts the **Tacchie and Porcini Mushroom Festival** ("Sagra delle Tacchie ai Funghi Porcini"), a traditional autumn event organised by the local Pro Loco that celebrates two excellences of local cuisine: tacchie, handmade pasta made following an old peasant recipe and cut into irregular shapes, and porcini mushrooms harvested from the surrounding woods.

Based on the Pro Loco's annual calendar, the 2026 edition should take place over the **first and second weekend of October**, tentatively **2-4 and 9-11 October 2026**, in the town's historic squares (Piazza del Municipio, Piazza Giovanni Paolo II and Piazza della Libertà). *The exact dates have not yet been officially confirmed by the Pro Loco di Bellegra: check their official channels for updates before planning a visit.*

**What to expect, based on previous editions**

- Food stalls open for lunch and dinner (dinner on Saturdays) serving traditional dishes with tacchie and porcini mushrooms
- Craft and local produce market
- Music and folk shows, including the "Gens Vitellia" historical procession in costume
- Children's play area and a covered, heated square for eating in case of bad weather
- Free shuttle parking and a tourist information point
- Guided tours of the [Grotte dell'Arco](/en/luoghi/grotte-dell-arco) caves
