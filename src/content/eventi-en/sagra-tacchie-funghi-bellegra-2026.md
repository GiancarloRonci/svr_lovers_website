Bellegra, a village in the Monti Prenestini mountains, once again hosts the **Tacchie and Porcini Mushroom Festival** ("Sagra delle Tacchie ai Funghi Porcini"), a traditional autumn event organised by the local Pro Loco that celebrates two excellences of local cuisine: tacchie, handmade pasta made following an old peasant recipe and cut into irregular shapes, and porcini mushrooms harvested from the surrounding woods.

The 18th edition runs over two weekends, **2-3-4 and 9-10-11 October 2026**, in Piazza del Municipio, with both outdoor and indoor areas. The food stalls open at 12:00 PM for lunch and at 7:30 PM for dinner.

**The programme**

- **Friday 2 October:** opening at 6:00 PM, an evening with Michelin-starred chef Marco Bottega and, at 9:30 PM, the "90 Mania" music show
- **Saturday 3 October:** at 9:00 PM folk music, songs and dancing with "I Brigallè"
- **Friday 9 October:** market from 5:00 PM and, at 9:30 PM, the "Indie Power" evening
- **Saturday 10 October:** at 9:30 PM tribute to the band 883 with the 883 Max Mania Tribute Band, at 11:30 PM DJ set
- **Sunday 11 October:** at 11:30 AM a display by the "Civiltà Romana" historical group, at 1:00 PM music with the "Banda Smile", at 5:00 PM the 12th "Gens Vitellia" historical procession, with ladies and knights in costume, and at 6:00 PM the flag-throwers' display

**The menu**

The full menu costs 22 euros and includes polenta crostini with porcini mushrooms, a plate of tacchie with porcini, a meat main course with porcini and a small bottle of wine or water; dishes can also be ordered individually. For children there is a 10-euro menu with breaded chicken, fries and a soft drink.

The festival also features a craft market, music and folk shows, a children's play area and parking with a free shuttle.
