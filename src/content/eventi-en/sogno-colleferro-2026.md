On Saturday 12 December 2026, at 9:00 PM, the Teatro Comunale Vittorio Veneto in Colleferro hosts "Sogno" ("Dream"), a dance show with live music based on Shakespeare's "A Midsummer Night's Dream". Direction and choreography are by Loredana Parrella, the texts by Aleksandros Memetaj and the live music by Fabio Recchia. It is produced by Twain centro di produzione danza, Festival Abano Danza and Fondazione Entroterre.

The story is set in a venue, the "Bar del Bosco", where insecure work and broken dreams send Oberon, Titania and Puck off the rails: a raw, rock rewriting in which art becomes a form of resistance.

The show is part of the 2026-2027 subscription season, organised by the Municipality of Colleferro with ATCL. A full-price ticket costs 20 euros plus a 2-euro booking fee, a reduced ticket 17 euros plus 2, and an under-30 reduced ticket 10 euros; in the gallery, 18 euros plus 2, or 15 plus 2 reduced. The box office, at Via Artigianato 47, is open from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM on the days before the show and from 4:00 PM on the day itself. For information: 345 798 3567, 06 870 85371, teatrocomunalecolleferro@gmail.com.
