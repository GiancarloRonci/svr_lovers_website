On Saturday 2 January 2027 the Centro Musica Subiaco APS brings "AltAcustica Nix" to Monte Livata: music by Antonio Vivaldi and traditional songs, for the Simbruini Mountains Park's "Gli Archimenti nei borghi" series. It is the third stop, after [Jenne](/en/eventi/altacustica-nix-jenne-2026) on 26 December and [Cervara di Roma](/en/eventi/altacustica-nix-cervara-di-roma-2026) on the 27th.

The Park calendar does not yet give the time, exact venue or cost: ask the organiser. Information: 328 626 8915, centromusicasubiaco@gmail.com.
