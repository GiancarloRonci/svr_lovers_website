On Saturday 7 November 2026, from 2:30 to 4:30 pm, Monti Simbruini Regional Nature Park guide Domenica Panzini leads a free-paced walk and Nordic Walking session in the countryside of Subiaco. The meeting point is in Montore, next to the Cappella della Madonna.

The route runs through Forma Focerale, Contrada Bottiano and Contrada Colle Perino before returning to Montore. Booking is required by Friday 6 November; the Park's page does not mention a fee.

For information and booking: +39 328 4538950, ss-antares@tiscali.it.
