On Sunday 8 November 2026, from 10:00 AM, the historic centre of Palestrina hosts the traditional Fiera Agricola di San Martino (Saint Martin's Farm Fair), organised by the Pro Loco Palestrina APS and Uniagraria Palestrina with the involvement of the Municipality. The streets of the centre become a space devoted to the area's farming, food and wine.

The programme includes exhibits, tastings, demonstrations and set menus, with entertainment for adults and children. Times for the individual activities have not been published yet: for updates, follow the Pro Loco Palestrina's Facebook page.
