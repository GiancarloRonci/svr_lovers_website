On Saturday 24 October 2026, at 5:00 PM, the MuDi, the Museo Diffuso of Castel San Pietro Romano, hosts the presentation of the book "Mare Nero" by Marco Ansaldo.

The event is part of the October calendar in the Monti Prenestini put together by Visit Monti Prenestini. The programme of the talk and the names of the speakers have not been announced yet.
