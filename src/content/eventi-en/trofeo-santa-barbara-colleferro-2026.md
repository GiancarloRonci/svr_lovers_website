On Sunday 22 November 2026 Colleferro hosts the tenth edition of the Trofeo Santa Barbara, the "Rinaldo Perfetti and Roberto Di Felice Memorial", a 9.8 km competitive race through the town centre. Start and finish are at the "Maurizio Natali" municipal sports ground.

Runners gather from 8 AM, the race starts at 9:30 AM and the prize-giving is expected around 11 AM. Registration is online via Race Service; for information call 347 6800877, 393 536 3014 or 320 557 6311. The race is organised by A.S.D. Runners Team Colleferro.
