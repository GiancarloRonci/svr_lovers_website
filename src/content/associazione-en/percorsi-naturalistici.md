---
title: Nature trails
categoria: percorsi-naturalistici
description: Walks and hikes to discover the natural surroundings of San Vito Romano.
---

The idea is to organise group walks and hikes along the trails in the surrounding
area, through woods, hills and nearby villages, to discover together the landscape
and nature around San Vito Romano. In the meantime, some routes can already be
found in the Trails section of the site.
