---
title: Photo contests
categoria: concorsi-fotografici
description: Photography contests to tell the story of San Vito Romano and its surroundings through the shots of residents and visitors.
---

The idea is to organise photo contests open to everyone, residents and visitors
alike, to tell the story of San Vito Romano through images: the alleys and squares
of the old town, the landscapes of the surrounding area, the festivals and the life
of the town. Themes, rules and how to take part will be announced on this page.
