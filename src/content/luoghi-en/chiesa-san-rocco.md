The church of San Rocco is one of the places of worship in San Vito Romano.

The interior is richly decorated: painted marble, columns, gilded stucco and pale stone statues flanking an elegant pulpit, while in the background, between wide curtains, the statue of the Madonna and Child can be glimpsed.
