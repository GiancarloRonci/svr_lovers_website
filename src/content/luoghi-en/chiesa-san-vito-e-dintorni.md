The church of San Vito is one of the landmarks of San Vito Romano and, together with its surroundings, one of the village's hearts.

The lanes around the church, paved with cobblestones and lined with iron railings, offer evocative views over the terracotta rooftops and the surrounding hills, with Castello Theodoli standing out in the distance.

If you have photos, memories or stories about the church and the area around it, share them with the [San Vito Romano Lovers community on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/sanvitoromano.lovers).
