La Macchiarella is a public park and a centuries-old chestnut wood owned by the municipality, located right inside the town of San Vito Romano. It covers more than 4 hectares and is made up mainly of old chestnut trees, which offer shade and shelter during the summer months.

The area is well kept: wooden fences mark out the paths inside the wood, and tables and benches invite you to stop and rest. A dedicated wide path links the Macchiarella directly to the [Villa Comunale](/en/luoghi/villa-comunale) park. In the right season, you can head into the chestnut wood to gather chestnuts and mushrooms.

Inside the park lies the Path of Conscience (Sentiero della Coscienza), a trail dedicated to the memory of the victims of the Holocaust, of totalitarian regimes and of the foibe massacres.

The Macchiarella is a short distance from Piazza Roma. Buses run nearby, with stops just a few minutes' walk away, such as those in Via Trento Trieste and Via Vittorio Emanuele.
