In Valmontone, about 40 km from Rome and just over half an hour by car from San
Vito Romano, stands the Valmontone Outlet (also known as Fashion District
Valmontone), one of the largest and most visited fashion outlets in central
Italy. Opened in 2003 and expanded several times since, the centre brings
together more than 180 stores of Italian and international clothing, footwear,
accessories and homeware brands, with discounts ranging from 30% to 70% all
year round.

The centre, surrounded by the greenery of the Roman countryside, is laid out
like a small open-air village, with pedestrian streets, squares, fountains and
dining areas. The outlet is easily
reached by car via the A1 motorway (Valmontone exit) and has ample free
parking.

A shopping destination for residents and tourists from the Castelli Romani and
the Monti Prenestini area, the outlet is open seven days a week, with longer
hours at weekends: it is worth checking them on the official website before
setting off, especially on public holidays.

## What visitors think

Judging by the reviews left online by people who have been there, impressions
are positive overall but not unanimous. The most appreciated aspects are the
wide variety of shops, for all ages and all budgets, the pleasure of shopping
while strolling outdoors, the many places to eat and the spaces designed for
children: many recommend it to families, partly because the MagicLand
amusement park is close by.

The most frequent criticisms concern parking and traffic: at weekends, on
public holidays and during the sales the outlet gets very crowded, finding a
space can take time and queues form on the way out. Opinions on prices are
divided: some report excellent bargains, while others find the discounts less
attractive than expected or note that part of the stock comes from past
collections, and a few consider the on-site cafés expensive.

The most common advice is therefore to go on a weekday, or to arrive early in
the morning, and to compare prices before buying. Setting off from San Vito
Romano, the short distance makes it easy to pick the quietest time.
