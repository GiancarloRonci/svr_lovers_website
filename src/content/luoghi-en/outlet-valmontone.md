In Valmontone, about 40 km from Rome and just over half an hour by car from San
Vito Romano, stands the Fashion District Valmontone (formerly known as the
McArthurGlen Outlet), one of the largest fashion outlets in central Italy.
Opened in 2006 and expanded several times since, the centre brings together
more than 200 stores of Italian and international clothing, footwear,
accessories and homeware brands, with discounts ranging from 30% to 70% off
list prices.

The building, surrounded by the greenery of the Roman countryside, is laid out
like a small open-air village, with covered streets, squares and dining areas
that echo the style of the area's historic town centres. The outlet is easily
reached by car via the A1 motorway (Valmontone exit) and has ample free
parking.

A shopping destination for residents and tourists from the Castelli Romani and
the Monti Prenestini area, the outlet is open every day of the year, with
extended hours on weekends and during seasonal sales.

*Source: [Fashion District Valmontone - official website](https://www.fashiondistrict.it/en/outlet/valmontone).*
