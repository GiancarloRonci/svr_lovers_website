Il Bar 2000 is one of those places that live mostly in the memory of the people who spent time there: a vintage postcard shows it with its red sign, the covered terrace with its outdoor tables, and the small square it overlooked, back when San Vito Romano moved at a different pace.

Memories shared by the community suggest the bar was already a well-loved meeting point in the 1980s: a group of around thirty people, including the town's theatre group, used to gather here regularly, welcomed by Vincenzo, the owner. For generations of sanvitesi it was the place for evenings with friends, coffees, and first dates.

When this photo was shared in the community's Facebook group, many people found a piece of their own youth in it, with comments like "so many memories" and "what nostalgia for the good old days": a small but meaningful symbol of the San Vito Romano of yesterday.

*Source: memories shared by the community on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/sanvitoromano.lovers/posts/876252508038511/).*
