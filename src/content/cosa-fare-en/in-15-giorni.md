An itinerary for a two-week stay, assuming you visit from Monday to the
following Monday. The first week follows the "In 7 Days" itinerary: San Vito
Romano on foot, the sanctuaries and villages of the Monti Prenestini, the
villas of Tivoli, the monasteries of Subiaco and Bellegra, Monte Livata,
Genazzano and Olevano Romano, and Palestrina. The second week opens with a
second day in the old town of San Vito, with Trail B, the Stretta and the
town's stories and legends, and continues with Hadrian's Villa, Anagni, the
Castelli Romani, Zagarolo with the Valmontone outlet, a day in Rome and the
Arch Caves. On the last day you return to the old town for a third time, to
say goodbye.

From the second day on you will need a car for the trips. The times are
indicative — adjust them to the season and to places' opening hours. Anagni,
the Castelli Romani, Zagarolo and Rome do not yet have a page in the Places
section: check opening hours on the official websites.
