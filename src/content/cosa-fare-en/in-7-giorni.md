An itinerary for a week-long stay, assuming you visit from Monday to Sunday.
The first day explores San Vito Romano on foot, from its squares to the
castle, the alleys of the old town, its churches, springs and parks; the
second is devoted to the sanctuaries and villages of the Monti Prenestini,
from the Mentorella to Rocca di Cave; the third to the villas of Tivoli, Villa
d'Este and Villa Gregoriana; the fourth to the Benedictine monasteries of
Subiaco, with a stop in Bellegra on the way back; the fifth to the nature of
Monte Livata; the sixth to Genazzano and Olevano Romano; the seventh to
Palestrina, before saying goodbye in town. From the second day on you will
need a car. The times are indicative — adjust them to the season and to
places' opening hours.
