An itinerary for a three-day stay, assuming you visit from Thursday to
Saturday. The first day explores San Vito Romano on foot, from the castle to
the alleys of the old town, its churches, springs and parks; the second is
devoted to the sanctuaries and villages of the Monti Prenestini, from the
Mentorella to Rocca di Cave; the third to the Benedictine monasteries of
Subiaco, with a stop in Bellegra on the way back. You will need a car for the
second and third days. The times are indicative — adjust them to the season
and to places' opening hours.
