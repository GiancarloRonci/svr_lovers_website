Trail B starts from the Tre Fontane and crosses Borgo Mario Theodoli, passing through Piazza Governo Vecchio. It continues along Via Olevano to Via dell'Arringo and Via Porta Olevano, where you find the timeless cross and the old washing trough. The trail then climbs back up towards Via Romana and Via della Mola, reaching Piazza San Biagio, between the Fontana Ciocia and the Stretta. From here it climbs the Stretta, returns to Via delle Logge, passes in front of the Santa Maria church, and closes the loop back at the Tre Fontane.
