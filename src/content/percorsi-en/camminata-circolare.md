A loop walk in the heart of San Vito Romano, easy to do at any time of day and ideal for stretching your legs without leaving town.

It starts on Via Borgo Mario Theodoli, the long street laid out in the 17th century by Cardinal Mario Theodoli, and follows it west to Viale Piave. From here the walk climbs gently along the avenue up to Viale Giovanni XXIII, the highest and furthest point of the loop. The way back descends along Via Dante Alighieri and continues on Via Ungheria, returning to the starting point.

The route is almost entirely on paved roads and pavements, with a height difference of about 50 metres: suitable for everyone, including an evening stroll or a walk with children.
