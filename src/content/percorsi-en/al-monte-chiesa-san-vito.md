A short walk from the centre of the village up to the church of San Vito, at the top of the "Monte", the upper part of the village.

It starts from Via Vittorio Emanuele and climbs Via del Monte, a paved street between the houses that reaches the foot of the brick and white-stone wall on which the church stands. It then loops around the hill, with views over the terracotta rooftops and the mountains, to the tree-lined lawn in front of the church, with its benches and an old stone well, before heading back down towards the starting point.

The route is less than 300 metres long, almost entirely paved, with a short climb: suitable for everyone. To find out more about the church, see [Church of San Vito and Surroundings](/en/luoghi/chiesa-san-vito-e-dintorni) in the Places section.
