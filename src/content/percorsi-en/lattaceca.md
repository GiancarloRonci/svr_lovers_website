A short loop walk in the centre of San Vito Romano, starting and ending in [Piazza Augusto Baccelli](/en/luoghi/piazza-augusto-baccelli).

From the square the route first heads west for a short stretch and comes back, then turns north and goes around the hill of [Theodoli Castle](/en/luoghi/castello-theodoli). The way back descends to [Piazza Governo Vecchio](/en/luoghi/piazza-governo-vecchio) and from there returns to the starting point.

The whole walk is about 560 metres, to be taken at an easy pace in around ten minutes.
