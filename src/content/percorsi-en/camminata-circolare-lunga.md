The long version of the [Circular Walk](/en/percorsi/camminata-circolare/): a loop of about four kilometres for those who have an hour to spare and want to discover the streets beyond the historic centre too.

It starts on Via Borgo Mario Theodoli and, via Via Ungheria and Via Dante Alighieri, reaches Viale Giovanni XXIII. From here the walk heads north, uphill, as far as the first houses of Vado Canale, the highest and furthest point of the loop. You come back along the same avenue and go down Viale Piave towards Via Vittorio Emanuele, which leads south to Via Sisto Iella. The way back runs along Via Guido Baccelli and returns to the starting point.

The route is almost entirely on paved roads, with a height difference of about 80 metres: suitable for everyone, but take it easy on the uphill stretches.
