**Tell the story of San Vito Romano through a photograph**

*This is a courtesy translation of the Italian rules.*

## 1. Promoters and purpose

The contest is promoted by a group of people who, spontaneously and without forming an association, wish to help enhance and promote San Vito Romano through photography.

The initiative is amateur and non-profit, and aims to collect images that tell the story of the area: its places, landscapes, monuments, traditions and moments of everyday life.

## 2. Taking part

Participation is **free and open to all**.

Each participant may submit **an unlimited number of photographs**.

The following photographs are accepted:

- taken directly by the participant;
- belonging to the participant's personal or family collection, including historical photographs;
- taken by other people, provided the participant holds the necessary rights or permissions for their publication, distribution and exhibition as part of the contest and of activities promoting the area.

Images taken with any device, including cameras and smartphones, are accepted.

Post-production and retouching are allowed, provided they do not distort the main subject of the photo or substantially alter its content, meaning or distinctive elements. By way of example, adjustments to brightness, contrast, colour, exposure and sharpness, cropping and the removal of small distracting elements are allowed. Changes that add, replace or substantially manipulate elements present in the scene, such that the photograph becomes an image no longer traceable to the situation originally captured, are not allowed.

Taking part implies acceptance of these rules.

## 3. Theme and characteristics of the photographs

The photographs must be taken within the territory of **San Vito Romano** and have the town and its territory as their subject or reference.

Photographs will not be accepted if they are:

- contrary to the law, to public decency or to the dignity of persons;
- containing material to which the participant does not hold the rights;
- in breach of the copyright, image rights or privacy of third parties;
- not consistent with the purpose of the contest.

The organisers reserve the right to exclude photographs that do not comply with these rules.

## 4. Copyright and liability

The participant declares that they are the author of the photograph or that they hold all the rights necessary for its publication and distribution.

The participant is responsible for the content of the photographs submitted and guarantees that their publication does not infringe the rights of third parties.

Where recognisable people appear, the participant declares that they have obtained, where necessary, the relevant permissions to photograph them and to publish the image.

Where minors appear in the photograph, the participant declares that they have obtained permission to distribute the image from the parents or from whoever holds parental responsibility.

Any dispute concerning copyright, image rights or privacy will be the sole responsibility of the participant who submitted the photograph.

## 5. Use of the photographs

By taking part in the contest, **the copyright in the photograph remains with its owner**; the participant grants the organisers, free of charge, a **non-exclusive** permission to use the image.

The photographs may be used, without any payment to the participant or to the author, for:

- the contest and the related voting;
- shows and exhibitions, including online;
- posters, brochures and other communication materials;
- cultural activities and initiatives to promote and enhance San Vito Romano.

The permission is granted without territorial limits and for the time necessary for the purposes listed above.

In particular, the photographs may be displayed online on the website [sanvitoromanolovers.org](https://www.sanvitoromanolovers.org/en/) for an indefinite period.

The photographs will not be passed on to third parties for commercial purposes nor used to sell the image without the specific permission of the rights holder.

The participant accepts that the photograph may be made publicly available as part of the activities promoting the contest and the area, without being able to claim payment, compensation or any other consideration for such uses.

## 6. Submitting the photographs

The photographs must be submitted by **30 November 2026**, in the manner indicated by the organisers:

Dedicated form available online: [www.sanvitoromanolovers.org/en/culturaturismo/contest-fotografico-il-sole-ridisegna-i-contorni/](https://www.sanvitoromanolovers.org/en/culturaturismo/contest-fotografico-il-sole-ridisegna-i-contorni/)

For each participant, at least a first name, surname and a contact detail must be given.

For each photograph, a title may be given and, if considered useful, the place where it was taken.

## 7. Voting and winner

All accepted photographs will be put to a vote in the manner announced by the organisers.

The photograph that receives the most votes will be declared the **winner of the contest**.

In the event of a tie, the organisers may hold a run-off vote or apply the criterion that will be announced before voting closes.

The organisers' decision on the validity of the votes and on the application of these rules will be final.

## 8. Prizes and recognition

The contest **does not offer cash prizes**.

The winning photograph, together with any other photographs selected, will be showcased through **publication, exhibition and promotion** as part of the initiatives organised for the contest and for the enhancement of the area.

The winner may be awarded a certificate or a symbolic mention.

## 9. Privacy

The personal data provided by participants will be used exclusively to manage the contest and for communications connected with it, in compliance with the data protection legislation in force.

Taking part in the contest implies acceptance of the conditions on the publication and distribution of the photographs set out in these rules.

## 10. Acceptance

Submitting a photograph constitutes **full acceptance of these rules** and of the conditions on participation, publication, exhibition and use of the images.
